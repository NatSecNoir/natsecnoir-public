The Letter of Assurances requires Amalgamated Telecom Holdings Limited (ATH) to implement national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety safeguards as a condition of the Federal Communications Commission’s approval of its proposed acquisition of control from Amper S.A.

**What it does** This Team Telecom mitigation agreement makes ATH’s operation of the BlueSky System—including the American Samoa-Hawaii Cable System and related U.S. telecommunications infrastructure—subject to compliance monitoring by DOJ and DHS. It requires a U.S.-based security officer, designated points of contact for lawful interception, U.S. personnel authority to respond to lawful U.S. process, equipment and vendor disclosures, advance notice and review of material changes, logical and physical security policies, personnel screening, U.S. availability of records, audits, incident reporting, training, periodic reporting, and agency visitation. ATH must also maintain the ability to interrupt U.S.-bound traffic on the cable system when required by lawful process.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind ATH and cover its U.S.-located facilities, principal network and cable equipment, domestic communications, records, personnel, contractors, vendors, and remote-access arrangements. DOJ and DHS act as the Compliance Monitoring Agencies, while the FCC is the agency asked to condition and grant the pending transfer applications.

**Why it matters** The LOA provides the operational controls through which Team Telecom addresses foreign ownership and access risks in a U.S.-connected telecommunications carrier and submarine cable system. It preserves U.S. control over responses to foreign-government requests for U.S. records and communications and gives the monitoring agencies continuing review and enforcement leverage.

**Key dates and numbers**
- The document does not state an execution or release date.
- ATH must nominate a security officer within 30 business days of execution.
- Security, physical-security, remote-access, and screening policies are generally due within 60 business days.
- ATH must report certain incidents and qualifying ownership or control changes within 10 business days.
- A foreign entity or individual acquiring more than 10% ownership, or control, triggers notice obligations.
