The House Select Committee on the CCP asks the International Olympic Committee (IOC) to conduct an independent investigation into the handling of positive tests by 23 Chinese swimmers and asks the Department of Justice (DOJ) and Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) to investigate possible international doping fraud under the Rodchenkov Anti-Doping Act of 2019.

**What it does**
The document combines Chairman Moolenaar’s prepared remarks with letters signed by Moolenaar, Ranking Member Raja Krishnamoorthi, and Members Carlos Gimenez and André Carson. The IOC letter describes reports that 23 Chinese swimmers tested positive for trimetazidine before the 2021 Tokyo Olympic Games, were cleared following a Chinese investigation, and were allowed to compete. It urges the IOC to review the case file and WADA’s decision independently. The DOJ/FBI letter requests an investigation using the Act’s asserted extraterritorial jurisdiction, along with a briefing for the committee by June 5, 2024.

**Who it affects**
The requests target the IOC, DOJ, and FBI, and concern WADA, Chinese swimming officials and athletes, and the broader international anti-doping system. The document focuses on U.S. athletes’ interest in a fair competition at the 2024 Paris Olympics.

**Why it matters**
The committee frames the episode as a potential failure of anti-doping oversight and possible state-sponsored conduct by the People’s Republic of China. It also argues that the alleged handling of the tests raises legal, ethical, competitive, and diplomatic concerns, while calling for accountability and transparency rather than asserting that wrongdoing has been established.

**Key dates and numbers**
- 2021: The swimmers’ positive tests preceded the Tokyo Olympic Games.
- May 21, 2024: The letters to IOC President Thomas Bach and DOJ/FBI officials are dated.
- May 22, 2024: The prepared remarks are dated.
- 23: Chinese swimmers reportedly tested positive for trimetazidine.
- June 5, 2024: Requested deadline for the DOJ/FBI briefing.
- 2019: The Rodchenkov Anti-Doping Act was enacted.
