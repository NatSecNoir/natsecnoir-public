The Letter of Agreement requires Neutral Networks USA LLC to implement extensive national-security and law-enforcement safeguards as a condition of FCC authorization for its proposed Neutral Networks Laredo Cable submarine system.

**What it does** This agreement commits Neutral Networks to provide DHS and DOJ, acting as the Compliance Monitoring Agencies, with network, equipment, vendor, operations, and ownership information; obtain review of specified personnel, facilities, equipment, vendors, and security policies; and maintain incident-reporting, audit, training, and compliance procedures. It covers the U.S. portion of the proposed private fiber-optic cable linking Laredo, Texas, and Nuevo Laredo, Tamaulipas, Mexico. The CMAs may conduct visits and request third-party audits, and breaches may support a recommendation that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify relevant authorization.

**Who it affects** The obligations bind Neutral Networks, its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, affiliates, contractors, vendors, and service providers involved with the cable system. DHS and DOJ receive monitoring and approval powers, while the FCC is the licensing authority whose cable landing authorization would be conditioned on compliance.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for a cross-border submarine cable, addressing foreign ownership, supply-chain risk, physical and logical access, protection of U.S. records and communications, and cybersecurity incidents without identifying specific restricted vendors or foreign owners.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated May 12, 2022; FCC File No. SCL-LIC-20210930-00042 and TT #21-071.
- Proposed POC within 30 days; many equipment and change notices require 30 days, while certain vendor changes require 45 days.
- Report qualifying incidents within 48 hours; third-party breaches within 72 hours; notify qualifying ownership or control changes within 15 days.
- Annual compliance reports and no more than one CMA-requested audit per calendar year, absent an unsatisfactory audit.
