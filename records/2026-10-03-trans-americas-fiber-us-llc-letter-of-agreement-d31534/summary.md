The Letter of Agreement requires Trans Americas Fiber and its affiliated entities to implement extensive national-security and law-enforcement safeguards as a condition of pursuing an FCC license for the TAM-1 submarine cable system.

**What it does** The agreement establishes compliance obligations covering security governance, cable-system and equipment inventories, covered third parties, vendors and marine-maintenance providers, logical and physical security, personnel screening, incident reporting, operational changes, foreign ownership, annual reporting, audits, and CMA site visits. TAF must appoint a U.S.-citizen Security Officer eligible for a Secret-level clearance, maintain the ability to interrupt U.S.-bound traffic, provide advance notice of specified equipment, provider, testing, and operational changes, and obtain CMA non-objection where required. Security incidents must generally be reported within 48 hours, while service providers must report breaches to TAF within 72 hours.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind TAF US, TAF BVI, TAFS, their successors, affiliates, covered third parties, service providers, contractors, and personnel with relevant access to TAM-1, its principal equipment, domestic communications, or sensitive U.S. records. The CMAs—DHS, DOJ, and DoD—receive monitoring, objection, audit, and inspection rights.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom’s use of enforceable, continuing mitigation to address risks associated with foreign participation in a U.S.-connected submarine cable. Breach may lead the CMAs to recommend that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, or terminate relevant authorizations; the agreement automatically terminates if TAF does not obtain an FCC license.

**Key dates and numbers**
- March 26, 2025: date printed on the LOA.
- 30 days: common advance-notice period for changes, access, equipment, providers, and operations.
- 48 hours: CMA incident-reporting deadline; 72 hours applies to specified service-provider breach notices.
- 60 days: deadline for initial cable, covered-third-party, equipment, and service-provider information.
- 90 days: CMA response period for many notices and deadline for several policies and reports.
- 5 percent: foreign ownership/control threshold requiring notice under the change-in-control provision.
