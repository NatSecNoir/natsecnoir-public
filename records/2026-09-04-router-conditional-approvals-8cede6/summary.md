PSHSB announces that two routers received Department of War Conditional Approval and therefore are exempt from the FCC Covered List.

**What it does** The Public Notice updates the Covered List and the associated Conditional Approvals appendix to exclude Gryphon Safety Online, Inc.’s Parental Control WiFi Router and Sercomm Corporation’s Air4992 Wi-Fi 7 Router. PSHSB finds that each approval is a “specific determination” that the device does not pose risks to U.S. national security, and says the Commission is required to update the list accordingly. The notice also republishes the broader Covered List and identifies the devices’ approval periods.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects Gryphon and Sercomm and entities that manufacture, test, authorize, purchase, or use the identified routers. It also provides guidance for telecommunications certification bodies, test laboratories, and equipment-authorization applicants. The notice concerns the Covered List regime for routers produced in foreign countries, while preserving the general list’s other covered equipment and services.

**Why it matters** The notice demonstrates how Executive Branch Conditional Approvals modify the scope of the FCC Covered List without removing the general router category. It clarifies that qualifying devices are exempt only through the applicable approval and highlights the Commission’s duty to maintain and monitor the list under its rules.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released July 20, 2026; DA 26-756.
- Routers were added to the Covered List on March 23, 2026.
- Both Conditional Approvals run through January 10, 2028.
- Relevant proceedings: WC Docket No. 18-89, ET Docket No. 21-232, and EA Docket No. 21-233.
