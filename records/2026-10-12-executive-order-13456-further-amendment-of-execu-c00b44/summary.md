This executive order substantially revises the framework governing the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States (CFIUS) and assigns executive-branch responsibilities for reviewing and mitigating national-security risks from foreign investment.

**What it does** The order replaces the operative text of Executive Order 11858, formally establishes CFIUS under section 721 of the Defense Production Act, adds the United States Trade Representative and the Director of the Office of Science and Technology Policy to the Committee, and permits other officials to be added case by case. It assigns specified review, investigation, regulatory, reporting, and congressional-notification functions to the Secretary of the Treasury; establishes lead-agency procedures; and authorizes risk-mitigation agreements or conditions after a written assessment of the transaction’s threat, vulnerabilities, and potential consequences. The Secretary of Commerce receives duties to collect, analyze, and report information about foreign investment.

**Who it affects** The order governs CFIUS, participating departments and agencies, lead agencies, the Treasury Secretary as chair, the parties to transactions reviewed by the Committee, and executive officials involved in national-security analysis, including the Director of National Intelligence.

**Why it matters** It provides the central executive-branch operating structure for CFIUS reviews under the amended statute, including when investigations must occur, when matters go to the President, and how mitigation is negotiated and monitored. It also preserves other agency authorities and states that the order creates no enforceable rights.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Signed January 23, 2008; published January 25, 2008, at 73 FR 4677.
- Implements section 721 of the Defense Production Act of 1950, as amended (50 U.S.C. App. 2170).
- Revokes section 801 of Executive Order 12919 (1994) and further amends Executive Order 11858 (1975).
