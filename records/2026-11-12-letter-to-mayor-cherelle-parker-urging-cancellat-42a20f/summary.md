The House Select Committee on the CCP urges Philadelphia Mayor Cherelle Parker to cancel a planned PRC flag-raising ceremony and end the city’s sister-city relationship with Tianjin.

**What it does** The September 2025 letter argues that an official flag-raising for a foreign adversary could signal support for the PRC and provide a platform for CCP propaganda. It raises concerns about the Pennsylvania United Chinese Coalition and the Greater Philadelphia Fujian Hometown Association, citing their reported relationships with PRC and United Front-linked organizations. The letter also references the Committee’s November 2023 “United Front 101” memorandum, concerns about diaspora influence and transnational repression, and Chairman John Moolenaar’s proposed H.R. 4519 concerning sister-city ties involving foreign adversaries.

**Who it affects** The request is directed to Mayor Parker and the City of Philadelphia. It specifically concerns the September 30, 2025, ceremony and Philadelphia’s relationship with Tianjin, while naming local Chinese community organizations and PRC-affiliated or United Front-related bodies discussed in the letter.

**Why it matters** The letter frames municipal ceremonies and sister-city relationships as potential channels for foreign influence. It asks Philadelphia to demonstrate support for U.S. values and for people facing repression by rejecting what the Committee describes as CCP exploitation of local government and civic associations.
