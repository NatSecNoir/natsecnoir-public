The order prohibits ByteDance’s acquisition and ownership of Musical.ly in the United States and requires divestment of specified TikTok-related assets and U.S. user data on national-security grounds.

Note - this is separate from the action in TikTok v. Trump (D.D.C. 2020) action taken under the Commerce Dept ICTS authority under IEEPA and EO 13873 https://cases.justia.com/federal/district-courts/district-of-columbia/dcdce/1:2020cv02658/222257/30/0.pdf?ts=1601371372

which was superseded by TikTok v. Garland SCOTUS opinion
https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/24-656_ca7d.pdf

and then later abrogated by the Protecting Americans from Foreign Adversary Controlled Apps Act, 15 USC 9901

**What it does** The President finds credible evidence that ByteDance’s acquisition of Musical.ly might threaten to impair U.S. national security. It prohibits the transaction and ByteDance’s ownership interests in Musical.ly in the United States. Within 90 days, unless CFIUS grants an extension of up to 30 days, ByteDance and specified affiliates and Chinese shareholders must divest assets supporting TikTok’s U.S. operation and data obtained from U.S. users. ByteDance must certify completion and destruction of covered data; CFIUS may audit and verify compliance. Proposed buyers require advance notice, followed by a 10-business-day CFIUS objection period.

**Who it affects** The order directly binds ByteDance Ltd., its subsidiaries, affiliates, and Chinese shareholders, as well as TikTok Inc. It gives CFIUS implementation, review, auditing, and access authorities and authorizes the Attorney General to enforce the order.

**Why it matters** This is a presidential action under section 721 of the Defense Production Act, using CFIUS authorities to address a completed foreign acquisition involving a major social-media application and U.S. user data. It also bars evasion or circumvention and reserves authority for further protective orders.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Adopted August 14, 2020; published August 19, 2020 (85 FR 51297).
- Divestment deadline: 90 days, with a possible extension of no more than 30 days.
- CFIUS review period for a proposed transfer: 10 business days.
- CFIUS verification concludes within 90 days after divestment certification.
- Authority cited: section 721, 50 U.S.C. 4565.
