The House Select Committee on the CCP asks Eli Lilly to provide information about its clinical trials in China, citing ethical, human-rights, medical-data, and national-security risks associated with trials in Xinjiang and at Chinese military medical facilities.

**What it does** The June 29, 2026 letter from Chairman John Moolenaar to Lilly CEO David A. Ricks identifies more than 220 Lilly-sponsored or collaborative clinical studies in China since 2003, according to the letter, including at least 11 trials involving Xinjiang hospitals and at least 16 involving PRC military medical centers or hospitals between 2016 and 2024. It requests nine categories of information, including Lilly’s Good Clinical Practice due diligence, site inspections, trial and contractor data, protection of intellectual property and sensitive data, and agreements with Chinese companies. The committee asks Lilly to respond by July 17, 2026.

**Who it affects** The request directly concerns Eli Lilly, its Chinese partners and contractors, investigators and trial sites, and patients participating in the identified studies. The letter highlights Chinese PLA hospitals, Xinjiang facilities, Innovent Biologics, and Haisco Pharmaceutical Group.

**Why it matters** The committee links clinical-trial data and biotechnology research to the U.S.-China strategic competition, warning that work at military hospitals could expose American intellectual property and medical data to Chinese military biotechnology programs. It also questions whether participants in Xinjiang and Chinese trials receive meaningful informed consent, while expressly stating that it found no evidence Lilly engaged in illegal activity or wrongdoing.

**Key dates and numbers**
- June 29, 2026: letter date; June 30, 2026: catalogue date.
- July 17, 2026: requested response deadline.
- More than 220: Lilly-linked Chinese clinical studies identified since 2003.
- At least 11: trials involving Xinjiang hospitals; at least 16: trials involving PRC military medical centers or hospitals, during 2016–2024.
- Up to $8.8 billion and $3 billion: potential values cited for Lilly collaborations with Innovent and Haisco, respectively.
