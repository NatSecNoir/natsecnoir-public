Pacific Carriage Limited and Pacific Carriage Limited Inc. commit to extensive national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety safeguards for the Southern Cross Cable System as conditions proposed for related FCC cable-landing authorizations.

**What it does** This Letter of Assurances establishes compliance obligations covering the Southern Cross NEXT Cable System and the existing Southern Cross Cable Network. The licensees must maintain a U.S.-citizen security point of contact; provide DHS with network, equipment, vendor, and operational information; preserve the ability to interrupt U.S. traffic; obtain DHS review of specified equipment, vendor, testing, and network-operations changes; implement logical and physical security, personnel screening, incident reporting, training, annual reporting, third-party audits, and facility-access procedures. DHS may inspect facilities and request remediation, while the FCC may be asked to modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify relevant authorizations if the commitments are breached.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind PCL, PCLI, and their successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates. They reach personnel, contractors, vendors, subcontractors, network operators, and third-party auditors connected with the cable systems, and protect domestic communications carried through U.S. portions of the systems.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for submarine cable infrastructure: DHS receives continuing visibility and approval or objection rights over critical equipment, suppliers, operations, access, and ownership changes. It also links compliance failures to potential FCC licensing consequences.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated July 3, 2020; related FCC files include SCL-LIC-20190809-00026, SCL-97-005, and SCL-MOD-19990216-00002.
- Proposed POC, system information, and principal-equipment information are generally due within 45 days of execution.
- Certain incidents must be reported within 48 hours; requested follow-up reports are due within 15 days.
- Foreign ownership or control changes above 10 percent require notice within 15 days; many equipment or vendor changes require 45 days’ advance notice.
