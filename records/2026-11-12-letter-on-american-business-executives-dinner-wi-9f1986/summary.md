The House Select Committee on the Strategic Competition Between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party requests information from the National Committee on U.S.-China Relations (NCUSCR) about its planned November 15, 2023 dinner with Xi Jinping and senior Chinese Communist Party officials.

**What it does** The November 13, 2023 letter asks NCUSCR and the U.S.-China Business Council (USCBC) to provide, by November 21, a complete list of dinner ticket purchasers; a separate list of individuals and companies that paid $40,000 to sit at Xi’s table; a breakdown of how profits will be distributed among USCBC, NCUSCR, and other entities; and information about steps taken to defend human rights in China and prevent the genocide of Uyghurs and other ethnic minorities in Xinjiang. The letter warns that engagement may expose executives to arbitrary detention, exit bans, raids, market-access pressure, and supply-chain risks.

**Who it affects** The direct recipients are NCUSCR President Stephen Orlins and, through the requests, USCBC and NCUSCR. The inquiry focuses on American companies, financial institutions, and executives attending or purchasing access to the dinner, especially those involved in pharmaceuticals, biotechnology and medical technology, batteries, artificial intelligence, advanced computing, and semiconductor manufacturing.

**Why it matters** The letter links participation in the dinner to congressional scrutiny of business ties, capital and technology flows to Chinese entities, and corporate responses to alleged human-rights abuses and military advancement.

**Key dates and numbers**
- November 13, 2023: letter date.
- November 15, 2023: planned San Francisco dinner.
- November 21, 2023: response deadline.
- $40,000: price identified for a seat at Xi’s table.
- H. Res. 11: cited authority for the committee’s investigation.
