The Letter of Agreement requires Colt Technology Services Group Limited and Camelot Landing, LLC to adopt extensive national-security and law-enforcement safeguards as a condition of the proposed transfer of control of the Yellow submarine cable system.

**What it does** This agreement commits the parties to DHS and DoD oversight after execution, with the agencies expected to petition the FCC to condition Yellow’s cable landing license on compliance. It requires a U.S.-citizen Security Officer eligible for a Secret-level clearance, detailed cable-system and principal-equipment inventories, CMA approval or review of specified network operations, equipment, vendors, sensing technology, and security policies, and prompt interruption capability for U.S.-bound traffic. It also establishes personnel screening, logical and physical security, incident reporting, annual compliance reports, third-party audits, consultations, and site visits.

**Who it affects** The obligations apply primarily to Camelot and Colt, including their successors, subsidiaries, affiliates, vendors, contractors, subcontractors, and relevant personnel. DHS and DoD serve as the Compliance Monitoring Agencies; the FCC is the licensing authority. The arrangement concerns Yellow, formerly held by entities associated with Lumen, Global Crossing Telecommunications, and Level 3.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for foreign participation in U.S. telecommunications infrastructure. It gives the monitoring agencies continuing visibility and approval rights over sensitive equipment, access, personnel, cyber incidents, and operational changes, while allowing FCC license consequences for breach.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated September 6, 2023; FCC application SCL-T/C-20221123-00034 and TT 23-004.
- Yellow entered service November 1, 2000.
- Security incidents must be reported within 48 hours; third-party providers generally have 72 hours to report breaches.
- The agreement uses recurring deadlines of 30, 45, 60, and 90 days, and requires annual compliance reporting.
