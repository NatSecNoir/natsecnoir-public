The agreement conditions DOJ’s non-objection to First Technology Development, Inc.’s FCC authorization on commitments addressing U.S. law-enforcement access, records handling, lawful interception, and security oversight.

**What it does** First agrees to designate a U.S. law-enforcement point of contact, subject to DOJ review, to receive service of process and support lawful requests for U.S. Records. It must make responsive records available promptly and generally within five business days, refer non-U.S.-government requests to DOJ, provide advance notice of records-storage locations, and report material corporate changes. Before beginning interconnected VoIP service or becoming a facilities-based provider, First must give DOJ 60 days’ notice, comply with applicable lawful-interception requirements including CALEA, support an FBI compliance test, and submit annual compliance reports. A breach may lead DOJ to ask the FCC to modify, condition, revoke, cancel, or nullify relevant authorization.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind First, its successors-in-interest, and its handling of U.S. Records, Domestic Communications, and lawful process. They support oversight by DOJ, its National Security Division, the FBI, and the FCC; First is identified as owned by Ruslan, Mahreen, and Hossain Nasir.

**Why it matters** The letter illustrates Team Telecom mitigation as an enforceable condition of FCC authorization, extending beyond approval of foreign-owned telecommunications applications to records access, foreign-government requests, equipment and partner disclosures, cybersecurity incidents, and CALEA readiness.

**Key dates and numbers**
- August 25, 2014: Agreement date.
- 14 days: deadline to notify DOJ and FBI of the designated point of contact after receiving FCC certification.
- Five business days: general deadline for producing U.S. Records and referring non-U.S. government requests.
- January 31, 2015: first annual report due; later reports are due each January 31.
- 60 days: advance notice before beginning interconnected VoIP service or becoming facilities-based.
