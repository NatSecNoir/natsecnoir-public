The House Select Committee on the Strategic Competition Between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party asks the Secretary of Defense and FBI Director for a classified briefing and documents concerning reported PRC-linked espionage attempts at U.S. military bases and other sensitive sites.

**What it does** The September 19, 2023 letter requests details about measures taken by the Department of Defense and Federal Bureau of Investigation to protect sensitive locations, identify suspected agents, and counter espionage. It seeks a classified briefing and all documents related to Pentagon base-security reviews, including reviews of the roughly 1,400 gates at U.S. military bases.

**Who it affects** The requests are directed to Secretary of Defense Lloyd Austin and FBI Director Christopher Wray. The subject matter concerns U.S. military bases, missile sites, sensitive facilities, White House security and communications equipment, and agencies responsible for protecting them. The letter is signed by Chairman Mike Gallagher and other Select Committee members.

**Why it matters** The letter places reported PRC espionage and access attempts at sensitive U.S. sites before senior national-security officials and seeks concrete oversight information. It cites reporting that PRC citizens had gained or attempted access at least 100 times, including an alleged intrusion involving a U.S. Army test range and missile sites, as well as the January 2023 balloon incident.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter date: September 19, 2023.
- Requested briefing and records deadline: October 6, 2023.
- Reported access attempts: at least 100 over the prior few years.
- Base-security review referenced: approximately 1,400 gates.
- Committee authority cited: H. Res. 11.
