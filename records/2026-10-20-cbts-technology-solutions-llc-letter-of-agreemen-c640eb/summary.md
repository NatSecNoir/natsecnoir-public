The Department of Justice notifies the FCC that the Committee for the Assessment of Foreign Participation in the United States Telecommunications Services Sector has terminated CBTS Technology Solutions LLC’s mitigation agreement and requests removal of the related license condition.

**What it does** The October 13, 2026 letter asks the Commission to remove the condition on CBTS’s referenced licenses requiring compliance with the October 10, 2024, mitigation agreement with the Department of Justice. The Committee states that it terminated the agreement after determining that the mitigation is “no longer warranted,” and asks the FCC to notify it once the license condition has been changed.

**Who it affects** The action directly concerns CBTS Technology Solutions LLC, the FCC licenses identified in the letter, the Committee, and the Department of Justice. FCC staff and the licensees associated with the cited proceedings are the parties involved in implementing or receiving notice of the requested condition change.

**Why it matters** The letter records the end of a Team Telecom mitigation arrangement and seeks corresponding removal of the FCC license condition. It therefore marks a change in the national-security safeguards attached to CBTS’s telecommunications authorizations, although the document does not describe the underlying agreement’s terms or explain why mitigation is no longer warranted.

**Key dates and numbers**
- October 13, 2026: DOJ letter notifying the FCC of termination.
- October 10, 2024: mitigation agreement the license condition required CBTS to follow.
- TT-24-010 TO -011; FCC File Nos. ITC-214-20071024-00434 and ITC-T/C-20240207-00028; WC Docket 24-42.
