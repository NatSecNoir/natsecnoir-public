The Letter of Agreement establishes binding national-security and law-enforcement mitigation commitments for operation of the BRUSA submarine cable system and supports FCC review of a transaction transferring certain negative control rights from KKR to Pontegadea.

**What it does** The Telxius Parties agree to maintain a U.S.-citizen security point of contact, provide cable-system and principal-equipment information, control changes to equipment and vendors, and obtain approval for deployment of distributed acoustic sensing technology. The LOA also requires logical and physical security policies, personnel screening, incident and breach reporting, annual compliance reports, possible third-party audits, and CMA access to facilities and records. Telefónica, Telxius Telecom, and Pontegadea agree not to cause a breach and to provide adequate resources for compliance. The LOA supersedes and terminates Telxius USA’s July 30, 2017 Letter of Assurances upon FCC approval.

**Who it affects** The obligations primarily bind Telxius Cable América, Telxius Cable USA, and Telxius Cable Puerto Rico, with specified Section 19 obligations for Telefónica, Telxius Telecom, and Pontegadea. DHS, DOJ, and DoD act as the Compliance Monitoring Agencies, while the FCC is asked to condition its consent on compliance.

**Why it matters** The agreement links continued cable-landing-license authorization to operational control, supply-chain oversight, cybersecurity, lawful-process support, and protection of U.S. communications and records. Breach may support recommendations to modify, condition, revoke, cancel, or terminate FCC authorization.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated December 9, 2022; FCC application references include SCL-T/C-20220222-00006 to -00009 and TT #22-004 to -009.
- POC nomination is due within 30 days of execution; security and physical-security policies are generally due within 90 days of FCC approval.
- Certain incidents and breaches must be reported within 48 hours; third-party disclosures are generally due within 72 hours.
