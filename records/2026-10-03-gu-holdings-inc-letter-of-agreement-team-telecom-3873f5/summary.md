The Letter of Agreement requires GU Holdings Inc. to implement extensive national-security and law-enforcement safeguards for the Firmina submarine cable system as a condition recommended for its FCC cable landing license.

**What it does** This agreement with DHS, DOJ, and DoD establishes compliance commitments covering a U.S.-citizen security point of contact; disclosure of cable-system architecture, operations, equipment, vendors, and restoration plans; operational ability to interrupt U.S. traffic; advance notice and agency review of equipment, vendor, testing, and operational changes; logical and physical security; personnel screening; incident reporting; training; annual compliance reports; third-party audits; and agency consultation and site visits. GU Holdings must also notify the agencies of certain foreign ownership or control changes and permit specified disclosures of confidential FCC submissions.

**Who it affects** The obligations bind GU Holdings and extend to its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, affiliates, vendors, contractors, subcontractors, and service providers involved with Firmina. DHS, DOJ, and DoD act as the Compliance Monitoring Agencies, while the FCC may condition, modify, revoke, cancel, or terminate relevant authorization upon a breach.

**Why it matters** The LOA makes Team Telecom mitigation operationally enforceable through detailed agency approvals, reporting deadlines, audit rights, and potential FCC license remedies. It also links cybersecurity and supply-chain controls to continued operation of a U.S.-connected submarine cable.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed and DocuSigned on November 30, 2022; it takes effect only if the FCC grants the underlying license.
