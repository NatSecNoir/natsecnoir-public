The House Select Committee on the CCP and Senate Judiciary Committee Chairman Charles E. Grassley ask NASA and the FBI to investigate possible Wolf Amendment compliance failures involving NASA-funded research linked to Chinese institutions and to produce related records.

**What it does** The two letters, sent to NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman and FBI Director Kash Patel, focus on NASA Award No. 80NSSC23K0265 and research associated with Stanford Professor Wendy Mao. They request award and subaward records, information about any FBI-certified congressional waiver, NASA’s compliance policies and reviews, and the handling of possible violations. The FBI letter additionally seeks records and outcomes for Wolf Amendment waivers, the Bureau’s assessment process, briefings to NASA, and tracking of referrals.

**Who it affects** The requests directly affect NASA, the FBI, and researchers and institutions involved in the cited award or related collaborations. The letters identify Wendy Mao, Hong Yang, Stanford University, HPSTAR, the Chinese Academy of Engineering Physics, the University of Science and Technology of China, Argonne National Laboratory, and the Department of Energy. HPSTAR and CAEP are discussed in connection with the Commerce BIS Entity List.

**Why it matters** The letters frame potential access to taxpayer-funded research, Chinese state infrastructure, and nuclear-related institutions as national-security and research-integrity concerns. They seek oversight information rather than imposing a new restriction or making a final finding of a Wolf Amendment violation.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letters dated February 12, 2026; responses requested by February 26, 2026.
- NASA award: 80NSSC23K0265.
- The letters cite at least 58 publications acknowledging DOE funding or support, including 31 with HPSTAR since 2013.
- Requested historical waiver and compliance information generally covers fiscal years 2015–2026.
