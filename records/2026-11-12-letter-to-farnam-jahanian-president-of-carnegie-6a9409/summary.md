The House Select Committee on the CCP requests that Carnegie Mellon University disclose information about Chinese-national students, their funding and research, and university safeguards addressing national-security and technology-transfer risks.

**What it does** The March 18, 2025 letter from Chairman John Moolenaar to Carnegie Mellon President Farnam Jahanian asks for written responses by April 1, 2025. It seeks records on the prior universities and research affiliations of Chinese-national students; their tuition and other funding sources; their programs, laboratories, and research; and country-by-country applicant, admission, and enrollment data. It also asks about the share of graduate students and tuition revenue represented by Chinese nationals, participation in federally funded research, screening and monitoring mechanisms, restrictions involving export-controlled coursework, faculty ties to China, and post-graduation destinations.

**Who it affects** The direct recipient is Carnegie Mellon University, while the requests concern its Chinese-national undergraduate, graduate, and PhD students, faculty, research programs, and federally funded projects. The letter specifically references research areas including artificial intelligence, quantum computing, semiconductors, aerospace engineering, and robotics, and asks about links to Chinese institutions, talent-recruitment programs, grants, and companies.

**Why it matters** The committee frames foreign-student enrollment and academic research access as potential channels for espionage, intellectual-property theft, and transfer of dual-use technology to China. The letter invokes the committee’s investigative authority under H. Res. 5, section 4(a), and seeks institutional transparency rather than imposing a regulatory order or announcing an enforcement action.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter dated March 18, 2025; known document date is March 19, 2025.
- Requested response deadline: April 1, 2025.
- Requests for information: 6; numbered questions: 14.
- Cited H. Res. 5, section 4(a), as the committee’s authority.
