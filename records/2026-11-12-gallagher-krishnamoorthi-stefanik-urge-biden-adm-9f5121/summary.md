The letter urges the Departments of Commerce, Defense, and the Treasury to investigate Autel Robotics and Autel Robotics USA LLC for possible placement on the Commerce Entity List, the DoD Chinese Military Companies List, and the Treasury Non-SDN Chinese Military-Industrial Complex Companies List.

**What it does** The members ask the agencies to assess whether Autel meets the designation criteria for those three lists and, if not, to brief the Select Committee on the reasons for declining to designate it. The letter presents allegations concerning Autel’s connections to the People’s Liberation Army, Chinese state support, military and police-related activities, surveillance operations in Xinjiang, component sourcing from ZTE and HiSilicon, and possible distribution of drones in Russia under the Patriot name.

**Who it affects** The request directly concerns PRC-headquartered Autel Robotics and its apparent U.S. subsidiary, Autel Robotics USA LLC. It asks Commerce, DoD, and Treasury to conduct the potential designation reviews, with copies sent to the Secretaries of State and Energy.

**Why it matters** The letter frames Autel drones as a national-security and data-security risk because local law-enforcement and government entities in the United States use them. It also illustrates congressional pressure for coordinated action across three separate federal designation regimes, while leaving any eventual listing decision to the executive agencies.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter dated November 29, 2023; catalogue date: November 30, 2023.
- Requests review under three designation regimes: Commerce Entity List, DoD Chinese Military Companies List, and Treasury NS-CMIC List.
- The letter is four pages and cites H. Res. 11 as the Select Committee’s investigative authority.
- Autel Robotics USA LLC was established in 2017, according to the letter.
