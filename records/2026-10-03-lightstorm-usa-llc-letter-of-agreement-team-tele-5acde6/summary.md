Poseidon International Connectivity II, LLC acknowledges that it will assume RAM Telecom International, Inc.’s obligations under the 2017 National Security Agreement for the SEA-US Cable System if the FCC approves the proposed license assignment, and DHS states that it does not object.

**What it does** This letter explains that Poseidon will become RAM Telecom’s successor under Section 8.12 of the SEA-US NSA upon assignment of RAM Telecom’s submarine cable landing license interests. It provides for DHS to countersign its non-objection, transmission of the countersigned letter to the court-appointed receiver, and notification to the FCC that the assignment should be granted subject to Poseidon’s compliance with the NSA. Poseidon also undertakes to provide DHS with compliance contacts after assignment.

**Who it affects** The arrangement directly concerns Poseidon, RAM Telecom, the SEA-US Cable System, and the Committee for the Assessment of Foreign Participation in the United States Telecommunications Services Sector. DOJ, DHS, and DoD receive the submission because of their Committee roles; DHS is identified as the sole Compliance Monitoring Agency for the SEA-US NSA.

**Why it matters** The letter preserves national-security mitigation obligations through a change in ownership or control of a submarine cable license interest. It links FCC approval of the assignment to continued performance of the existing NSA rather than treating the transfer as releasing the successor from those obligations.
