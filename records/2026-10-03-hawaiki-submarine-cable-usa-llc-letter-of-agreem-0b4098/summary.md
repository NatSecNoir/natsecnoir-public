The Letter of Agreement sets binding national-security and law-enforcement mitigation commitments for Hawaiki Submarine Cable USA LLC and BW Digital Pte. Ltd. in connection with the proposed transfer of control of Hawaiki and its U.S. cable license to BW Digital.

**What it does** It requires the Hawaiki Parties to maintain a U.S.-citizen security point of contact; provide system, equipment, vendor, and operational information; preserve the ability to interrupt U.S. traffic; obtain review of specified equipment, vendors, testing, and operational changes; and implement logical and physical security, personnel screening, incident reporting, training, annual compliance reporting, and possible third-party audits. The Compliance Monitoring Agencies may inspect facilities and recommend FCC license remedies for breach.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind Hawaiki Submarine Cable USA LLC, BW Digital, and their successors, assigns, subsidiaries, affiliates, contractors, and service providers, with oversight by DHS, DOJ, and DoD. They address Hawaiki’s U.S. cable landing facilities, domestic communications infrastructure, wet plant, records, personnel, and supply chain.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for foreign participation in a submarine cable transaction. It makes FCC approval conditional on compliance, supersedes HSC’s October 31, 2019 Letter of Assurances upon effectiveness, and preserves agency control over security, access, equipment, and ownership-change risks.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed March 23, 2022; proposed transfer application SCL-ASG-20210831-00038.
- Security POC nomination due within 30 days of execution; system information within 60 days.
- Certain breaches must be reported within 48 hours; third-party breach notice within 72 hours.
- Foreign ownership or control changes above 10% require notice within 15 days.
