GlobeNet, BTG New Fund, and Oi S.A. agree to extensive national-security and law-enforcement mitigation measures as a condition for FCC approval of the proposed transfer of control of GlobeNet and its submarine cable license.

**What it does** This Letter of Agreement (LOA) establishes compliance obligations monitored by DHS, DOJ, and DoD. It requires a U.S.-citizen security point of contact; delivery of network, equipment, vendor, and restoration information; operational capabilities to interrupt U.S. traffic; advance notice and review of equipment, vendor, testing, and operational changes; logical and physical security controls; personnel screening; incident and breach reporting; employee training; annual compliance reports; and potential third-party audits and site visits. The parties must report qualifying incidents to the agencies within 48 hours, while covered third-party providers generally have 72 hours to report breaches.

**Who it affects** The obligations bind GlobeNet, BTG New Fund, and Oi S.A. as minority owner, with compliance oversight by DHS, DOJ, and DoD and licensing consequences administered through the FCC. The covered system connects the United States with Bermuda, Brazil, Colombia, and Venezuela.

**Why it matters** The LOA converts Team Telecom review concerns into enforceable conditions on a U.S.-connected submarine cable transaction. Breach may support a recommendation that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify relevant authorization.

**Key dates and numbers**

- The 2013 Letter of Assurances is superseded and terminated upon FCC approval conditioned on this LOA.
