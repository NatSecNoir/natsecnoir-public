The House Select Committee on the CCP urged TikTok to immediately pursue a qualified divestiture and warned Apple and Google that they must take steps to comply with the Protecting Americans from Foreign Adversary Controlled Applications Act by January 19, 2025, following the D.C. Circuit’s decision upholding the law.

**What it does** The three letters, dated December 13, 2024, describe the December 6, 2024, unanimous D.C. Circuit ruling in *TikTok v. Garland* and its rejection of TikTok’s constitutional claims. The letter to TikTok urges a qualified divestiture that would end foreign-adversary control and prevent specified operational relationships with formerly affiliated entities. The letters to Apple and Google state that, absent a qualified divestiture, the Act makes it unlawful for app marketplaces to provide services to distribute, maintain, or update TikTok.

**Who it affects** The letters directly address TikTok CEO Shou Zi Chew, Apple CEO Tim Cook, and Google CEO Sundar Pichai. They also concern Apple and Google’s U.S. app-marketplace operations, TikTok users in the United States, and the company’s relationship with entities controlled by a foreign adversary.

**Why it matters** The correspondence presents the court decision as confirming the law’s constitutionality and emphasizes Congress’s view that TikTok has had sufficient time to pursue compliance. It highlights the potential effect on TikTok’s availability in the United States and the obligations of major app marketplaces if no qualified divestiture occurs.

**Key dates and numbers**
- April 24, 2024: The Act was signed into law.
- December 6, 2024: The D.C. Circuit unanimously upheld the Act in *TikTok v. Garland*, No. 24-1113.
- December 13, 2024: The Committee issued the letters.
- January 19, 2025: The stated deadline for qualified divestiture or app-marketplace compliance, subject to a possible 90-day presidential extension based on progress toward divestiture.
- The letters state that TikTok had had 233 days and counting to pursue a solution.
