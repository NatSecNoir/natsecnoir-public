The report concludes that JPMorgan, Bank of America, and Morgan Stanley helped Chinese companies raise billions despite U.S. government designations tied to military activity and forced-labor risks, while exploiting gaps in existing law.

**What it does** The House Select Committee on the CCP examines JPMorgan and Bank of America’s 2025 Hong Kong IPO underwriting for Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., Ltd. (CATL), identified by the Department of War on January 7, 2025, under Section 1260H, and Morgan Stanley’s role as joint sponsor of Zijin Gold’s 2025 Hong Kong IPO. It reviews bank diligence materials, company representations, supply-chain evidence, and internal approvals. The report alleges that the banks relied heavily on CATL’s denials, did not obtain complete audits, and proceeded with Zijin Gold after recognizing its parent Zijin Mining’s UFLPA Entity List designation. It recommends prohibiting U.S. financial institutions from raising capital for blacklisted foreign-adversary entities, expanding OFAC’s authorities, and designating CATL and relevant subsidiaries on the NS-CMIC List.

**Who it affects** The report directly concerns CATL, Zijin Gold, Zijin Mining, JPMorgan, Bank of America, and Morgan Stanley, and discusses their relationships with Chinese military-linked, state-owned, telecommunications, and forced-labor-related entities.

**Why it matters** The report argues that Section 1260H and the UFLPA largely impose identification or import restrictions—not a general prohibition on underwriting—allowing U.S. banks to finance designated companies through overseas markets. It presents the transactions as evidence of a systemic gap between national-security designations and financial consequences.

**Key dates and numbers**
- January 7, 2025: CATL was placed on the Section 1260H list.
- January 14, 2025: Zijin Mining and Xinjiang subsidiaries were added to the UFLPA Entity List.
- May 2025: JPMorgan and Bank of America underwrote CATL’s Hong Kong IPO.
- September 2025: Zijin Gold raised approximately $3.2 billion in its Hong Kong IPO.
- April 2026: CATL announced a further H-share placement; the report says JPMorgan, Bank of America, and Morgan Stanley participated.
