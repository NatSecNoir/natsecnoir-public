The House Select Committee on Strategic Competition between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party asks FBI Director Christopher Wray to brief the Committee on the FBI’s efforts to investigate and combat alleged PRC law-enforcement outposts operating in the United States.

**What it does** The February 24, 2023 letter from Chairman Mike Gallagher describes the alleged overseas police stations as instruments of transnational repression and asks the FBI to schedule a briefing. It poses seven questions concerning the FBI’s prior awareness of the New York City outpost, the existence of other facilities, investigative and closure efforts, available resources and legal tools, intelligence-gathering functions, targeting of U.S. residents and citizens, the alleged roles of Operation Fox Hunt and Operation Skynet, and coordination with the State Department and foreign partners.

**Who it affects** The request is directed to FBI Director Christopher A. Wray and concerns American citizens, residents, visitors, and members of the Chinese diaspora, including Uyghur and Tibetan minorities, whom the letter says may face coercion or intimidation. It also concerns PRC-linked facilities alleged to conduct law-enforcement tasks on U.S. soil.

**Why it matters** The letter frames the alleged outposts as a sovereignty, civil-rights, and national-security issue, and presses the FBI to explain whether it acted promptly and has adequate authority and resources. It follows reporting about an FBI search of a New York City location and cites Safeguard Defenders’ identification of more than 100 such stations worldwide.

**Key dates and numbers**
- February 24, 2023: letter date.
- More than 100: PRC police stations identified globally by Safeguard Defenders.
- Seven: questions posed to the FBI Director.
