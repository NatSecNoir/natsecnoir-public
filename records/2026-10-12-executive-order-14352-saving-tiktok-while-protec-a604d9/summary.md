The order determines that the proposed divestiture of TikTok and related applications is a “qualified divestiture” under the Protecting Americans from Foreign Adversary Controlled Applications Act, while directing a temporary enforcement pause to allow the transaction to be completed.

**What it does** The order approves the proposed Framework Agreement in principle, under which a new United States-based joint venture would be majority-owned and controlled by U.S. persons, with ByteDance Ltd. and its affiliates owning less than 20 percent. It states that the arrangement would place algorithms, code, content-moderation decisions, and sensitive U.S. user data under specified U.S. controls. The Attorney General is directed not to enforce the Act or impose penalties for 120 days, and to issue guidance and letters to relevant providers. The order also amends the 2020 ByteDance/Musical.ly divestment order and conditions mitigation of related CFIUS concerns on an agreement with certain investors.

**Who it affects** The order directly concerns TikTok, ByteDance, Lemon8, CapCut, the proposed joint venture and its investors, application stores, hosting providers, trusted security partners, and federal officials responsible for enforcement and oversight. It affects the roughly 170 million Americans identified as TikTok users, including creators and businesses.

**Why it matters** The order uses the Act’s qualified-divestiture mechanism to permit continued operation of TikTok and associated applications while addressing the administration’s stated concerns about foreign-adversary control, data access, algorithmic influence, and operational relationships.

**Key dates and numbers**
- September 25, 2025: Order signed and adopted.
- January 19, 2025: Act’s prohibitions became effective for the covered applications.
- 120 days: Directed pause on DOJ enforcement from the order’s date.
- Less than 20 percent: Maximum stated ownership or control by foreign-adversary entities or persons in the new joint venture.
- About 170 million: Americans identified as using TikTok.
