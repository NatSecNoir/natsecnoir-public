The President prohibits HieFo Corporation’s acquisition of specified EMCORE Corporation assets and requires divestment under section 721 of the Defense Production Act because the transaction might threaten U.S. national security.

**What it does** The order prohibits the April 30, 2024, transaction and HieFo’s ownership of interests or rights in EMCORE’s digital-chip and related wafer design, fabrication, and processing businesses. It requires HieFo and its affiliates to divest the assets, including intellectual property, contracts, inventory, property, permits, real estate, and accounts receivable. Until CFIUS verifies completion, access to the assets and non-public technical information is restricted, proposed buyers must be notified to CFIUS, and transfers require a 30-day non-objection period. CFIUS may impose mitigation measures, conduct audits and inspections, and enforce compliance.

**Who it affects** The order directly binds HieFo, its affiliates, and the EMCORE assets; it also assigns implementation, verification, and enforcement responsibilities to CFIUS and authorizes action by the Attorney General.

**Why it matters** This is a presidential CFIUS remedy that unwinds a completed acquisition involving semiconductor-related assets and a company controlled by a citizen of the People’s Republic of China. It combines mandatory divestment with interim access controls, continuing reporting, buyer review, and post-divestment verification.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Order date: January 2, 2026; Federal Register publication: January 8, 2026.
- Transaction completed: April 30, 2024.
- Divestment deadline: 180 calendar days after the order, unless extended by CFIUS.
- Access-control measures: due within 7 calendar days after the order or CFIUS notification.
- CFIUS verification: within 90 calendar days after divestment certification; buyer notification requires a 30-day CFIUS non-objection period.
