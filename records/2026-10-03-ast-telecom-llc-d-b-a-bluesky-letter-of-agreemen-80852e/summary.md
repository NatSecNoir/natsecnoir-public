The U.S. Government Parties and the Licensees irrevocably terminate their February 2, 2011 mitigation agreement because Amalgamated Telecom Holdings Limited (ATH) assumed the applicable obligations through a May 7, 2018 Letter of Assurances to DHS.

**What it does**
The agreement ends the prior arrangement concerning the transfer to ATH of control of FCC licenses and authorizations associated with the American Samoa-Hawaii Cable System. It states that the Letter of Assurances modified and updated the Licensees’ obligations, making continuation of the earlier agreement unnecessary. Under Section 7.6, all rights are relinquished and all duties and obligations under the earlier agreement are waived and released.

**Who it affects**
The parties are DOJ and DHS, together identified as the U.S. Government Parties, and American Samoa Hawaii Cable, Samoa American Samoa Cable, American Samoa License, and AST Telecom, LLC d/b/a Blue Sky Communications, collectively the Licensees. ATH is the transferee that undertook the relevant obligations. The covered infrastructure is a fiber-optic submarine cable system linking the United States and the Independent State of Samoa.

**Why it matters**
This document records a transition in Team Telecom mitigation from the 2011 agreement to ATH’s Letter of Assurances, while preserving the obligations in updated form rather than leaving the cable-system transaction without stated government assurances. The termination became effective upon the last authorized signature.

**Key dates and numbers**
- February 2, 2011: date of the agreement being terminated.
- May 7, 2018: date stated on the Letter of Assurances and the Licensees’ signature blocks.
- Section 7.6: provision authorizing irrevocable termination, waiver, and release.
