The Letter of Agreement requires AFRIX Telecom LLC to adopt extensive national-security, law-enforcement, data-protection, and network-security commitments as a condition supporting FCC consent to its Section 214 international telecommunications application.

**What it does** This Team Telecom agreement establishes controls over AFRIX’s U.S. records, domestic communications infrastructure, lawful-process response, personnel, principal equipment, outsourced and offshore services, and network operations centers. AFRIX must maintain a U.S. law-enforcement point of contact, preserve responsive information in the United States, comply with lawful U.S. process, restrict disclosure to foreign governments or persons, submit security plans and equipment/provider lists, and obtain Department of Justice objection or non-objection for specified changes. It also requires incident reporting, annual compliance reports, site visits, and advance notice of ownership, service, storage, and operational changes.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind AFRIX and extend to its employees, contractors, vendors, managed network service providers, outsourced or offshore providers, and foreign persons with remote or logical access to domestic infrastructure. DOJ, including the FBI, is the principal reviewing and oversight agency, with the DHS and DoD participating in the Team Telecom review; the FCC is the licensing authority.

**Why it matters** The LOA links AFRIX’s international Section 214 authority to enforceable safeguards for U.S. communications and records, including limits on foreign access and mandatory handling of lawful surveillance and production requests. A breach or unresolved national-security, law-enforcement, or public-safety concern could lead DOJ to ask the FCC to modify, condition, revoke, cancel, or nullify the relevant authorization.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated April 27, 2020; FCC docket ITC-214-20190821-00141.
- LEPOC PII due within 15 days after FCC approval; responsive records generally due within five days of lawful U.S. process.
- Security plans due within 90 days of the LOA date; third-party breach or loss notices within 48 hours and AFRIX’s DOJ notice within 72 hours.
- Annual report due one year after the LOA date and every year thereafter.
