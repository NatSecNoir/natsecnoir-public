The President prohibits Nippon Steel Corporation’s proposed acquisition of United States Steel Corporation on national-security grounds under section 721 of the Defense Production Act.

**What it does** The order finds credible evidence that Nippon Steel, Nippon Steel North America, and 2023 Merger Subsidiary might take action threatening U.S. national security, and concludes that other legal authorities are inadequate to address the matter. It prohibits the proposed acquisition and any substantially similar transaction, including arrangements intended to evade or circumvent the order. The purchasers and U.S. Steel must fully and permanently abandon the transaction, certify that abandonment to CFIUS, and provide weekly compliance certifications until the abandonment is certified and verified. CFIUS may impose additional protective measures and use remedies available under section 721; the Attorney General may enforce the order.

**Who it affects** The order directly binds the three purchasers and U.S. Steel, while authorizing CFIUS and the Attorney General to act. The Secretary of the Treasury is directed to transmit the order to the parties.

**Why it matters** This is a presidential CFIUS action blocking a proposed acquisition and requiring divestment-like abandonment steps rather than permitting the transaction subject to negotiated conditions. It also preserves executive authority to issue further orders concerning the parties.

**Key dates and numbers**
- January 3, 2025: order date.
- 30 days: deadline for abandonment, unless CFIUS extends it.
- Weekly: compliance certifications required until abandonment is certified and verified.
- Section 721 of the Defense Production Act; 50 U.S.C. 4565.
- Published at 90 FR 2605; FR Doc. 2025-00621.
