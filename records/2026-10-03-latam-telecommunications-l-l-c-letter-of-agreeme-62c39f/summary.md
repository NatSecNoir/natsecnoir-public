The Committee requires LATAM Telecommunications, L.L.C. and Puerto Rico Telephone Company, Inc. to implement extensive national-security and law-enforcement safeguards as a condition of FCC approval of modifications to the América Móvil Submarine Cable System (AMX1).

**What it does** The Agreement conditions the AMX1 cable landing license on compliance with controls covering security officers, cable-system information, network operations, covered third parties, principal equipment, service providers, personnel screening, physical and logical access, incident response, reporting, audits, and CMA oversight. Licensees must maintain the ability to interrupt U.S. traffic, provide detailed equipment and vendor information, obtain advance notice or non-objection for specified changes, and report qualifying incidents within 48 hours. Equipment containing technology associated with foreign adversaries, Foreign Countries of Concern, the FCC Covered List, or the Commerce Entity List requires prior CMA non-objection.

**Who it affects** The direct obligations apply to LATAM and PRTC, their affiliates and subsidiaries, covered third parties, service providers, equipment providers, foreign personnel, and other parties with access to AMX1, its U.S. facilities, communications, or sensitive records. DHS, DOJ, and DoD serve as Compliance Monitoring Agencies.

**Why it matters** The NSA converts Team Telecom mitigation commitments into an enforceable framework for a submarine cable connecting the United States and foreign landing points. A breach may support a Committee recommendation that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify the relevant authorization.

**Key dates and numbers**
- FCC referral to the Committee: July 12, 2024.
- Security officer nominations: within 30 days of FCC approval.
- Initial system, covered-party, equipment, and service-provider submissions: generally within 90 days.
- Security policies and access lists: generally within 120 days.
- Incident reporting: 48 hours; service-provider breach disclosure: 72 hours.
- CMA objections or non-objections: generally within 45 days for Articles 5.1–5.5 submissions.
