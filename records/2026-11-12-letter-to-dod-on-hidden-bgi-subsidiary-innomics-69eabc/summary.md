The House Select Committee on the CCP urges Secretary of Defense Lloyd Austin to implement Section 1312 of the FY2024 National Defense Authorization Act and consider seven Chinese biotechnology entities for inclusion on the Department of Defense’s Section 1260H list of Chinese Military Companies.

**What it does** The March 29, 2024 letter describes alleged military-civil fusion, Chinese Communist Party, People’s Liberation Army, Russian, and state-support ties involving MGI Group, Complete Genomics, Innomics, STOmics, Origincell, Vazyme Biotech, and Axbio. It specifically argues that MGI Group and Complete Genomics are BGI subsidiaries; that Innomics and STOmics operate in the United States while allegedly obscuring or inconsistently presenting their BGI affiliations; and that the other entities have relevant military, government, investment, or research connections. The members ask DoD to act “without delay” and request a staff briefing.

**Who it affects** The request is directed to DoD and concerns the seven named biotechnology companies and their alleged affiliates, investors, and institutional partners. It also asks for a briefing with Select Committee staff.

**Why it matters** If DoD determines the entities meet Section 1260H criteria, listing could identify them as Chinese Military Companies under the statutory regime. The letter frames biotechnology, genetic data, sequencing, and biological sample infrastructure as national-security concerns and links the requested review to the FY2024 NDAA’s 180-day implementation requirement.

**Key dates and numbers**
- March 29, 2024: letter date.
- April 1, 2024: catalogue document date.
- FY2024 NDAA Section 1312: directs analysis of certain biotechnology entities.
- Section 1260H: DoD Chinese Military Companies listing authority.
- May 1, 2024: requested date for a DoD implementation briefing.
- Seven entities are identified for consideration: MGI Group, Complete Genomics, Innomics, STOmics, Origincell, Vazyme Biotech, and Axbio.
