The House Select Committee on the CCP requests that the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign provide information about Chinese national students, their funding and research participation, and university safeguards for sensitive and federally funded technology.

**What it does** The March 18, 2025 letter from Chairman John Moolenaar to University President Timothy K. Killeen asks for records concerning the prior universities and research affiliations of Chinese national students; their tuition and other funding sources; the programs, laboratories, and research initiatives in which they participate; and country-by-country application, admission, and enrollment data. It also poses questions about the share of graduate students and tuition revenue represented by Chinese nationals, participation in federally funded research, screening and monitoring practices, export-controlled coursework, faculty ties to Chinese institutions, and post-graduation destinations.

**Who it affects** The direct recipient is the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. The requests concern Chinese national undergraduate, graduate, and PhD students; university faculty and research programs; and projects funded by agencies including DARPA, the Department of Defense, the Department of Energy, and the National Science Foundation.

**Why it matters** The Committee frames foreign-student enrollment and access to advanced research as potential channels for technology transfer, intellectual-property theft, and support for China’s military and economic objectives. The letter seeks institution-specific information to assess risks involving artificial intelligence, quantum computing, semiconductors, aerospace, robotics, and other sensitive fields, while asserting the Committee’s investigative authority under H. Res. 5, section 4(a).

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter dated March 18, 2025; the catalogue date is March 19, 2025.
- Written responses requested by April 1, 2025.
- Requests for information: 6; additional questions: 14.
- Cited GAO report: GAO-23-106114; cited House report: *CCP on the Quad* (September 2024).
