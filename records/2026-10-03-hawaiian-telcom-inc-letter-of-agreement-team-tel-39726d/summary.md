The letter confirms Cincinnati Bell Inc.’s commitment to maintain and, if necessary, update existing national-security mitigation arrangements as it seeks FCC consent to acquire control of Hawaiian Telcom’s licensees and related submarine-cable interests.

**What it does** The letter describes the proposed transfer of control from Hawaiian Telcom Holdco, Inc. to Cincinnati Bell, under which Cincinnati Bell would indirectly acquire 100% of Hawaiian Telcom, Inc. and Hawaiian Telcom Services Company, Inc. It identifies the Hawaii Island Fiber Network, Hawaii Inter-Island Cable System, and SEA-US submarine cable system, and acknowledges a 2012 Letter of Assurances to the Department of Homeland Security and Department of Justice and a 2017 National Security Agreement with DHS.

**Who it affects** The commitments concern Cincinnati Bell, Hawaiian Telcom’s corporate entities and licensees, DHS, DOJ, and the other parties associated with the 2017 agreement and cable systems. The arrangements address national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety concerns tied to the proposed transaction and the affected communications infrastructure.

**Why it matters** The letter preserves continuity of Team Telecom mitigation during a change in ownership. Cincinnati Bell recognizes that DHS and DOJ may evaluate the existing documents and commits to negotiate in good faith and on a reasonably expedited basis to update or modify them if concerns arise.

**Key dates and numbers**
- 2012-12-07: Hawaiian Telcom, Inc. provided the LOA concerning HIFN and HICS.
- 2017-01-11: Hawaiian Telcom Services Company, Inc. and other parties entered the NSA with DHS.
- SCL-T/C-20170811-00019 and SCL-T/C-20170811-00020: FCC transfer-of-control file numbers cited in the letter.
- SCL-LIC-19921015-00008: File number for the Hawaii Inter-Island Cable System.
