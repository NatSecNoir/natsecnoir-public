The U.S. Department of Justice, with the concurrence of the Department of Homeland Security, advised the FCC that it had no objection to approving the referenced Zayo-related applications if approval was conditioned on compliance with the January 21, 2020 Letter of Agreement (LOA).

**What it does** This petition asks the Commission to adopt the LOA’s commitments and undertakings as conditions on the authorizations and licenses involved in the applicants’ transfers of control and petition for declaratory ruling. The Agencies stated that the additional commitments would help USDOJ enforce the law, protect national security, and preserve public safety. The filing relies on Section 1.41 of the FCC’s rules and identifies the relevant Communications Act and Cable Landing License Act authorities.

**Who it affects** The conditions would apply to Zayo Group Holdings, Inc., Zayo Group, LLC, Electric Light Wave LLC, and Allstream Business US, LLC, collectively referred to in the filing as Zayo. The proceedings also name Front Range Topco, Inc. and concern multiple international, submarine-cable, wireless, and related authorizations. The FCC would impose the conditions, while DOJ and DHS supplied the national-security and law-enforcement review.

**Why it matters** The filing illustrates Team Telecom’s role in FCC authorization proceedings: executive-branch agencies supported approval while requiring compliance with negotiated mitigation commitments. It also shows that national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety concerns were incorporated into the FCC’s public-interest review through enforceable conditions.

**Key dates and numbers**
- LOA dated January 21, 2020; petition filed January 22, 2020.
- WC Docket No. 19-166 and associated ITC-T/C, SCL-T/C, ISP-PDR, and ULS proceeding numbers are listed on the filing’s caption.
- The petition cites Section 1.41 of the FCC rules, 47 C.F.R. § 1.41.
