The House Select Committee on the Strategic Competition Between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party requests information and records from four venture-capital firms concerning their investments in PRC-based artificial-intelligence, semiconductor, and quantum-computing companies.

**What it does** The July 18, 2023 letters to GGV Capital, GSR Ventures, Qualcomm Ventures, and Walden International seek investment lists, Chinese and English company names, investment amounts and dates, management advice, control or influence rights, due-diligence factors, compliance policies, and communications with limited partners. They also request information about PRC state-linked funding, government or CCP influence, and documents concerning the investments. Each recipient is asked to preserve relevant hard-copy and electronic records.

**Who it affects** The requests directly affect the four named investment firms and their affiliated investment vehicles, personnel, portfolio companies, and limited partners. The letters identify examples including Megvii, SenseTime, Horizon Robotics, Zongmu, Gantu AI, Intellifusion, Biren Technology, SMIC, Jingneng Microelectronics, and Qingchun Semiconductor, while asking each firm to provide a complete response rather than limiting the inquiry to those examples.

**Why it matters** The Committee frames U.S. investment in PRC technology companies as potentially contributing to military-civil fusion, military modernization, human-rights abuses, and technology transfer. It specifically asks how the firms would respond if a portfolio company were placed on a U.S. sanctions or trade-restriction list, such as the Commerce Department’s Entity List.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letters dated July 18, 2023; the catalogue record date is July 19, 2023.
- Responses requested by August 1, 2023.
- GGV is described as having made 43 PRC AI investment transactions from 2015 to 2021; GSR is identified with at least 33; Qualcomm Ventures with at least 13.
- The letters invoke H. Res. 11 and request responses to five numbered categories of questions.
