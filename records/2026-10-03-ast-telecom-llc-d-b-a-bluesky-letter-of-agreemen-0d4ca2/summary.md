Amalgamated Telecom Holdings Limited (ATH) agrees to national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety mitigation measures concerning the proposed transfer of control of the American Samoa-Hawaii Cable System from Amper S.A. to ATH, enabling DHS to notify the FCC that it has no objection to the pending application.

**What it does** This Letter of Assurances requires ATH to designate U.S.-based security points of contact; provide DHS with network, operations, equipment, vendor, and restoration information; maintain the ability to interrupt U.S.-bound and U.S.-originating traffic; and obtain DHS review or non-objection for specified equipment and vendor changes. ATH must implement cybersecurity and physical-security measures, screen personnel with logical access to domestic communications infrastructure, report incidents and breaches, train relevant personnel, notify DHS of material service or ownership changes, and submit annual compliance reports. DHS may conduct reasonable on-site reviews and consult with ATH about submitted materials and concerns.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind ATH and its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates, and cover the U.S. portion of the submarine cable system, its network operations centers, principal equipment, vendors, contractors, subcontractors, and screened personnel. They support the FCC proceeding on transfer of the cable landing license from Amper to ATH.

**Why it matters** The agreement makes continued FCC authorization contingent on operational visibility, supply-chain and access controls, incident reporting, and government oversight of a U.S.-connected submarine cable. If ATH breaches the commitments, DHS may ask the FCC to modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify relevant authorization.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated 7 May 2018; FCC File No. SCL-T/C-20161220-00026.
- Initial security contacts and several policies are due within 60 business days of execution.
- Principal equipment information is due within 30 business days; DHS generally has 30 business days to respond.
- DHS requests for cable-system information must be answered within 15 business days; many notices require 10 or 30 business days.
- Foreign ownership or control changes above 10 percent require notice within 10 business days.
