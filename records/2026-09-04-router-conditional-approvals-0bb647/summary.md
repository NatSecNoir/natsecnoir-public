PSHSB announces that the Department of War’s conditional approval of specified eero and Amazon Leo routers requires their exclusion from the FCC Covered List until October 31, 2027.

**What it does** This Public Notice explains the Covered List treatment of foreign-produced routers and records a DoW Conditional Approval for eero LLC’s eero, eero Pro, eero Max, eero PoE, eero Outdoor, eero Signal, and Amazon Leo routers. PSHSB determines that the approval is “a specific determination” that the devices do not pose risks to U.S. national security, and updates the Covered List and related conditional-approval materials accordingly. Appendices A and B provide the updated Covered List and conditional-approval tables.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects eero LLC and the identified router products, as well as equipment authorization applicants, testing laboratories, and telecommunications certification bodies that rely on the Covered List. It also informs manufacturers of foreign-produced routers that they may seek evaluation by DoW or DHS for a Conditional Approval.

**Why it matters** Foreign-produced routers were added to the Covered List on March 23, 2026, subject to an exception for routers receiving DoW or DHS approval. This notice illustrates how an executive-branch national-security determination changes the scope of the FCC restriction and confirms PSHSB’s role in maintaining and updating the list.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released April 22, 2026; DA 26-390.
- Covered List proceedings: WC Docket No. 18-89, ET Docket No. 21-232, and EA Docket No. 21-233.
- Router category added to the Covered List March 23, 2026.
- eero/Amazon Leo Conditional Approval runs through October 31, 2027.
