The House Select Committee on the CCP and Senator Rick Scott urged Commerce to investigate and re-add the Chinese Ministry of Public Security’s Institute of Forensic Science (IFS) to the Entity List.

**What it does** The August 14, 2025 letter to Commerce Under Secretary for Industry and Security Jeffrey Kessler thanks the administration for national-security efforts and asks the Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) to act through the End User Review Committee. The requested designation would require BIS licensing for most exports of controlled items to the IFS. The letter also points to the proposed *Confronting CCP Human Rights Abusers Act*, which would place the re-designation in law.

**Who it affects** The request concerns the IFS, an entity of China’s Ministry of Public Security, and U.S. exporters of controlled technology and expertise. It also addresses Commerce officials responsible for Entity List decisions and the legislators seeking statutory action.

**Why it matters** The authors argue that the IFS supports surveillance, biometric data harvesting, forensic tracking, internment, forced labor, and other abuses targeting Uyghurs and other minorities in the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region. They contend that removing the IFS from the Entity List during the Biden administration weakened restrictions on technology transfers.

**Key dates and numbers**
- August 14, 2025: letter date.
- 2020: prior IFS Entity List designation referenced in the letter.
