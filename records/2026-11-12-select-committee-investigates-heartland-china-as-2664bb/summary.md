The House Select Committee on the CCP requests records and assurances from the United States Heartland China Association concerning possible undisclosed foreign influence and alleged ties to Chinese Communist Party United Front organizations.

**What it does** The letter asks Heartland China to provide verifiable assurances that it is not operating as an unregistered agent of the CCP or the People’s Republic of China. It requests four categories of material by May 7, 2026: programs and participants from 2021–2025; contacts with U.S. officials; agreements with Chinese partners; and records sufficient to show foreign funding. The committee also directs the organization to preserve relevant hard-copy and electronic records and calls on it to end relationships with CCP United Front actors.

**Who it affects** The principal recipient is Heartland China and its chairman and president, Robert Lee Holden Jr. The letter focuses on the association’s reported relationships with CPAFFC, CUSEF, CAAIE, CCIEE, and United Chinese Americans, as well as programs involving U.S. mayors, lawmakers, and other officials.

**Why it matters** The letter frames civic, educational, agricultural, business, and delegation activities as potential channels for foreign malign influence and raises possible Foreign Agents Registration Act implications. It illustrates congressional scrutiny of subnational engagement with PRC-linked entities and seeks information relevant to transparency and counterintelligence concerns.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter dated April 16, 2026; catalogue date: April 20, 2026.
- Requested response deadline: May 7, 2026.
- Requested records generally cover January 1, 2021, through December 31, 2025.
- The letter cites 50 U.S.C. § 3059 and 22 U.S.C. § 611.
