The House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party requests written information and a further classified briefing from FBI Director Christopher Wray about the FBI’s response to PRC transnational repression and illegal overseas police stations in the United States.

**What it does** This April 26, 2023 letter follows a February 24 request and a March 30 classified briefing that the Committee says did not answer its questions. It asks the FBI to respond to seven earlier questions concerning PRC police outposts, including the FBI’s awareness of them, investigative and closure efforts, their possible intelligence-gathering and targeting roles, protection of U.S. residents and citizens, and connections to Operation Fox Hunt or Operation Skynet. It adds five questions about FBI strategy, field-office guidance, pre-September 2022 investigations, interagency coordination, and the opening dates of relevant cases.

**Who it affects** The request concerns the FBI, U.S. law-enforcement coordination, PRC law-enforcement operations, and people targeted by transnational repression, which the letter says primarily includes Chinese Americans. It specifically references the April 17 arrests of Lu Jianwang and Chen Jinping in connection with an alleged illegal PRC police station in lower Manhattan.

**Why it matters** The Committee argues that delayed awareness or action could leave secret police facilities and repression schemes unchecked in the United States. It seeks information to assess the FBI’s capacity to detect, investigate, and counter these activities and to improve protection of affected communities.

**Key dates and numbers**
- February 24, 2023: prior Committee letter requesting a classified briefing.
- March 30, 2023: FBI classified briefing referenced in the letter.
- April 17, 2023: arrests announced by the Department of Justice.
- May 10, 2023: deadline requested for written responses and documents.
- May 31, 2023: deadline requested for a follow-up classified briefing.
- Seven earlier questions and five additional questions are listed.
