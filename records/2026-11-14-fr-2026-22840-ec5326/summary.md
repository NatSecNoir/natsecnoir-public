The Department of Defense removes WuXi AppTec from the unclassified Section 1260H list of Chinese military companies operating in the United States, without prejudice to a future relisting on additional or supplementary grounds.

**What it does** This notice implements the Deputy Secretary of Defense’s decision to delist WuXi AppTec Co., Ltd. (WuXi AppTec) from the most recent Section 1260H list. The notice states that the removal is required in compliance with an August 7, 2026, order of the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia granting WuXi AppTec’s motion for a preliminary injunction. That order enjoins the Department from enforcing, implementing, or otherwise giving effect to the designation. The notice also explains that a Mandarin version of the delisting notice is available in the docket’s supporting materials.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects WuXi AppTec and the Department of Defense’s administration of the Section 1260H list. It removes the company from the list published most recently on June 10, 2026, while preserving the Department’s stated ability to relist it on other or additional grounds.

**Why it matters** Section 1260H requires the Secretary of Defense to identify and publish the list annually through December 31, 2030, and to make additions or deletions at least annually. This notice illustrates how federal court relief can affect a company’s status on that national-security list without necessarily foreclosing a later designation on a different basis.

**Key dates and numbers**
- August 7, 2026: District court issued the preliminary-injunction order.
- June 10, 2026: Date of the most recent Section 1260H list referenced by the notice.
- November 5, 2026: Notice dated.
- November 10, 2026: Federal Register publication date.
- Docket ID: DOD–2026–OS–2410.
