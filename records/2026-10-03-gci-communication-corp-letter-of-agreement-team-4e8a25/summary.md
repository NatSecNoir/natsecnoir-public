GCI Communication Corp. commits to a set of national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety safeguards for the AU-Aleutian submarine cable system so that DHS can support the FCC’s consideration of GCI’s authorization application.

**What it does** This Letter of Assurances requires GCI to designate a U.S.-based security point of contact; provide DHS with network, operations, equipment, vendor, and restoration information; and give advance notice of material equipment, vendor, testing, service, and operational changes. It also requires cybersecurity, physical-security, personnel-screening, incident-reporting, employee-instruction, annual-reporting, and DHS consultation and visitation measures. DHS may object to proposed equipment, vendors, and testing, with deemed consent if it does not object within specified periods. A breach may lead DHS to ask the FCC to modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify a relevant authorization.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind GCI Communication Corp. and its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates, and cover personnel, contractors, vendors, and subcontractors involved with the AU-Aleutian Cable System. DHS receives review, information, consultation, and site-visit rights; the FCC is the licensing authority whose authorization may be conditioned on compliance.

**Why it matters** The agreement illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for U.S. communications infrastructure, emphasizing government visibility into system architecture and supply-chain changes, controlled access, resilience, and prompt reporting of unauthorized access or breaches.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed and dated June 3, 2019.
- Security point of contact: within 30 business days of execution.
- System information, equipment lists, and security policies: generally within 60 business days.
- Advance notice periods include 15 business days for material equipment modifications and 30 business days for vendor, testing, and service changes.
- Incidents or breaches must be reported in writing within 10 business days of discovery; annual compliance reports are due on the LOA anniversary.
