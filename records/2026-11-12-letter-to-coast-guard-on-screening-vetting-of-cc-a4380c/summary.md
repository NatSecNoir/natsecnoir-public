The letter requests that the U.S. Coast Guard strengthen screening, vetting, monitoring, and interagency coordination concerning COSCO SHIPPING vessels, owners, operators, and foreign mariners at U.S. ports.

**What it does** The signatories identify China COSCO SHIPPING Corporation Limited, COSCO SHIPPING (North America) Inc., and Cosco SHIPPING Finance Co., Ltd. as entities recently added to the Department of Defense’s Chinese Military Companies list under Section 1260H. They ask the Coast Guard to describe its protocols for reviewing Advance Notice of Arrival data, biographic information, vessel and ownership records, classified and unclassified datasets, automation, third-party systems, vessel monitoring, threat-detection tools, and coordination with federal agencies. The letter also requests risk assessments specific to COSCO SHIPPING operations and a classified staff briefing.

**Who it affects** The request is directed to Acting Commandant Admiral Kevin E. Lunday and concerns Coast Guard personnel, foreign mariners, foreign-flagged vessels, shipping companies, terminal operators, and U.S. ports and Captain of the Port Zones. It also implicates coordination with DHS, DoD, the FBI, and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence.

**Why it matters** The committees frame COSCO SHIPPING’s state ownership, alleged PLA connections, control of container traffic, and presence near critical infrastructure as potential sources of espionage, cyber intrusion, sabotage, and supply-chain disruption. The letter seeks consistent, intelligence-informed safeguards across the U.S. Marine Transportation System without prescribing a specific Coast Guard action.

**Key dates and numbers**
- January 22, 2025: letter date.
- February 5, 2025, by 5:00 p.m.: requested deadline for a written response.
- February 12, 2025: requested deadline for a classified staff briefing.
- Nine numbered questions address screening, data, automation, monitoring, tools, coordination, and COSCO-specific assessments.
- Section 1260H of the National Defense Authorization Act of 2021 is cited as the authority for the designation.
