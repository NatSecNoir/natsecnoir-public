DOCOMO Pacific, Inc. commits to security, reporting, access-control, and ownership-transparency measures so DHS can support the FCC’s authorization of the Atisa submarine cable system.

**What it does** This Letter of Assurances establishes obligations tied to DOCOMO’s proposed private, non-common-carrier fiber-optic system linking Guam with Saipan, Rota, and Tinian. DOCOMO must designate U.S.-citizen security points of contact; provide DHS with an updated list of principal equipment, manufacturers, vendors, contractors, and subcontractors; give advance notice of material equipment changes, vendor changes, and significant operational changes; submit annual compliance reports; and notify DHS of specified changes in foreign ownership or control. It also requires practicable measures against unauthorized access, prompt reporting of security incidents, consultation with DHS, and access for DHS on-site reviews. The commitments bind successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates, and breach may lead DHS to request FCC action on the relevant authorization.

**Who it affects** The primary affected party is DOCOMO Pacific and its Atisa Cable System operations, including domestic communications infrastructure, wet infrastructure, network operations centers, and associated suppliers. DHS receives review, consultation, reporting, and visitation rights; the FCC is the licensing authority whose authorization may be conditioned on compliance.

**Why it matters** The letter shows Team Telecom mitigation centered on continuing visibility into submarine-cable equipment, suppliers, network changes, ownership, and security incidents. It links compliance failures to potential modification, revocation, cancellation, termination, or nullification of an FCC authorization, while preserving DHS involvement after licensing.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed/document date: February 15, 2017.
- Initial security POCs and principal-equipment list: within 60 business days of execution.
- Notice periods: 10 business days for material equipment changes and qualifying ownership/control events; 30 business days for vendor or significant service/operations changes.
- Foreign ownership/control threshold: above 10 percent, or actual or likely foreign control.
