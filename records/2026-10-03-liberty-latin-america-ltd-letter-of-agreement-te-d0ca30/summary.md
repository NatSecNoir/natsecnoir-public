Liberty Latin America Ltd. (LLA) commits to national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety mitigation measures supporting U.S. government non-objection to its acquisition of AT&T Mobility Puerto Rico and AT&T Mobility USVI assets and related FCC authorizations.

**What it does** This Letter of Agreement requires LLA to maintain U.S.-based law-enforcement and security contacts, support lawful interception and CALEA compliance, preserve and produce U.S. Records in response to lawful U.S. process, and restrict foreign access to domestic communications, infrastructure, and customer data. It also establishes controls over personnel screening, principal equipment, outsourced or offshore providers, network operations centers, data-storage locations, foreign persons with access, security plans, incident reporting, site visits, and annual compliance reports. LLA must provide advance notice of specified changes and obtain USG Parties’ objection or non-objection in several areas.

**Who it affects** The obligations bind LLA and cover its U.S. telecommunications operations, personnel, vendors, managed network service providers, contractors, and affiliates involved in the transaction. AT&T affiliates may provide transition services, but that arrangement does not relieve LLA of compliance.

**Why it matters** The agreement ties approval of the cross-border license transfer and foreign-investment request to continuing safeguards for U.S. communications, records, and lawful government access. A breach or unresolved national-security concern may lead the FCC to modify, condition, revoke, cancel, or nullify relevant authorizations.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed July 1, 2020.
- LLA generally must report security incidents within 72 hours; third-party providers must report data breaches or losses within 48 hours.
- LLA must respond to lawful U.S. process for U.S. Records within five days, absent an extension.
- The annual compliance report is due one year after the LOA date and annually thereafter.
- The agreement concerns FCC applications under Sections 214 and 310(b)(4), including WC Docket No. 19-384.
