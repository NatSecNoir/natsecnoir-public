The Letter of Agreement conditions the proposed transfer of OneWeb-related FCC authorizations to Eutelsat on extensive national-security and law-enforcement safeguards for U.S. communications, systems, and records.

**What it does** This Team Telecom agreement requires OneWeb, OneWeb Technologies, Eutelsat, Eutelsat America Corp., and related entities to maintain U.S. law-enforcement points of contact and security officers; comply with lawful U.S. process; restrict foreign access to U.S. Records and domestic communications infrastructure; and submit cybersecurity, system-security, equipment, outsourcing, and network-location information for review. It also requires incident reporting, personnel screening, third-party testing and audits, annual compliance reports, and site visits. The parties must give advance notice of material changes in ownership, services, facilities, equipment, and providers.

**Who it affects** The commitments apply principally to the OneWeb and Eutelsat entities involved in the acquisition and to their subsidiaries, vendors, contractors, outsourced or offshore providers, and proxy-related operations. DOJ, the FBI, DoD, and other U.S. Government parties receive review, objection, oversight, and enforcement roles.

**Why it matters** The LOA makes national-security and law-enforcement protections an operational condition of satellite-system ownership and U.S. market access. Breach or unresolved risk can support a recommendation that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, or terminate relevant authorizations.
