The House Select Committee on the CCP urges President Donald J. Trump to use his upcoming summit with Xi Jinping and subsequent executive action to intensify U.S. national-security measures against the People’s Republic of China.

**What it does** The September 18, 2026 letter commends the administration’s response to PRC industrial overcapacity and alleged security risks from adversary-produced information-technology equipment, power equipment, and robotics. It recommends additional import controls on connected technologies and smart displays; consequences for lethal support to U.S. adversaries and hostage diplomacy; heightened scrutiny of PRC-linked investment and automobile assembly; continued arms sales and security cooperation with Taiwan and U.S. allies; stronger controls on advanced AI products, cloud-service loopholes, and semiconductor-manufacturing technology; routine Commerce Department Entity List designations; implementation of the COINS and BIOSECURE Acts; and further action under Executive Order 13873.

**Who it affects** The recommendations are directed to President Trump, his departments and agencies, Congress, and U.S. officials managing trade, investment, export controls, arms sales, and China policy. They would affect PRC entities, Chinese AI laboratories, state-subsidized automakers, semiconductor actors, and suppliers of connected and industrial technology.

**Why it matters** The letter frames China as the United States’ “foremost foreign adversary” and argues that enforcement, import restrictions, investment screening, and technology controls should continue even after bilateral engagement, rather than treating a summit as a reason to pause policy action.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated September 18, 2026; sent before President Trump’s anticipated summit with Xi Jinping.
- References the tenth anniversary of Trump’s first electoral victory and the United States’ 250-year horizon.
- Cites Executive Order 13873 and pending COINS and BIOSECURE Act implementation.
- Signed by John Moolenaar, Chairman of the House Select Committee on China.
