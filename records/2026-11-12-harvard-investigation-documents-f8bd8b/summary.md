The documents record Harvard’s response to a House Select Committee inquiry into disruptions and an alleged physical removal of a protester at the April 20, 2024 China Conference, together with related police and student-conduct materials.

**What it does** The collection includes Harvard’s July 31, 2024 response to the Committee’s July 1 letter, a public Harvard University Police Department log, a Massachusetts State Police submission, Administrative Board materials, an HGSE conduct referral and resolution, and excerpts from the 2023–2024 Harvard College and HGSE student handbooks. Harvard states that HUPD provided event security, found no partnership with Chinese government representatives or a private security agency, filed an assault-and-battery report, and later closed its investigation after the protester declined to pursue criminal charges. It also describes disciplinary action against three Harvard College students and a separate HGSE decision not to impose sanctions on the graduate student who removed a protester.

**Who it affects** The materials concern Harvard students, the protester, the HGSE student, HUPD, Harvard administrators, the Administrative Board, HGSE’s Committee on Rights and Responsibilities, and the Select Committee. Redactions and FERPA explanations limit disclosure of student identities, nationality, immigration status, and education records.

**Why it matters** The collection documents how campus security, criminal reporting, student discipline, free-expression principles, physical-violence rules, and educational-record privacy intersected in a politically sensitive event involving remarks by Ambassador Xie Feng. It also preserves Harvard’s account of its policies on student organizations and alleged foreign-government links.

**Key dates and numbers**
- April 20, 2024: China Conference incident; HUPD incident number 2024-0000874A.
- May 17, 2024: Administrative Board placed three students on disciplinary probation until May 20.
- July 1 and July 31, 2024: Committee inquiry and Harvard response.
- October 18, 2024: document date supplied in the catalogue record.
- FERPA authorities cited: 20 U.S.C. § 1232g and 34 C.F.R. §§ 99.3, 99.30, 99.31(b)(1).
