The House Select Committee on the Strategic Competition Between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party requests a Bureau of Industry and Security briefing on export-control restrictions applicable to PRC-national access to U.S. university supercomputers.

**What it does** The March 6, 2025 letter to Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick asks BIS to explain how export controls apply when university or academic supercomputers use export-controlled chips. It raises possible unlicensed deemed exports involving access, research results, and information about supercomputer design or operations, and asks for greater understanding of the associated national-security risks. The letter cites concerns about PRC entities using intermediaries or remote access to circumvent controls and about talent-recruitment programs transferring expertise.

**Who it affects** The request concerns BIS, U.S. universities operating advanced computing resources, PRC nationals and entities seeking access, and researchers or students using those systems. It highlights the University of Florida’s HiPerGator AI, which uses Nvidia A100 GPUs, and its planned HiPerGator AI 2.0, which is described as using Nvidia Blackwell GPUs. The letter also discusses universities hosting students connected to PRC institutions on the BIS Entity List or identified as medium or high risk for defense research.

**Why it matters** The committee frames university supercomputing access as both an important academic resource and a potential pathway for advanced artificial-intelligence capabilities, technical knowledge, or research to reach PRC military and economic programs. It argues that guardrails must account for the openness of academic institutions without undermining legitimate research.

**Key dates and numbers**
- March 6, 2025: date shown on the letter to Secretary Lutnick; the catalogue date is March 10, 2025.
- 22 of the world’s 500 most powerful computers are hosted by U.S. universities, according to the letter.
- HiPerGator AI is reported at 17,200 teraFLOPS (Rmax).
- The letter invokes H. Res. 5, section 4(a), as the committee’s investigative authority.
