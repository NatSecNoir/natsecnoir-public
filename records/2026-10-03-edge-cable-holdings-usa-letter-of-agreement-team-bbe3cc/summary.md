Telxius Cable USA commits to a comprehensive set of national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety safeguards as a condition supporting FCC authorization for the MAREA private submarine cable system linking the United States and Spain.

**What it does** This Letter of Assurances requires Telxius to designate U.S.-citizen security points of contact; provide network, equipment, vendor, and restoration information; maintain the ability to interrupt U.S.-bound traffic; and provide advance notice of specified equipment, vendor, service, operational, and control changes. It also requires logical and physical security measures, personnel screening, incident reporting, employee instruction, annual compliance reports, and cooperation with government consultations, site visits, and possible third-party audits. Breach may prompt the U.S. government parties to request that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify a relevant authorization.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind Telxius Cable USA and its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates, and concern the MAREA applicants—Edge Cable Holdings USA, Microsoft Infrastructure Group, and Telefónica entities—as well as DHS, DoD, and the FCC.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for a U.S.-connected submarine cable, focusing on operational control, access to domestic communications infrastructure, supply-chain visibility, foreign ownership changes, resilience, and government verification of compliance.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated April 28, 2017; tied to FCC application SCL-LIC-20160525-00012.
- Many initial submissions and policies are due within 60 business days of execution.
- Vendor or significant operational changes generally require 30 business days’ notice; material equipment modifications generally require 10 business days’ notice.
- Foreign ownership or control changes above 10 percent require notice within 10 business days.
- Annual reports are due on each anniversary of execution; a repeat audit is not required within 18 months of the prior audit’s conclusion.
