This Letter of Assurances (LOA) updates Telxius Cable USA, Inc.’s national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety commitments for the SAm-1 submarine cable and is intended to support DHS’s request that the FCC condition Telxius’s cable-landing authorization on compliance.

**What it does** The LOA terminates and replaces the commitments in Telxius’s October 2, 2017 LOA. It requires a U.S.-based security point of contact, detailed cable-system and principal-equipment disclosures, advance notice of equipment, vendor, testing, service, and operational changes, and the capability to interrupt U.S. traffic. It also requires logical and physical security controls, personnel screening, incident reporting, annual compliance reports, possible third-party audits, and DHS consultation and site visits. DHS may object to equipment or vendors and may seek FCC action if Telxius breaches the commitments.

**Who it affects** The obligations bind Telxius and extend to its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates. They cover SAm-1’s U.S. and territorial infrastructure, network operations centers, equipment, vendors, contractors, subcontractors, and personnel with relevant access. DHS administers the commitments, while DoD is identified with DHS as a USG Party and the FCC is the licensing authority.

**Why it matters** The LOA provides a Team Telecom mitigation framework for a U.S.-connected submarine cable, giving DHS continuing visibility and approval or objection rights over sensitive infrastructure, personnel, access, and ownership changes. It also links compliance to the FCC’s licensing remedies.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed December 4, 2019; replaces the October 2, 2017 LOA.
- Notices generally require 15, 30, or 60 business days, depending on the matter.
- Security incidents or breaches must be reported within 48 hours or, for specified conduct, 10 business days of discovery.
- Foreign ownership or control above 10% triggers notice within 10 business days.
