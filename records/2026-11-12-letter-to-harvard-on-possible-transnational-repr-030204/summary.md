The House Select Committee on the CCP asks Harvard University to brief the committee and answer detailed questions about the forcible removal and subsequent intimidation of student protesters at an April 20, 2024 Harvard Kennedy School event featuring Chinese Ambassador Xie Feng, including whether the conduct may reflect transnational repression.

**What it does**
The July 1, 2024 letter requests a briefing and a written response by July 26, 2024. It asks about event security, possible coordination with Chinese government representatives or private agencies, faculty involvement, reporting to law enforcement, administrative and disciplinary investigations, and the identity and institutional affiliations of the person who removed Cosette Wu. It also asks about the relationship between the Kennedy School’s Greater China Society and Harvard’s Chinese Students and Scholars Association (CSSA), possible Chinese government funding or coordination, use of federal funds, and Harvard’s protections for students’ civil liberties.

**Who it affects**
The immediate recipient is Harvard President Alan Garber, with questions directed to Harvard University and the Harvard Kennedy School. The letter concerns Cosette Wu, Tsering Yangchen, other student protesters, the unidentified or disputedly identified student who removed Wu, the Greater China Society, and the CSSA. It also addresses possible involvement by Chinese diplomatic missions and government entities.

**Why it matters**
The committee frames the incident as raising serious questions about foreign-government-backed harassment and intimidation on U.S. university campuses. It links the inquiry to broader concerns about transnational repression, academic freedom, student safety, and whether universities adequately resist efforts to silence critics of the Chinese government.

**Key dates and numbers**
- April 20, 2024: Event and removal of Wu.
- July 1, 2024: Letter date.
- July 26, 2024: Requested response deadline.
- 13: Numbered questions posed to Harvard.
- H. Res. 11: Authority cited by the committee.
