The FCC proposes to strengthen the integrity of its equipment-authorization program by restricting recognition and use of telecommunications certification bodies (TCBs) and test labs tied through ownership or control to Covered List entities, while seeking comment on additional national-security eligibility restrictions and disclosure requirements.

**What it does** The notice proposes to bar FCC recognition or reliance on any TCB or test lab in which a Covered List entity has direct or indirect ownership or control, including for Supplier’s Declaration of Conformity proceedings. It directs the Office of Engineering and Technology to suspend recognition, pending the proceeding, where sufficient evidence indicates such ownership or control, with an opportunity to provide documentation and show cause. The FCC also seeks comment on using other government lists, including the DoD 1260H List and Commerce’s Entity List, and proposes reporting of direct or indirect equity or voting interests of 5% or more, with a 10% ownership-or-control threshold for the Covered List prohibition.

**Who it affects** FCC-recognized TCBs, test labs, accreditation bodies, equipment manufacturers and applicants, and entities whose certification or testing work supports equipment authorization would face possible eligibility, transparency, and compliance changes.

**Why it matters** The proposal extends supply-chain safeguards beyond equipment manufacturers to the private entities that test and certify radio-frequency devices and handle sensitive technical information, reducing opportunities for prohibited equipment or foreign-influenced actors to undermine authorization controls.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Adopted May 23, 2024; released May 24, 2024.
- Comments: 60 days after Federal Register publication; replies: 90 days after publication.
- Proposed reporting threshold: 5%; proposed Covered List ownership/control threshold: 10%.
- OET-directed interim suspension certification period: 30 days.
- As of March 2024, the FCC recognized 40 TCBs and listed 639 test labs.
