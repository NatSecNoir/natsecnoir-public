The October 31, 2019 Letter of Assurances updates and supersedes Hawaiki’s 2017 commitments and establishes mitigation obligations for the Hawaiki Cable System as a condition of its FCC cable landing license and transfer-of-control application.

**What it does** This LOA requires Hawaiki to maintain a U.S.-based, cleared Security Point of Contact; provide detailed cable, network, vendor, contractor, and equipment information; and obtain USG review of principal equipment, vendor changes, and testing of new equipment. It requires the ability to interrupt U.S. traffic, logical and physical security controls, personnel screening, incident reporting within 48 hours, advance notice of operational or service changes, and notice of certain foreign ownership or control changes. Hawaiki must also submit annual compliance reports and policies, support permitted third-party audits and site visits, and negotiate with the USG Parties over identified concerns.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind Hawaiki and extend to its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates. They directly govern the Hawaiki Cable System, its U.S. domestic communications infrastructure, network operations centers, principal equipment, vendors, contractors, subcontractors, and personnel with relevant access. DHS and DoD receive oversight, while the FCC may condition the relevant authorizations on compliance.

**Why it matters** The agreement illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for a submarine cable connecting the United States, Australia, and New Zealand. It gives national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety authorities continuing visibility into system architecture, equipment, ownership, security, and operations, with potential FCC licensing consequences for breach.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated October 31, 2019; supersedes the December 15, 2017 LOA.
- Hawaiki must nominate a Security POC within 30 business days of execution.
- Most policies and cable information are due within 60 business days; incident reports are due within 48 hours.
- Foreign ownership or control changes above 10% require notice within 10 business days.
