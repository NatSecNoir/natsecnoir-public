The House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party and the House Committee on Education and Workforce request additional records from Harvard concerning alleged formal relationships with CCP and PRC-government entities, while preserving earlier information demands.

**What it does** The July 30, 2025 letter asks Harvard to produce, for the period beginning January 1, 2015, all documents concerning engagements, partnerships, or other collaborations between Harvard and entities subordinate to, controlled by, or directed by the PRC government or CCP. It also requests a native Microsoft Excel list of all money and non-monetary benefits Harvard received from such entities, including government bodies, state-owned enterprises, research institutions, universities, military and paramilitary organizations, and their affiliates or representatives. The letter identifies whistleblower-reported ties involving the CCP Organization Department and a formal Harvard Kennedy School partnership with the Chinese Executive Leadership Academy Pudong (CELAP).

**Who it affects** The requests are directed to Harvard University and cover all Harvard schools. The committees identify CCP cadres receiving training at Harvard and seek information about institutional relationships and benefits involving Chinese government or Party bodies.

**Why it matters** The letter frames Harvard’s alleged training and institutional ties as relevant to congressional oversight of the CCP’s economic, technological, security, and ideological threats. It also clarifies that the committees’ May 19, 2025 document and testimony requests remain valid and active.

**Key dates and numbers**
- July 30, 2025: letter date; response requested by August 7, 2025.
- January 1, 2015: beginning of the principal review period.
- Two additional document requests: collaboration records and a benefits ledger.
- May 19, 2025: earlier requests expressly preserved.
