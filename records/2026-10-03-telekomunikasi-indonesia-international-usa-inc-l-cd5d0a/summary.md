The Agreement permits the Department of Justice to notify the FCC that it does not object to Telkom USA’s Section 214 application, provided the FCC conditions approval on compliance with the Agreement’s national-security and law-enforcement safeguards.

**What it does** The Agreement establishes operational, technical, data-handling, personnel, reporting, audit, and enforcement requirements for Telkom USA and its parent and affiliate companies. It requires U.S.-based routing, principal equipment, network operations centers, and storage of specified domestic communications, transactional and subscriber data, while requiring capability to comply with lawful U.S. process. It also requires a DOJ-approved Implementation Plan, a U.S.-based law-enforcement point of contact, a security officer, screening of personnel, controls on outsourcing and offshoring, notice and review of foreign infrastructure or storage, and DOJ access for inspections and audits.

**Who it affects** The obligations bind PT Telekomunikasi Indonesia Tbk. (PT Telkom), PT Telekomunikasi Indonesia International (Telin), and Telekomunikasi Indonesia International (USA), Inc. (Telkom USA), including relevant successors, controlled entities, contractors, and service providers. The arrangement supports Telkom USA’s proposed wholesale transport and enterprise telecommunications services between the United States and Indonesia.

**Why it matters** This is a Team Telecom mitigation agreement linking FCC authorization to continuing DOJ oversight of foreign-owned telecommunications infrastructure, customer information, lawful surveillance support, and changes in ownership or operations. Breach may support judicial relief, civil or criminal sanctions, or FCC action concerning authorization.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Telkom USA filed the FCC application on September 19, 2014; the FCC file number is ITC-214-20140918-00265.
- The Implementation Plan is due within 150 days of the Effective Date and the network architecture description within 120 days.
- NOC and law-enforcement contact notices are due within 45 days; billing records must generally be retained for at least 18 months.
- Annual compliance reports are due on each anniversary of the Effective Date; third-party audits generally occur every two years.
