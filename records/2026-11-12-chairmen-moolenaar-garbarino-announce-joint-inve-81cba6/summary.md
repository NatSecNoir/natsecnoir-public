The House Committee on Homeland Security and the House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party opened a joint investigation into the national-security, data-security, and software-supply-chain risks of PRC-developed AI models used by Anysphere and Airbnb.

**What it does** The committees sent separate April 29, 2026 letters to Anysphere CEO Michael Truell and Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky. They request records about relationships with PRC-based AI entities, model selection and provenance, security testing, data pathways, inference and cloud providers, retention and processing terms, and customer or user data transmitted to PRC-origin models. The Anysphere letter focuses particularly on Cursor’s Composer 2, reportedly based on Moonshot AI’s Kimi K2.5, while the Airbnb letter examines reported use of Alibaba’s Qwen model. Both letters also request descriptions of measures to prevent PRC access, disclose model origin, assess model integrity, and address federal security and export-control requirements.

**Who it affects** The requests directly affect Anysphere and Airbnb, their subsidiaries, affiliates, model providers, infrastructure providers, intermediaries, enterprise customers, and users whose code, prompts, booking information, payment metadata, or other data may be processed by the relevant systems.

**Why it matters** The committees characterize PRC model adoption as a national-security issue involving alleged adversarial distillation, censorship, unsafe outputs, possible data exposure, and compromised software supply chains—not merely a cost or performance decision.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letters dated April 29, 2026; document production requested by May 13, 2026.
- In-person briefings requested by May 20, 2026.
- Cursor is described as having more than one million daily active users and more than 50,000 business customers.
- The letters identify DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, and MiniMax as collectively associated with more than 16 million exchanges through approximately 24,000 fraudulent accounts, according to cited disclosures.
