Telesat and its named subsidiaries commit to national-security and law-enforcement mitigation measures as a condition associated with FCC applications to transfer control of international Section 214, satellite space, and earth-station authorizations.

**What it does** This Letter of Agreement (LOA) replaces, except where otherwise stated, a 2007 mitigation letter and establishes Telesat’s obligations to the U.S. Department of Justice (USDOJ), including the FBI. It requires a U.S.-based law-enforcement point of contact and U.S.-citizen Security Officer; prompt production of U.S. Records in response to lawful U.S. process; compliance certifications under CALEA; controls on foreign access, disclosure, and handling of U.S. communications and records; cybersecurity and system-security plans; reporting of security incidents; and advance notice or review of principal equipment, outsourced or offshored services, network-operations-center changes, ownership changes, and service changes. Telesat must also submit annual compliance reports and permit USDOJ site visits and verification.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind Telesat Canada, Telesat Corporation, Infosat Able Holdings, Inc., Skynet Satellite Corporation, and Telesat Network Services, Inc., while giving USDOJ review, objection, and oversight roles. The requirements extend to relevant employees, foreign personnel, contractors, service providers, network facilities, equipment, and systems supporting U.S. communications.

**Why it matters** The LOA is a Team Telecom mitigation instrument linking continued FCC authorization to controls over lawful access, data sovereignty, foreign involvement, cybersecurity, and supply-chain risk. Breach or unresolved national-security concerns may support FCC action against relevant licenses or authorizations.

**Key dates and numbers**
- LOA date: July 15, 2021.
- LEPOC and Security Officer nominations are generally due within 15 days after FCC approval.
- Cybersecurity and System Security Plans, and principal-equipment information, are due within 90 days after FCC approval.
- Known or suspected security incidents and material LOA breaches must generally be reported within 48 hours.
- The first annual compliance report is due one year after FCC approval and annually thereafter.
