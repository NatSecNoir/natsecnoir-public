The President prohibits MineOne’s acquisition and requires divestment of a 12.06-acre Wyoming property near Francis E. Warren Air Force Base, together with removal of cryptocurrency-mining equipment and post-closing improvements.

**What it does** The order, issued under section 721 of the Defense Production Act, bars the purchasers and their affiliates from retaining any direct or indirect interest in the real estate. It requires divestment within 120 calendar days, equipment and improvements removal within 90 calendar days, and written certifications to CFIUS. It also restricts access, asset transfers, restructuring, and sales to third parties unless CFIUS is notified and has 10 business days to object. CFIUS may inspect premises and records, audit systems, interview personnel, verify compliance, and impose additional measures.

**Who it affects** The order applies to MineOne Partners, MineOne Cloud, MineOne Data, MineOne Wyoming, and identified affiliates including Terra Crypto and Terra Global entities. It also directs CFIUS, the Secretary of the Treasury, and the Attorney General in implementation and enforcement.

**Why it matters** The order treats foreign ownership near a strategic missile base, combined with foreign-sourced specialized equipment, as an unresolvable national-security risk that could not be addressed through a verifiable negotiated mitigation agreement.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Order date: May 13, 2024.
- Property: 12.06 acres at 635 Logistics Drive, Cheyenne, Wyoming, within 1 mile of Warren AFB.
- Divestment deadline: 120 calendar days; removal deadline: 90 calendar days.
- Access controls: measures required within 7 calendar days; proposed buyers receive a 10-business-day CFIUS review period.
