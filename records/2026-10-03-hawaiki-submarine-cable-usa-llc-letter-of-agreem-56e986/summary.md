Hawaiki Submarine Cable USA LLC and BW Digital Pte. Ltd. agree that the obligations in their March 23, 2022 mitigation agreement will apply in full to the Hawaiki Submarine Cable’s Tonga branch and landing station, supporting conditional approval of the related FCC license modification.

**What it does** The letter acknowledges to the Committee for the Assessment of Foreign Participation in the United States Telecommunications Services Sector that the existing 2022 Mitigation Agreement extends to the Tonga branch and landing station covered by the December 4, 2024 FCC modification application. It resets the timing for initial submissions for that branch to run from execution of this letter. After the parties’ authorized representatives execute it, the Committee is expected to notify the FCC that it has no objection to granting the application, provided continued compliance with the 2022 Mitigation Agreement is made a condition of the grant. The parties also certify, under 18 U.S.C. § 1001, the truth of their prior submissions to the reviewing agencies and adopt those statements as the basis for the letter.

**Who it affects** The agreement binds Hawaiki and BW Digital in connection with the Tonga branch, and informs the Committee’s recommendation and the FCC’s license-modification decision.

**Why it matters** It carries existing national-security mitigation obligations into a new submarine-cable branch and ties FCC authorization to continuing compliance.

**Key dates and numbers**
- March 23, 2022: underlying mitigation agreement.
- December 4, 2024: FCC modification application.
- September 17, 2025: letter date.
