Anthropic’s Amendment No. 3 to its Form S-1 registration statement describes a proposed initial public offering of Class A common stock, a concurrent private placement of Class N common stock, and extensive risks concerning advanced AI, compute infrastructure, governance, privacy, intellectual property, government regulation, and litigation.

**What it does** The filing registers 825,000,000 Class A shares, including 801,351,354 sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 sold by existing stockholders, with an estimated offering price of $70 to $78 per share and proposed Nasdaq symbol “ANT.” It also describes a conditional $26.0 billion private placement of Class N stock to Google, NVIDIA, Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft, and TSMC Partners. Anthropic explains its Delaware public benefit corporation structure, Long-Term Benefit Trust, Founder LLC, multiple share classes, and planned use of proceeds for compute, infrastructure, operations, personnel, and taxes.

**Who it affects** The disclosure addresses prospective investors, existing stockholders, employees, strategic partners, customers, government agencies, and counterparties supplying cloud services, chips, data centers, financing, and distribution. It also identifies the effects of AI-related safety, export-control, privacy, cybersecurity, copyright, patent, consumer-protection, antitrust, and government-contract risks.

**Why it matters** The filing presents Anthropic as a rapidly growing but highly capital-intensive frontier-AI company whose commercial strategy depends on access to compute and autonomous-agent deployment. It warns that model misalignment, misuse, regulatory action, litigation, supply constraints, and concentrated voting power could materially affect operations, public-sector access, investor rights, and the value of Class A stock.
