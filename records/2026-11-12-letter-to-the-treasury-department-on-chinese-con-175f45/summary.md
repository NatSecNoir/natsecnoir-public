The House Select Committee on the CCP urges the Treasury Secretary to take immediate CFIUS action regarding Grand Pharmaceutical Group Limited’s controlling investment in FastWave Medical Inc., a U.S. medical-device company developing laser-based intravascular lithotripsy technology.

**What it does** The letter argues that FastWave’s laser IVL system is “critical technology” under 31 C.F.R. § 800.215(b), because it is subject to multilateral export controls and has national-security relevance. It describes the laser as dual-use technology that could potentially be adapted for applications including extreme ultraviolet lithography in semiconductor manufacturing. The Committee alleges that Grand Pharma has blocked FastWave from raising capital, contributed to acute financial distress, and may be positioned to obtain or extract the company’s core technology. It asks Treasury to give the matter immediate attention through CFIUS.

**Who it affects** The requested review concerns Grand Pharma, FastWave, FastWave’s U.S. engineering and manufacturing workforce, and patients who may depend on its medical technology. The letter also identifies Grand Pharma’s strategic partnership with Chinese IVL competitor Zhenyi Medical and references the Committee’s concerns about Grand Pharma’s conduct in another North American medical-technology investment.

**Why it matters** The Committee frames the investment as a potential pathway for control of sensitive U.S. medical and laser technology to move to China, with implications for American competitiveness, semiconductor capabilities, jobs, and patient access. The allegations are presented as grounds for CFIUS scrutiny, not as a reported CFIUS disposition.

**Key dates and numbers**
- January 30, 2026: Letter date.
- 31 C.F.R. § 800.215(b): Provision cited in the letter’s critical-technology argument.
- March 17, 2025: Date cited for the Grand Pharma–Zhenyi Medical strategic-cooperation announcement.
- The letter refers to dozens of potentially affected U.S. engineering and manufacturing jobs.
