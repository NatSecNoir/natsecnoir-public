The House Select Committee on the CCP urges the UK ambassador to oppose plans for a new, expanded Chinese embassy at Royal Mint Court in London and to coordinate with the United States on securing Jimmy Lai’s unconditional release.

**What it does** This February 26, 2025 letter from Representatives Christopher Smith and John Moolenaar raises concerns about the proposed “super-embassy,” which the authors describe as a potentially counterproductive and unearned reward for the Chinese Communist government. It asks Ambassador Peter Mandelson to convey those concerns to Prime Minister Keir Starmer before Starmer’s upcoming meeting with President Donald J. Trump.

**Who it affects** The letter addresses UK officials and the proposed Chinese diplomatic facility, while discussing the People’s Republic of China, the Chinese Communist Party, Hong Kong political prisoners including UK citizen Jimmy Lai, and alleged Chinese transnational repression and influence operations. It also references concerns involving Uyghurs, Tibetans, Taiwan, forced labor, and Hong Kong Economic and Trade Offices.

**Why it matters** The committee frames embassy expansion as a national-security and human-rights concern, arguing that a prominent diplomatic foothold could embolden intimidation of dissidents and critics across the UK and Europe. It links the issue to alleged espionage, coercion, malign influence, forced labor, and threats to democratic values, and calls for U.S.-UK coordination on Lai’s detention.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter dated February 26, 2025.
- Proposed site: Royal Mint Court, London.
- The authors characterize the proposed facility as the largest Chinese embassy in Europe.
- Starmer’s planned meeting with Trump is identified as the immediate diplomatic context.
