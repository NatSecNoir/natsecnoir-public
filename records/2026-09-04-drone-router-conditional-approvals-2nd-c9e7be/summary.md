The FCC Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau updates the Covered List to exempt specified foreign-produced uncrewed aircraft systems (UAS), UAS critical components, and routers covered by Department of War Conditional Approvals.

**What it does** The Public Notice finds that the Department of War’s Conditional Approvals are “a specific determination” that the identified devices do not pose unacceptable risks to U.S. national security, and therefore removes those devices from the Covered List. The appendices provide the June 12, 2026 Covered List and the current Conditional Approvals, including approvals for ABZ Innovation Kft.’s L50 UAS and Arcadyan Technology Corporation routers, plus previously approved devices from other manufacturers. The Bureau also notes that FCC staff will provide guidance to TCBs, test labs, and equipment-authorization applicants.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects manufacturers and suppliers of foreign-produced UAS, UAS critical components, and routers, as well as entities seeking FCC equipment authorizations. It identifies exemptions for devices from ABZ Innovation, Arcadyan, Miri Technologies, Flock Safety, Netgear, Adtran, eero, Calix, Nokia, Sagemcom, Blueflite, and Innovation First, among others listed in Appendix B.

**Why it matters** The notice clarifies how Executive Branch national-security determinations and device-specific Conditional Approvals modify the scope of the FCC Covered List. Covered-list treatment remains category-based for foreign-produced UAS and routers, but approved devices are excluded for the stated approval periods.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released June 12, 2026; DA 26-585.
- ABZ L50 approval terminates December 31, 2026.
- Arcadyan router approval terminates December 12, 2027.
- Relevant proceedings: WC Docket No. 18-89; ET Docket No. 21-232; EA Docket No. 21-233.
