The House Select Committee on the CCP urges the National Science Foundation (NSF) to pause and review its SECURE initiative awards to the University of Washington (UW) and Texas A&M University (TAMU) over alleged research-security concerns involving collaborations with PRC military- and defense-linked entities.

**What it does** The March 10, 2026 letter from Chairman John Moolenaar to NSF Interim Director Brian Stone requests that NSF suspend current SECURE award activity, conduct a comprehensive review of TAMU, UW, and other participating institutions, and consider repurposing the SECURE center as a national research-security center. It asks NSF to assess compliance with NSPM-33, Section 117 of the Higher Education Act, export-control laws, SECURE contract terms, the Wolf Amendment, and restrictions involving foreign entities on U.S. government lists. The letter also requests award and contract details and asks whether NSF will prohibit award-funded research with listed entities.

**Who it affects** The letter focuses on UW and TAMU, which receive the initiative’s principal awards, and also identifies Stanford University’s Hoover Institution and university co-principal investigators as participants. It discusses publications involving PRC defense-linked institutions, including entities identified as appearing on the Commerce BIS Entity List.

**Why it matters** The committee argues that institutions tasked with protecting federally funded research should not simultaneously maintain relationships that may expose sensitive research, dual-use technologies, or taxpayer-funded advances to PRC military and defense organizations. The document presents these as concerns and requests for NSF review, not final agency findings.

**Key dates and numbers**
- SECURE is described as a five-year, $67 million initiative: $50 million for UW and $17 million for TAMU.
- The requested NSF response date is March 31, 2026.
- The letter cites collaborations and publications from 2023 through 2025, including a NASA award identified as NNX14AR40G.
