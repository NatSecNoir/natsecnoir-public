The House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party requests that Treasury investigate six China-domiciled companies and their subsidiaries and affiliates for possible support of Iran’s military and energy sectors and determine whether sanctions are warranted.

**What it does** The May 31, 2024 letter asks the Office of Foreign Assets Control and the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network to examine Norinco, AVIC, COSCO, CNOOC, CRRC, and Sinopec under the Countering America’s Adversaries Through Sanctions Act, the Iran Freedom and Counter-Proliferation Act, Executive Order 13224, Executive Order 13846, and other relevant authorities. It requests an analysis of whether the companies’ activities constitute material support or otherwise violate U.S. sanctions, including through their subsidiaries and affiliates, and asks Treasury to provide the analysis in a secure setting and brief the committee.

**Who it affects** The request concerns the six named Chinese companies, their majority- and minority-owned subsidiaries, and affiliates. It also addresses U.S. investors whose capital may flow to those companies, Iranian military and energy entities, and Treasury officials responsible for sanctions enforcement. The letter identifies alleged connections to the IRGC, Iranian oil, arms, shipping, rail, and aircraft activity.

**Why it matters** The committee argues that U.S. investment, goods, and services may be supporting Iran’s military, energy sector, and terrorist proxies, including groups involved in attacks in the Middle East. It cites billions of dollars in U.S. investment and urges Treasury to use existing sanctions authorities rather than permitting continued access to U.S. capital markets. The letter is a congressional investigative and policy request, not a sanctions designation or Treasury determination.

**Key dates and numbers**
- May 31, 2024: Letter date.
- June 14, 2024: Requested deadline for Treasury’s analysis, findings, and decisions on sanctions.
- Six: Chinese companies named for investigation.
- $6.5 billion: U.S. investment identified by the committee in April 2024 as capitalizing PRC companies it deemed national-security risks as of 2023.
- At least 1 million barrels per day: PRC imports of Iranian oil reported for January 2024.
