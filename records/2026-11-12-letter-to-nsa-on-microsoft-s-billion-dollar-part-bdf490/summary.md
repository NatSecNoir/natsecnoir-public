The House Foreign Affairs Committee and the House Select Committee on Strategic Competition with the CCP ask National Security Advisor Jake Sullivan to ensure heightened executive-branch scrutiny of Microsoft’s partnership with UAE-based artificial-intelligence company Group 42 Holding Ltd. (G42) before the deal advances to its second phase.

**What it does** The letter describes Microsoft’s announced $1.5 billion partnership with G42 and expresses concern about the transfer of sensitive U.S.-origin technology, including AI hardware, model weights, GPUs, research, and engineering advances. It requests an official Intelligence Community assessment of G42’s ties—including those of its subsidiaries and affiliates—to the Chinese Communist Party, the People’s Liberation Army, or other parts of the PRC apparatus. It also asks that the assessment and information about safeguards be shared with the committees before technology, services, or research are transferred to G42 or its affiliates, and before approval of a transition to Phase II.

**Who it affects** The immediate subjects are Microsoft and G42, including G42 CEO Peng Xiao and its PRC-linked affiliations. The requested review would involve the National Intelligence Council and other executive-branch officials, while the UAE and PRC are the principal foreign-policy and national-security contexts.

**Why it matters** The committees argue that G42’s PRC connections, the UAE’s cooperation with China, and the sensitivity of AI technologies create risks of direct or indirect transfer to PRC-linked entities. The letter calls for stronger national-security guardrails and congressional consultation as the partnership moves from a first phase with restricted sensitive-technology transfers toward possible Phase II exports.

**Key dates and numbers**
- July 11, 2024: Date of the letter; the document itself is dated July 10, 2024.
- April 15, 2024: Microsoft announced the partnership.
- $1.5 billion: Announced Microsoft investment in G42.
- 2031: UAE target year for becoming a global AI leader, as cited in the letter.
- Phase II: Proposed stage involving exports of sensitive U.S.-origin technology to the UAE.
