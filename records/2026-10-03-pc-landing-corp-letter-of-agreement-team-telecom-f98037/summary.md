The Agreement requires PC Landing Corp. to implement extensive national-security and law-enforcement mitigation measures for the Pacific Crossing Cable System (PC-1) as a condition of the FCC’s renewal of its submarine cable landing license.

**What it does** The National Security Agreement establishes a compliance framework covering PC-1’s network operations, facilities, equipment, service providers, covered third parties, personnel access, cybersecurity, incident reporting, and government oversight. PC Landing must appoint a U.S.-citizen Security Officer eligible for a Secret-level clearance, provide detailed system, equipment, vendor, and personnel information, obtain Compliance Monitoring Agency non-objection for specified equipment and service-provider changes, and maintain the ability to interrupt U.S.-bound or U.S.-originating traffic. Security incidents generally must be reported within 48 hours, while service-provider breaches must be disclosed within 72 hours.

**Who it affects** The obligations bind PC Landing and extend to covered third parties, service providers, equipment providers, contractors, and personnel with logical or physical access to PC-1, its Network Operations Centers, principal equipment, or sensitive U.S. records. DHS, DOJ, and DoD monitor compliance through the Committee.

**Why it matters** The Agreement operationalizes Team Telecom review of a submarine cable renewal by imposing continuing controls over foreign access, supply-chain risk, sensitive records, and operational resilience. Breach may prompt a recommendation that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, or terminate the relevant license.

**Key dates and numbers**
- PC-1’s existing license was due to expire on December 28, 2024; the renewal application was referred to the Committee on June 28, 2024.
- Most initial system, third-party, and equipment disclosures are due within 30–60 days of the NSA Effective Date; core security policies are due within 90 days.
- The Agreement was signed in April 2025, with DOJ and DoD signature dates of April 30, 2025.
- The Agreement supersedes PC Landing’s September 11, 2009 Letter of Assurance upon grant of the renewal license.
