The House Select Committee on the CCP requests that PayPal demonstrate full compliance with U.S. anti–money laundering obligations in its integrations with Tencent’s Tenpay Global and Weixin Pay.

**What it does** The letter seeks a staff briefing and records concerning PayPal’s April and July 2025 integrations, which allow users in the United States, Canada, and Europe to send funds to Weixin Pay wallets and linked bank accounts in China and to make purchases in China by scanning Weixin Pay QR codes. It asks for agreements, compliance organizational charts, AML risk assessments, customer-identification and enhanced-due-diligence procedures, suspicious-activity information, audits, examinations, and legal materials concerning reliance on Tenpay or offshore entities. It also directs PayPal to preserve relevant paper and electronic records.

**Who it affects** The requests are directed to PayPal President and CEO Alex Chriss and concern PayPal, Tenpay Global, Tencent Holdings Limited, and Weixin Pay. The Committee focuses on PayPal’s responsibilities as a U.S. money transmitter and FinCEN-registered money-services business under the Bank Secrecy Act.

**Why it matters** The Committee argues that Weixin Pay has been associated with Chinese money laundering organizations and fentanyl-related illicit finance. It warns that integrating PayPal with the platform could turn a coordination channel into a direct payment rail and raises questions about oversight where compliance operations involve a Tencent-controlled entity subject to Chinese law.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter dated September 19, 2025; catalogue date September 22, 2025.
- PayPal’s integrations were announced in April and July 2025.
- Requested materials and a staff briefing are due October 3, 2025.
- The requests generally cover records from January 1, 2023 forward.
- The letter cites House Resolution 5, § 4(a), 119th Congress (2025), as the Committee’s investigative authority.
