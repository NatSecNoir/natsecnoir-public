The House Select Committee on the CCP urges Commerce, Defense, Treasury, and the FCC to treat Futurewei presumptively like Huawei because documents produced in response to a prior inquiry allegedly show Huawei controls Futurewei’s ownership, finances, operations, governance, and personnel.

**What it does** The March 5, 2026 letter asks the agencies to take immediate action against Futurewei, including evaluating it for placement on the BIS Entity List, designation under Section 1260H’s Chinese Military Companies List, addition to the Non-SDN Chinese Military-Industrial Complex Companies List, inclusion on the FCC Covered List, and review under Executive Order 13873 and the ICTS Supply Chain Rules. It also requests a staff-level briefing on the agencies’ assessments and next steps and directs preservation of relevant records.

**Who it affects** The principal subject is Futurewei, which the letter describes as Huawei’s U.S. research and development subsidiary. The requested reviews would involve Commerce BIS, DoD, Treasury, and the FCC, and could affect Huawei-related U.S. operations, contracting, export controls, securities restrictions, and communications-equipment authorization.

**Why it matters** The letter argues that incorporation in the United States does not eliminate national-security risks where a foreign parent controls financing, directors, research, intellectual property, and personnel. It presents Futurewei as a potential U.S. proxy allowing Huawei to maintain operations despite existing restrictions, while acknowledging that not every Futurewei activity is necessarily concerning.

**Key dates and numbers**
- March 5, 2026: letter to the four agency heads and FCC chairman.
- March 19, 2026: requested deadline for a staff-level briefing.
- May 21, 2019: Huawei added to the BIS Entity List.
- 100%: ownership of Futurewei attributed to Huawei through Huawei Technologies Coöperatief U.A.
- At least 10: Futurewei personnel identified as shared with Huawei.
