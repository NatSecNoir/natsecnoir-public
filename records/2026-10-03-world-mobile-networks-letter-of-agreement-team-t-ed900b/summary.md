The Letter of Agreement establishes binding national-security and law-enforcement mitigation commitments for World Mobile’s proposed global resale telecommunications service under Section 214 authority.

**What it does** The agreement requires World Mobile and affiliated companies to protect U.S. Records, domestic communications, domestic communications infrastructure, lawful U.S. process, and principal telecommunications equipment from unauthorized access or disclosure. It requires cybersecurity and system-security plans, country-of-access and storage notices, equipment inventories and advance notice of equipment changes, network diagrams, service-provider controls, incident reporting, annual compliance reports, site visits, and potential third-party audits. DOJ may object or non-object to specified submissions, and the companies must address identified risks.

**Who it affects** The commitments apply to World Mobile Networks, LLC, World Mobile Group, Inc., World Mobile Group, Ltd., their personnel, foreign persons with access, principal-equipment vendors, managed-service providers, data-storage providers, and other service providers supporting U.S. services. The FCC’s Section 214 authorization is conditioned in practice by the agreement’s enforcement provisions.

**Why it matters** The LOA operationalizes Team Telecom mitigation through continuing controls over data, lawful-access process, foreign access, network equipment, ownership, and supply-chain risk. Breach or failure to resolve DOJ concerns may prompt action affecting the FCC authorization.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed June 11, 2025; the document is dated June 12, 2025.
- Initial country and principal-equipment lists are due within 30 days of FCC approval; security plans and network diagrams are due within 60 days.
- Security incidents and specified violations must be reported within 48 hours; unauthorized CPNI access must be reported within seven business days.
- Equipment of concern was to be removed by June 30, 2025, or reported beginning July 1, 2025.
- Annual reports are due one year after FCC approval and every year thereafter.
