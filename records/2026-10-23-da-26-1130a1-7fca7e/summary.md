The FCC Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau announces that specified Micro-Star International routers and a Vulcan Robotics advanced robotic device are conditionally approved by the Department of War and therefore exempt from the FCC Covered List.

**What it does** The notice updates the Covered List and its accompanying Conditional Approvals appendix to exclude Micro-Star International Co., Ltd.’s Radix GRBE94 and GRBE36 routers and Vulcan Robotics Inc.’s Sourccey mobile robotic platform. The exemptions depend on continuing compliance with the companies’ onshoring plans and updated product vetting. Failure to comply, or a false statement or misrepresentation in an application, may terminate an approval and return the equipment to the Covered List. The notice also republishes the current Covered List and conditional approvals for other device categories.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects Micro-Star International and Vulcan Robotics, as well as manufacturers, distributors, equipment authorization applicants, test labs, and telecommunications providers handling covered or conditionally approved equipment. FCC staff will provide guidance to TCBs, test labs, and applicants on the updates.

**Why it matters** The notice illustrates how an Executive Branch national-security determination can narrow the practical scope of the Covered List for particular foreign-produced devices without removing the broader category-level listing. It also confirms that exemptions are conditional and subject to ongoing compliance and vetting.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released October 21, 2026; DA 26-1130.
- Dockets: WC 18-89, ET 21-232, and EA 21-233.
- Routers were added to the Covered List on March 23, 2026; foreign-produced advanced robotic devices were added on July 28, 2026.
- The listed router approvals run from October 21, 2026, through April 17, 2028; the Sourccey approval is dated October 21, 2026.
