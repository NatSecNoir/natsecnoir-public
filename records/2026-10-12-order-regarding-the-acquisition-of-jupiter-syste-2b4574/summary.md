The President prohibits Suirui International’s acquisition of Jupiter Systems and orders divestment of the acquired company and specified assets under section 721 of the Defense Production Act.

**What it does** The order prohibits the February 28, 2020, transaction and Suirui ownership of interests in Jupiter or its assets, subject to specified exceptions. Within 120 calendar days, unless extended by CFIUS, the purchasers must divest Jupiter and its tangible and intangible assets, including intellectual property, non-public source code, and customer contracts. Pending verified divestment, the purchasers must not access specified Jupiter information, systems, products, records, or U.S. facilities; must maintain controls within 7 days; and must not take steps that impede compliance. Proposed buyers require notice to CFIUS and a 30-day non-objection period.

**Who it affects** The requirements bind Suirui, Suirui International, their affiliates and personnel, Jupiter, and the Jupiter Asia Companies: Jupiter Systems China (Hong Kong) LTD and Jie Xian Tong Computer Systems (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. CFIUS receives verification, monitoring, auditing, enforcement, and mitigation authority.

**Why it matters** This is a presidential CFIUS remedy for a completed foreign acquisition the President found might threaten U.S. national security. It combines a forced divestment with interim access restrictions, certifications, transaction controls, and continuing CFIUS oversight.

**Key dates and numbers**
- July 8, 2025: order date.
- February 28, 2020: acquisition completed.
- 7 days: deadline for access-control measures, absent extension.
- 120 days: divestment deadline, absent extension.
- 30 days: CFIUS non-objection period for a proposed buyer.
- 90 days: CFIUS verification period after divestment certification.
