The FCC announces that Department of War Conditional Approvals require exclusion of specified uncrewed aircraft systems, routers, and advanced robotic devices from the FCC Covered List, subject to continuing conditions.

**What it does** This Public Notice updates and republishes the Covered List and the list of Conditional Approvals as of October 2, 2026. It exempts Dronus S.p.A.’s K500L UAS and docking station, Vivid-Hosting, LLC’s product class of routers (including the VH-109 V1 and V2), and Matic Robots, Inc.’s robotic floor cleaner and charging dock. The exemptions depend on compliance with approved onshoring plans and, for Dronus and Matic Robots, updated product vetting. Noncompliance or material misrepresentations may terminate an approval and return equipment to the Covered List.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects the three approved manufacturers and users, applicants, test labs, TCBs, and other parties relying on the Covered List. It also maintains Covered List entries for foreign-produced UAS, routers, advanced robotic devices, power inverters, and previously listed telecommunications and surveillance equipment and services.

**Why it matters** The notice demonstrates how executive-branch national-security determinations alter the scope of the FCC’s supply-chain restrictions without removing the underlying categories. Conditional exemptions can be time-limited or remain effective only while the stated compliance conditions are satisfied.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released October 2, 2026; DA 26-1063.
- Relevant dockets: WC 18-89, ET 21-232, and EA 21-233.
- Vivid-Hosting’s approval terminates March 28, 2028; the notice states other approvals without termination dates may continue indefinitely subject to conditions.
