The House Select Committee on the CCP urges Bank of America to withdraw from underwriting Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., Limited’s planned Hong Kong initial public offering and requests detailed information about the bank’s due diligence, compliance reviews, and risk assessments.

**What it does** The April 17, 2025 letter from Chairman John Moolenaar to Bank of America CEO Brian Moynihan argues that CATL’s designation by the Department of Defense under Section 1260H, its alleged links to China’s military modernization, and its reported relationships with entities associated with forced labor in Xinjiang create national-security, regulatory, human-rights, and reputational risks. If the bank would not withdraw, the committee requested responses and supporting records on 21 specified topics, including communications with CATL, internal risk analyses, government consultations, investor disclosures, pricing, marketing, legal protections, and prospectus revisions.

**Who it affects** The direct recipient is Bank of America and its senior leadership. The letter concerns CATL, the Department of Defense’s 1260H designation regime, the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps, U.S. investors, and congressional oversight of transactions involving Chinese military-linked companies.

**Why it matters** The document illustrates congressional scrutiny of U.S. financial institutions’ participation in offerings by companies designated under Section 1260H. It connects the proposed underwriting to broader concerns about military-civil fusion, forced labor, sanctions exposure, U.S. manufacturing, and the America First Investment Policy, while seeking records that could clarify how the bank assessed those risks. The letter does not itself impose a legal restriction or make a regulatory determination about the IPO.

**Key dates and numbers**
- April 17, 2025: letter date.
- Section 1260H: statutory basis cited for CATL’s Department of Defense designation.
- 21: numbered requests for information and documents.
- April XX, 2025: response deadline stated in the letter, with the day left unspecified.
- H. Res. 5, Section 4(a): authority cited for the committee’s investigative work.
