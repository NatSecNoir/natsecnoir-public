The Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau adds Kaspersky Lab, Inc.’s cybersecurity and anti-virus software to the FCC’s Covered List based on a Department of Commerce ICTS Final Determination finding unacceptable national-security and U.S.-person security risks.

**What it does** The Public Notice updates the Covered List under section 2 of the Secure and Trusted Communications Networks Act and FCC rules §§ 1.50002–1.50003. The added entry covers cybersecurity and anti-virus software produced or provided by Kaspersky Lab, Inc., including its successors and assignees; the notice explains that the broader action encompasses software integrated into third-party products or services and related commercial hardware or software containing Kaspersky software. The complete updated list appears in the Appendix and on the FCC website.

**Who it affects** The action concerns Kaspersky, its affiliates, subsidiaries, parent companies, successors, and assignees, as well as U.S. persons and entities using, supplying, integrating, or purchasing affected cybersecurity and anti-virus products or services. It supplements the March 2022 Covered List entry for information-security products, solutions, and services supplied by AO Kaspersky Lab and related entities.

**Why it matters** The FCC update implements a Commerce determination under the ICTS regime rather than making an independent risk finding. It expands the Kaspersky-related Covered List category beyond the earlier DHS-based entry, aligning the FCC supply-chain list with Commerce’s June 20, 2024 prohibition on specified ICTS transactions involving Kaspersky products and services.

**Key dates and numbers**
- July 23, 2024: Public Notice released; Kaspersky software added to the Covered List.
- June 20, 2024: Commerce issued the relevant ICTS Final Determination; it was published at 89 Fed. Reg. 52434 on June 24, 2024.
- DA 24-712; WC Docket No. 18-89; ET Docket No. 21-232; EA Docket No. 21-233.
