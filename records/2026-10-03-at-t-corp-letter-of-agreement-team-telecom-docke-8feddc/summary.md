AT&T Corp. commits to extensive national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety mitigation measures so DHS can seek conditioning of AT&T’s FCC cable landing license for the Taino-Carib Cable System.

**What it does** The Letter of Assurances requires AT&T to maintain a U.S.-based security point of contact; provide DHS with network, equipment, vendor, contractor, and restoration information; preserve the ability to interrupt U.S.-bound or U.S.-originating traffic; and give advance notice of specified equipment, vendor, testing, service, and operational changes. It also requires logical and physical security policies, personnel screening, incident reporting, employee instruction, annual compliance reports, possible third-party audits, and DHS consultation and site visits. DHS may object to proposed equipment or providers, and AT&T must not proceed with certain challenged activities without written authorization.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind AT&T and its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates, covering the portions of Taino-Carib under AT&T’s control and interactions with other consortium members, vendors, contractors, and subcontractors. DHS receives approval, oversight, inspection, and audit rights; the FCC may condition or take action against the relevant license if DHS requests a remedy after a breach.

**Why it matters** The agreement illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for a submarine cable connecting Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, and the British Virgin Islands. It translates national-security concerns into continuing controls over cable architecture, access, personnel, ownership changes, operational resilience, and foreign participation.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed and signed: June 22, 2020.
- POC, initial system information, and principal-equipment list: generally due within 45 days of execution.
- Security, physical-security, screening, and related policies: generally due within 90 days.
- Certain changes require 30 or 45 days’ advance notice; incidents require written notice within 48 hours and a follow-up within 10 days.
- Foreign ownership/control notice thresholds: above 10% for AT&T or Taino-Carib; above 5% for another consortium member’s interest.
