The FCC’s Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau updates the FCC Covered List to exempt specified uncrewed aircraft systems (UAS), UAS critical components, and routers that received Conditional Approvals from the Department of War (DoW).

**What it does** The Public Notice finds that DoW’s Conditional Approvals are “a specific determination” that the identified devices do not pose unacceptable risks to U.S. national security. It therefore removes 
- Ascento, Inc.’s Ascento Guard Dori USA v1 UAS and 
- WNC Corporation’s LVR5A, LVM9, and LV85C Wi-Fi routers 

from the Covered List. The notice also republishes the updated Covered List and the broader inventory of devices with Conditional Approvals, including prior approvals for other UAS and routers.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects manufacturers and applicants producing foreign-country UAS, UAS critical components, and routers, as well as equipment authorization applicants, test labs, and telecommunications certification bodies. The Ascento approval remains effective indefinitely if the applicant follows its onshoring plan and updated product vetting. WNC’s router approval runs through February 21, 2028.

**Why it matters** The notice shows how agency-specific national-security determinations change the scope of the FCC Covered List without eliminating the underlying category-based restrictions. A Conditional Approval can preserve market access for specified devices, but the exemption may end if an applicant fails its onshoring plan or the government discovers false statements or misrepresentations.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released and Covered List updated: August 21, 2026.
- DA number: DA 26-875; related dockets: WC 18-89, ET 21-232, and EA 21-233.
- UAS and UAS critical components produced abroad were added to the Covered List on December 22, 2025; foreign-produced routers were added on March 23, 2026.
- WNC router Conditional Approval terminates February 21, 2028; the Ascento UAS approval has no stated termination date, subject to compliance conditions.
