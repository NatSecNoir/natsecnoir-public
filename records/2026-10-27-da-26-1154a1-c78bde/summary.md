The FCC announces six new Department of War Conditional Approvals and exempts the specified foreign-produced devices from the FCC Covered List.

**What it does** The Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau updates the Covered List and identifies Conditional Approvals for Manna Drones Delivery’s 383.2 uncrewed aircraft systems; Alpha Networks’ XGSPON ONT 1000 series router; Bloom Energy’s INV-750, AC7, RI7, and Central power inverter platforms; Hitachi Energy Power Conversion Solutions’ WD3 and WD4 power inverters; Haystack Robotics’ Violet AI robotic device; and T-Robotics’ entire class of automated guided vehicles. The exemptions depend on continuing compliance with the applicable onshoring plans and, except for Alpha Networks, updated product vetting.

**Who it affects** The action affects the six named manufacturers and their identified devices, as well as equipment-authorization applicants, test labs, and telecommunications certification bodies that must account for the updated Covered List. The notice also maintains the broader restrictions on foreign-produced uncrewed aircraft systems, routers, power inverters, and advanced robotic devices unless an applicable exception or Conditional Approval applies.

**Why it matters** The notice translates national-security determinations into FCC equipment-authorization consequences: approved devices may be authorized despite otherwise falling within Covered List categories. A Conditional Approval may terminate, with the equipment returned to the Covered List, if the applicant violates its onshoring plan or made false or misleading statements.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released October 23, 2026; the six listed Conditional Approvals are dated October 23, 2026.
- Alpha Networks’ router approval runs through April 24, 2028.
- The action concerns DA 26-1154 and WC, ET, and EA Dockets 18-89, 21-232, and 21-233.
