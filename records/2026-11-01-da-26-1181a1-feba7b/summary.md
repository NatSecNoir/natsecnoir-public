The FCC’s Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau updates the Covered List to exempt specified foreign-produced uncrewed aircraft systems, advanced robotic devices, and power inverters after the Department of War granted Conditional Approvals.

**What it does** The notice treats the Department of War’s National Security Determinations as “a specific determination” that the identified devices do not pose unacceptable risks, and therefore removes those devices from the scope of the Covered List. The approvals cover Voliro AG’s T uncrewed aircraft system; specified products from ARAPL RaaS, ATI Motors, Lightberry Holdings, SunScout Holdings, Swap Robotics Canada, and OMRON Holdings; and Ingeteam Power Technology’s listed INGECON 3POWER inverter models. The exemptions are reflected in the attached Covered List and Conditional Approvals appendix.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects the named manufacturers and their covered devices, as well as equipment-authorization applicants, test labs, and telecommunications certification bodies relying on the Covered List. It does not remove the broader category-based restrictions on foreign-produced UAS, advanced robotic devices, or power inverters.

**Why it matters** Conditional Approval creates a route for specified foreign-produced equipment to obtain FCC equipment authorization despite otherwise falling within Covered List categories. The exemptions remain subject to continuing compliance with approved onshoring plans and updated product vetting; noncompliance, false statements, or misrepresentations can result in termination and return to the Covered List.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released October 30, 2026; DA 26-1181.
- Dockets: WC 18-89, ET 21-232, and EA 21-233.
- The notice grants approvals for one UAS, six advanced-robotics company groups, and one power-inverter company.
