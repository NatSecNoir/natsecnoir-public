The President prohibited Ralls Corporation’s acquisition of four Oregon wind-farm project companies and ordered divestment based on national-security concerns under section 721 of the Defense Production Act.

Note: see challenge in Ralls v CFIUS (DC Cir 2013)

**What it does** The order bars Ralls, its subsidiaries and affiliates, the Sany Group, and executives Dawei Duan and Jialing Wu from owning interests in the four Project Companies or their assets. Ralls must divest the companies, assets, intellectual property, technology, personnel, customer contracts, and related operations within 90 days, subject to a possible CFIUS extension of up to three months. The order also requires removal of stored or installed items from identified properties within 14 calendar days, ends company access to those properties, restricts transfers to third parties, and requires CFIUS compliance certifications and verification. It revokes CFIUS’s July 25 and August 2, 2012 interim mitigation orders.

**Who it affects** The order directly affects Ralls, the Sany Group, Duan, Wu, and Lower Ridge, High Plateau, Mule Hollow, and Pine City Windfarm, LLC. CFIUS receives oversight and verification authority; the Department of Defense must verify removal before a sale or transfer, and the Attorney General may enforce the order.

**Why it matters** This is a presidential blocking and divestment order addressing a foreign acquisition of U.S. assets on national-security grounds. It illustrates CFIUS’s ability to impose detailed post-order controls, including property access limits, buyer screening, record inspections, and technical-data review.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Order date: September 28, 2012; Federal Register publication: October 3, 2012.
- Divestment deadline: 90 days after the order, with a possible extension of up to three months.
- Property-removal deadline: 14 calendar days after the order.
- CFIUS verification: within 90 days after completed divestment.
- Proposed buyer review: no provisional or final CFIUS objection within 10 business days after notice.
