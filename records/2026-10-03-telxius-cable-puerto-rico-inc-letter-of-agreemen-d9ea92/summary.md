The Letter of Agreement establishes national-security and law-enforcement mitigation commitments for operation of the MAREA submarine cable system in connection with a proposed transfer of certain negative control rights from KKR to Pontegadea.

**What it does** The Telxius Parties agree to maintain a U.S.-citizen security point of contact, provide cable-system and equipment information, preserve the ability to interrupt U.S. traffic, obtain review of specified equipment, vendors, testing, and operational changes, and implement logical and physical security, personnel screening, incident reporting, training, audit, and inspection measures. The agreement also makes compliance a condition of the relevant cable landing license and allows the Compliance Monitoring Agencies to recommend FCC license remedies for breaches.

**Who it affects** The primary obligations apply to Telxius Cable América and Telxius Cable USA. Telefónica, Telxius Telecom, and Pontegadea undertake obligations concerning non-interference with compliance and provision of adequate resources; DHS, DOJ, and DoD monitor compliance, while the FCC is asked to condition approval of the transaction on the LOA.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for foreign participation in a U.S.-connected submarine cable, covering access to domestic communications infrastructure, U.S. records, network operations, supply chains, and foreign personnel. It supersedes and terminates Telxius USA’s April 28, 2017 Letter of Assurances upon FCC approval.
