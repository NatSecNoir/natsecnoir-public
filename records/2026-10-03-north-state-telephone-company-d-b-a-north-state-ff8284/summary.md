The Letter of Agreement establishes Team Telecom mitigation commitments addressing national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety concerns associated with MTN’s proposed indirect-control transfer of North State’s Section 214 authorization holders.

**What it does** This agreement requires North State to maintain a U.S.-based law-enforcement point of contact, preserve and produce U.S. Records in response to Lawful U.S. Process, restrict unauthorized foreign access or disclosure, and obtain DOJ objection or non-objection for specified foreign personnel, offshore storage, outsourced service providers, network equipment, and material business or service changes. It also requires network-security and cybersecurity plans, personnel screening, incident reporting, third-party breach provisions, principal-equipment inventories, annual compliance reports, and access for DOJ site visits and interviews. Material noncompliance may support an FCC action against relevant authorizations.

**Who it affects** The obligations bind North State and its approved vendors or trusted third parties, while MTN and North State are the parties whose transfer application prompted review. DOJ, including the FBI, receives notices and oversees objections; the agreement defines Team Telecom to include DOJ, DHS, and DoD.

**Why it matters** The LOA ties FCC approval of a telecommunications-control transfer to continuing controls over U.S. communications, customer data, lawful surveillance, foreign access, supply-chain visibility, and cybersecurity. It also provides that DOJ will notify the FCC of its non-objection after execution.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed April 7, 2020; FCC application ITC-T/C-20191223-00201 and WC Docket No. 19-393.
- LEPOC information is due within 15 days; responsive records generally within five days.
- Security-plan updates and the principal-equipment list are due within 90 days.
- Security-incident notice is due within 15 days; third-party breach notice must occur within 72 hours of discovery.
- Annual reports begin one year after the LOA date.
