The House Select Committee on the CCP urges University of Montana President Seth Bodnar to terminate the university’s ties with the China-United States Exchange Foundation (CUSEF) and adopt rigorous vetting of foreign partnerships.

**What it does** The December 3, 2023 letter objects to a summer 2024 China study trip promoted by the Alexander Blewett III School of Law and its affiliated Max Baucus Institute and hosted and funded by CUSEF. It characterizes CUSEF as aligned with Chinese Communist Party influence operations, citing its leadership connections to the Chinese People’s Political Consultative Conference, its reported Foreign Agent Registration Act filings through representatives, and public statements about Beijing’s use of nominally private civic organizations. The authors ask the university to reject funding and other support from entities closely aligned with the CCP.

**Who it affects** The requested actions directly concern the University of Montana, its law school, the Baucus Institute, prospective participants in the China study trip, and CUSEF. The letter is signed by Chairman Mike Gallagher and Representatives Ryan Zinke and Matt Rosendale.

**Why it matters** The letter frames university partnerships and study-abroad funding as potential channels for foreign influence that could affect academic freedom, public perceptions of the PRC, and U.S. national security. It also presents prior rejection of CUSEF funding by another U.S. university as a model for heightened institutional scrutiny.
