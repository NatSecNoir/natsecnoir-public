The House Select Committee on the CCP urges Panama to exclude entities subject to Chinese Communist Party direction from future control or operation of ports adjacent to the Panama Canal, particularly warning against China COSCO Shipping Corporation.

**What it does**
In a July 17, 2025 letter to Panama’s minister responsible for the canal-port audit, Chairman John Moolenaar applauds Panama’s efforts to counter PRC influence and asks the government to ensure that any firm or consortium taking over the Cristobal and Balboa ports is not beholden to the CCP. The letter highlights Panama’s audit of concessions managed by Hong Kong-based CK Hutchison and reports that CKH may owe approximately $300 million in unpaid fees. It also cites the Defense Department’s addition of COSCO to its 1260H list and research describing political commissars aboard Chinese merchant vessels.

**Who it affects**
The letter is directed to Administrator Luis A. Roquebert V. and concerns the Panamanian government, CK Hutchison, COSCO, prospective port operators, and U.S.-Panama national-security interests. It also references the CCP, PRC, Defense Department, and the Naval War College’s China Maritime Security Initiative.

**Why it matters**
The committee frames Chinese-linked control of canal-adjacent ports as a risk to Panama, the United States, and Latin America, and connects commercial port ownership with military-civilian influence and political control concerns.

**Key dates and numbers**
- July 17, 2025: Letter date.
- Approximately $300 million: unpaid fees the letter says CKH owes Panama.
- 1260H: Defense Department list designation cited for COSCO.
