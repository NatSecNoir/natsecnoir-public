The House Select Committee on the CCP urges the Secretaries of Commerce, Defense, and the Treasury to investigate WuXi AppTec and WuXi Biologics and consider placing them on U.S. national-security control lists.

**What it does** The February 12, 2024 letter alleges that the Chinese biotechnology and research-services companies have close ties to the Chinese Communist Party and the People’s Liberation Army, including through company leadership, military-civil fusion activities, PLA-linked investment, and cooperation involving Chinese state research institutions. It asks the departments to consider adding WuXi AppTec and its subsidiaries to the DoD Chinese Military Companies List (1260H list), the Commerce Bureau of Industry and Security Entity List, and Treasury’s Non-SDN Chinese Military-Industrial Complex Companies List. The letter also raises concerns about genetic testing in Xinjiang, data and intellectual-property exposure, and the companies’ integration into U.S. biotech supply chains.

**Who it affects** The request directly concerns WuXi AppTec, WuXi Biologics, their U.S. biotech and research-service partners, and the three departments addressed. It identifies company executives Li Ge and Chen Zhisheng as examples of alleged party and military connections.

**Why it matters** The letter frames biotechnology, genomic information, contract research, and access to sensitive intellectual property as national-security issues. It illustrates congressional pressure to use designation, export-control, and sanctions authorities against a Chinese biotech firm, while requesting investigation rather than announcing a listing or other agency action.

**Key dates and numbers**
- February 12, 2024: Letter date.
- 2016: WuXi AppTec’s reported strategic collaboration with Huawei to store population genomic data in China.
- Requested actions: consideration for three U.S. control lists, including the 1260H and Entity Lists.
- The letter is addressed to the Secretaries of Commerce, Defense, and the Treasury.
