The House Select Committee on the CCP requests that the FBI and the Director of National Intelligence brief the Committee on GenScript Biotechnology Co., Ltd., its subsidiaries, CCP ties, and potential U.S. national-security risks.

**What it does** The May 30, 2024 letter identifies GenScript’s three main business segments—Bestzyme, Legend Biotech, and ProBio—and asks for intelligence and law-enforcement information concerning the company and its U.S.-based subsidiaries. It highlights GenScript’s internal CCP Party Committee, named company officials with Party roles, its biotechnology and gene-synthesis activities, and its U.S. government contracts. The letter also references an accompanying corporate map showing alleged PRC state and CCP involvement.

**Who it affects** The request is directed to ODNI Director Avril Haines and FBI Director Christopher Wray, on behalf of the Select Committee chaired by John Moolenaar with Raja Krishnamoorthi as ranking member. It concerns GenScript, its business segments, U.S. subsidiaries, U.S. government customers, and the biotechnology sector more broadly.

**Why it matters** The Committee says GenScript’s CDMO services and access to sensitive biotechnology could create risks to U.S. intellectual property, national security, and competitiveness. It asks officials to assess the extent of CCP influence or control, while acknowledging that the company’s stated efforts to strengthen PRC biotech do not necessarily establish illicit conduct.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter dated May 30, 2024; catalogue date: May 31, 2024.
- Requested briefing response and scheduling by June 28, 2024.
- GenScript’s three identified business segments: Bestzyme, Legend Biotech, and ProBio.
- The letter states that GenScript received millions of dollars in U.S. government contracts since 2023.
