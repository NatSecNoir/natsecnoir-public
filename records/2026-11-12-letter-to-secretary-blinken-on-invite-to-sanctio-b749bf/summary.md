The House Select Committee on the CCP asked Secretary of State Antony Blinken to clarify whether the administration invited sanctioned Hong Kong Chief Executive John Lee Ka-chiu to the 2023 APEC summit and to rescind any invitation extended to Hong Kong officials.

**What it does**
The November 6, 2023 letter alleges that Hong Kong authorities reported Lee had “personally received” a U.S. invitation to attend the APEC Leaders’ Summit, despite a prior State Department assurance that the United States would not invite him because of U.S. sanctions. It asks Blinken to confirm whether Lee or the broader Hong Kong delegation received written communications, identify who made any invitation decision and when, and explain whether discussions with Wang Yi or pressure from the PRC influenced that decision. The authors also request that the Secretary respond by November 13, 2023.

**Who it affects**
The letter is addressed to Blinken and concerns the administration’s dealings with Hong Kong SAR officials, including Lee and Financial Secretary Paul Chan. It also implicates the People’s Republic of China, APEC participation, and congressional oversight of U.S. policy toward Hong Kong. Mike Gallagher signed as committee chairman, with Senator Marco Rubio as a co-signer.

**Why it matters**
The committee argues that inviting Hong Kong officials could conflict with U.S. treatment of Hong Kong after the imposition of the National Security Law and with annual State Department certifications that Hong Kong no longer holds its former level of autonomy. It characterizes official participation as potentially recognizing Hong Kong as an economy distinct from the PRC and asks whether continued APEC recognition is consistent with the statutory framework cited in the letter.

**Key dates and numbers**
- November 6, 2023: Letter date.
- November 13, 2023: Requested deadline for a response.
- November 15, 2023: Scheduled APEC summit in San Francisco.
- 2020: John Lee was placed under U.S. sanctions pursuant to Executive Order 13936, according to the letter.
- July 21, 2023: Cited State Department email exchange with congressional staff.
- July 2022: Lee became Hong Kong’s Chief Executive, according to the letter.
- October 26–28, 2023: Wang Yi’s visit referenced in a question to Blinken.
- Seven numbered questions were submitted to the Secretary.
