The five Havfrue System licensees commit to U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) mitigation measures addressing national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety concerns so DHS can notify the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) that it has no objection to the pending cable-landing application.

**What it does** The Letter of Assurances establishes governance, information-sharing, operational, cybersecurity, physical-security, personnel-screening, incident-reporting, training, change-control, and annual-reporting obligations for the Havfrue System, a private submarine fiber-optic network connecting the United States with Denmark, Ireland, and Norway. The licensees must maintain cleared points of contact, provide system and equipment information, preserve the ability to interrupt U.S. traffic, give advance notice of equipment and vendor changes, and permit DHS consultation and site visits. A breach may lead DHS to request that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify a relevant authorization.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind America Europe Connect 2 USA, America Europe Connect 2 Limited, Edge Cable Holdings USA, GU Holdings, and Optibulk Havfrue, including successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates, with individual responsibility for each licensee’s submarine line terminal equipment.

**Why it matters** This is a Team Telecom mitigation instrument tying a cable-landing authorization to continuing government oversight of network architecture, vendors, access, ownership, resilience, and lawful process.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated September 2, 2019; application docket SCL-LIC-20180511-00010.
- Initial points of contact are due within 30 business days of execution; the primary contact must be a U.S. citizen with a Secret-level-or-higher clearance.
- System information, security policies, and screening policies generally have 60-business-day deadlines; incident reports are due within 10 business days of discovery.
- Changes to principal equipment or vendors generally require 15 or 30 business days’ advance notice; foreign ownership or control above 10% requires notice within 10 business days.
