The interim final rule would restrict exports, reexports, and in-country transfers of advanced computing items when they could provide significant computing capacity to entities headquartered in Macau or Country Group D:5 destinations, while adding diversion controls for five additional countries and rescinding the Biden-era AI Diffusion Rule.

**What it does** The rule adds a license requirement under new 15 CFR § 744.24 for specified advanced computing items destined for data centers that provide more than 12,000,000 aggregate TPP through one or more linked data centers to Macau- or D:5-headquartered companies. It also requires due diligence concerning direct or indirect ownership and control, establishes a case-by-case licensing regime for Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Brazil, and Thailand, and requires monthly reports for transactions qualifying for a U.S.-headquartered-consignee exclusion. The rule revises controls and presumptions affecting front-end fabricators and OSAT companies, retains the advanced-compute manufacturing exception, and removes model-weights controls, country tiers, and AI-related license exceptions from the AI Diffusion Rule.

**Who it affects** U.S. and foreign chip exporters, reexporters, transferors, data-center operators, foreign foundries, chip packagers, advanced IC designers, and companies using overseas computing capacity would face new screening, licensing, notification, reporting, or recordkeeping obligations. Commercial recommender systems receive a limited exception if specified information and diligence are submitted.

**Why it matters** The rule seeks to prevent remote access and diversion pathways from enabling advanced model development, military and intelligence activities, cyber operations, or weapons-related capabilities, while shifting from worldwide AI-diffusion tiers toward destination- and end-use-based controls.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Effective date: date of publication in the Federal Register, not supplied in the draft.
- Comments: due 60 days after Federal Register publication.
- Existing affected data centers: must obtain a license within 90 days of the effective date.
- Savings clause: qualifying en-route shipments may be completed within 30 days after publication.
- New reporting: monthly reports are due by the first business day of the following month.
- Threshold: more than 12,000,000 aggregate TPP.
