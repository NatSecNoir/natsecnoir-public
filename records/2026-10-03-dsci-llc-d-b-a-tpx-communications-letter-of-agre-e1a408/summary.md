U.S. TelePacific Corp. and U.S. TelePacific Holdings Corp. agree to extend their existing Team Telecom mitigation commitments to DSCI, LLC and DSCI Corporation of Virginia, Inc. in connection with the proposed transfer of control of DSCI’s FCC international and domestic authorizations.

**What it does** The letter incorporates and extends commitment documents dating from 2005 through 2011, plus a 2010 FCC application amendment, to the DSCI transaction. The commitments require compliance with lawful-interception laws and legal process, including CALEA; maintenance of a CALEA-compliant solution; cooperation with law enforcement in resolving surveillance problems; and prompt notice to DOJ and DHS of changes in designated contacts. The company designates Michael I. Cowan as the U.S.-based point of contact responsible for accepting and overseeing wiretap, electronic-surveillance, pen-register/trap-and-trace orders, subpoenas, and other lawful process.

**Who it affects** The commitments apply to U.S. TelePacific and its operating subsidiaries, including DSCI and DSCI Corporation of Virginia, and continue to cover the previously identified Pac-West, Mpower, Arrival, NextWeb, and TelePacific operations.

**Why it matters** The letter supplies the national-security and law-enforcement assurances supporting Team Telecom’s lack of objection to the FCC’s Section 214 transfer-of-control application. It also ties authorization of the transaction to operational access, records-handling, lawful surveillance, and U.S.-based compliance oversight.

**Key dates and numbers**
- March 2, 2005: original Pac-West commitment letter.
- August 25, 2010: amendment in WC Docket No. 10-156 concerning O1 Communications.
- March 7, 2011 and October 18, 2011: later commitment letters concerning NextWeb and Tel West.
- September 2, 2016: DSCI extension letter.
- Five business days: stated deadline for referring non-U.S.-government requests or legal process to DOJ and DHS, subject to U.S. law and court orders.
