Speed Telco LLC submits an amendment to its pending application for an international Section 214 authorization, updating its address and ownership information.

**What it does** The September 6, 2013 letter, filed via IBFS and addressed to FCC Secretary Marlene H. Dortch, states that Exhibit A amends the response to Question 1 and that the letter also amends the response to Section 63.18(h), including information previously provided in the original application and Attachment 2. The amended applicant address is 6950 France Ave South, Suite 27, Edina, Minnesota 55435.

**Who it affects** The filing concerns Speed Telco LLC and identifies shareholders directly or indirectly owning or controlling at least 10 percent of its common stock. Abdikarim Nur is listed as an 80% owner, while DO Technology owns 20%; Alamjeet Singh is identified as DO Technology’s 100% owner. The filing also identifies their citizenship and principal telecommunications businesses.

**Why it matters** The amendment updates the ownership and contact information available to the FCC while Speed Telco’s international Section 214 application remains pending. It records the applicant’s stated ownership chain, including Nur’s U.S. citizenship granted on July 17, 2013, and foreign affiliations reflected in the filing.

**Key dates and numbers**
- September 6, 2013: amendment letter date.
- ITC-214-20130221-00063: international Section 214 application docket.
- Ownership reported: Abdikarim Nur 80%; DO Technology 20%; Alamjeet Singh 100% of DO Technology.
- July 17, 2013: U.S. citizenship date stated for Abdikarim Nur.
