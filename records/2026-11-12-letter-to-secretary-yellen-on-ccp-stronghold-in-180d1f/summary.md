The House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party urged Treasury Secretary Janet Yellen to withhold federal support from Gotion High-Tech Co. and explain how Treasury and CFIUS were handling the company’s planned Michigan battery plant.

**What it does** This September 14, 2023 letter from Chairman Mike Gallagher and Representative John Moolenaar asks Treasury whether it intends to provide taxpayer funding or other support to Gotion’s lithium-ion battery manufacturing project. It also requests information about Treasury’s communications with Gotion and Michigan officials, and asks why the project does not currently fall under CFIUS jurisdiction. The lawmakers further seek congressional reforms that would bring similar projects within CFIUS’s authority.

**Who it affects** The inquiry directly concerns Treasury, Gotion High-Tech Co., the State of Michigan, Governor Gretchen Whitmer’s office, and CFIUS. It also addresses federal tax incentives and other potential support for Chinese battery companies operating or investing in the United States.

**Why it matters** The letter links foreign investment screening with federal subsidies and economic-security concerns. It alleges that Gotion has ties to the Chinese Communist Party and state-owned financial institutions, and questions whether existing CFIUS authorities adequately cover a PRC-backed battery manufacturing project.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter dated September 14, 2023.
- Treasury was asked to respond by September 29, 2023.
- The letter poses four principal questions, with follow-ups concerning funding, communications, and CFIUS reform.
