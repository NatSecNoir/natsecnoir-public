The Letter of Agreement establishes national-security and law-enforcement mitigation commitments for operation of the Pacific Caribbean Cable System (PCCS) in connection with the proposed transfer of certain negative control rights from KKR to Pontegadea.

**What it does** The Telxius Parties agree to maintain a U.S.-citizen security point of contact; provide cable-system, equipment, vendor, and peering information; preserve the ability to interrupt U.S. traffic; obtain required notice or approvals for equipment, vendor, testing, and operational changes; implement logical and physical security, personnel screening, incident reporting, training, audits, and access controls; and report specified ownership or control changes. DHS, DOJ, and DoD may review, object to, audit, and visit relevant facilities. The agencies will ask the FCC to condition approval of the transaction on compliance with the LOA.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind Telxius Cable América, Telxius Cable USA, Telxius Cable Puerto Rico, Telxius Cable Colombia, and, for Section 19, Telefónica, Telxius Telecom, and Pontegadea. They apply to PCCS operations, infrastructure, personnel, contractors, vendors, and U.S. records.

**Why it matters** The agreement converts Team Telecom’s review into continuing operational controls backed by potential FCC license remedies, including modification, revocation, or termination. It also supersedes Telxius USA’s 2014 Letter of Assurance upon FCC approval.
