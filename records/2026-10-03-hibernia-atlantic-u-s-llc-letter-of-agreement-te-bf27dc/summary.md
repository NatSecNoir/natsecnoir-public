The Letter of Assurance sets out amended national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety commitments supporting the proposed transfer of control of Hibernia-related Section 214 authorities and a submarine cable landing license.

**What it does** This December 15, 2014 undertaking carries forward Hibernia’s 2010 commitments and expands them to the Project Express undersea cable system. It requires an initial list of manufacturers for principal equipment within five business days, government review within 30 calendar days, advance notice for certain newly introduced equipment, maintenance of detailed equipment and vendor lists, and post-deployment notice for previously identified or approved manufacturers. A lack of agency response within the specified 30-day periods may be treated as approval; equipment from a manufacturer that is not approved may not be deployed. The agreement also addresses equipment controlled by independent holders of indefeasible rights of use and Hibernia customers.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind KCK Limited and Hibernia NGS Limited, with operational implications for Hibernia Atlantic U.S. LLC and Hibernia Media, LLC, and provide review and enforcement roles to DHS, DOJ, and DoD.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation of foreign ownership and infrastructure-security concerns through continuing government oversight of equipment manufacturers, vendors, contractors, and subcontractors on a cable system connected to the United States.

**Key dates and numbers**
- December 15, 2014: LOA date.
- Five business days: deadline for the initial manufacturer list.
- Thirty calendar days: ordinary agency response and advance-notice periods.
- Twenty calendar days, if practicable: targeted review after a potential concern.
- Three FCC applications: ITC-T/C-20140820-00242, ITC-T/C-20140820-00244, and SCL-T/C-20140820-00007.
