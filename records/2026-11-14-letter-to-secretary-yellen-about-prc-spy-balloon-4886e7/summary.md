The House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party urged Treasury Secretary Janet Yellen to sanction PRC companies linked to the spy-balloon program and prevent U.S. financial and technological support for those entities.

**What it does** The February 20, 2023 letter asks Treasury to add the six PRC entities recently placed on the Commerce Department’s Entity List to the Non-SDN Chinese Military-Industrial Complex List (NS-CMIC List), investigate whether they should also be placed on the Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) List, and expand the NS-CMIC List to cover other companies meeting its statutory criteria. It specifically identifies Eagles Men Aviation Science and Technology Group as an example and highlights China National Chemical Corporation (ChemChina), which appears on DoD’s section 1260H list but not on the NS-CMIC List. The committee also requests a briefing on Treasury’s listing decisions, the relationship among the Entity List, NS-CMIC List, SDN List, and DoD’s 1260H list, and any U.S. technology used in the balloon program.

**Who it affects** The request concerns Treasury, Commerce, DoD, U.S. persons, and PRC companies involved in defense, surveillance, aerospace, or military-civil-fusion activities. It would particularly affect entities added to the NS-CMIC or SDN Lists and U.S. persons considering financial or commercial dealings with them.

**Why it matters** The letter argues that Entity List restrictions on exports do not by themselves restrict financial support and cites the spy balloon’s surveillance of U.S. territory as a reason for broader sanctions. It calls for full blocking sanctions against qualifying entities, which would block their assets and prohibit U.S. persons from transacting with them, while linking the requested action to Executive Order 13959 and DoD’s 1260H framework.

**Key dates and numbers**
- February 20, 2023: letter date.
- February 10, 2023: Commerce’s cited addition of six PRC entities to the Entity List, 88 Fed. Reg. 9389.
- June 3, 2021: Executive Order 13959, establishing the NS-CMIC framework cited by the letter.
- Six: recent Entity List additions the committee asks Treasury to evaluate for NS-CMIC or SDN designation.
- 1260H: section of the William M. (Mac) Thornberry National Defense Authorization Act of 2021 concerning Chinese military companies.
