The House Select Committee on the CCP requests information from BlackRock and MSCI about facilitating U.S. investment in Chinese companies identified on U.S. government red-flag lists, including entities linked to the PLA, forced labor, surveillance, and national-security risks.

**What it does** The paired July 31, 2023 letters examine five BlackRock funds and five MSCI indexes. They identify more than 20 companies or affiliates in the BlackRock funds and at least 40 in the MSCI indexes, including companies appearing on the FCC Covered List, the Section 1260H list, the Entity List, and other statutory or sanctions-related lists. The letters request machine-readable holdings or index data, investment or benchmark exposure, screening and inclusion policies, due-diligence methods, conflicts-of-interest materials, communications, and information about U.S. regulatory inquiries. Each recipient is also directed to preserve relevant documents.

**Who it affects** The requests are directed to BlackRock CEO Larry Fink and MSCI CEO Henry Fernandez. They concern investors and retirement savers whose funds may track the identified products, as well as named Chinese companies such as ZTE, CGN Power, BGI Genomics, Hoshine Silicon Industry, Qihoo 360, and Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation.

**Why it matters** The letters connect capital-market and index-provider decisions to national-security, human-rights, forced-labor, and military-civil-fusion concerns. They also illustrate congressional scrutiny of how private financial products incorporate companies designated under telecommunications, defense, procurement, and trade-control regimes.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letters dated July 31, 2023; responses requested by August 14, 2023.
- BlackRock review covered five funds and estimated more than $429 million in identified red-flag investments.
- MSCI review covered five indexes and found at least 40 listed companies or affiliates.
- The letters request exposure data as of June 30, 2023, January 1, 2023, and January 1, 2022.
