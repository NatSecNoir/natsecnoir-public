The Committee for the Assessment of Foreign Participation in the United States Telecommunications Services Sector consents to Poseidon International Connectivity Pte. Ltd. assuming the National Security Agreement obligations tied to the JGA North and South submarine cable systems, supporting FCC consideration of the related license assignments.

**What it does** This letter records Poseidon International’s acknowledgement that it will become successor to RTI JGA Pte. Ltd. and RTI Connectivity Pte. Ltd. under the separate July 6, 2020 National Security Agreements when the FCC assigns the relevant submarine cable landing-license interests. The compliance monitoring agencies—the Departments of Justice, Homeland Security, and Defense—provide written consent to the transfer of the RTI entities’ rights and obligations. The letter states that the FCC will be notified of no objection, conditioned on Poseidon’s compliance with the applicable agreements, and that the letter and agreements will accompany that notification.

**Who it affects** The arrangement directly affects Poseidon International, the RTI entities, the receivers Cosimo Borrelli and Jason Aleksander Kardachi, the three compliance monitoring agencies, and the JGA North and South cable systems.

**Why it matters** It makes national-security mitigation obligations follow the proposed ownership and license-interest transfer rather than end with the original licensees, while documenting interagency consent relevant to the FCC assignment process.

- Letter dated February 12, 2025.
- National Security Agreements dated July 6, 2020.
