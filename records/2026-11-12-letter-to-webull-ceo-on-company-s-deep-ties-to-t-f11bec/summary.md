The House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party asks Webull CEO Anthony Denier to explain the company’s ownership, operational, data-privacy, market-liquidity, and regulatory-reporting ties to the People’s Republic of China (PRC), and to provide supporting records by December 31, 2024.

**What it does** The November 25, 2024 letter expresses concern that Webull Corporation and Webull Financial LLC remain closely connected to PRC entities, including Hunan Fumi Information Technology Co., Ltd. and Hunan Weibu Information Technology Co., Ltd. It identifies Wang Anquan as a central link among those entities and asks for documentation concerning PRC governmental influence, corporate relationships, data storage and protections, broker locations, liquidity assurances, and SEC Rule 606 order-routing disclosures. The letter also asks for internal audits, compliance materials, regulatory correspondence, system records, and information about possible reporting gaps.

**Who it affects** The direct recipient is Webull CEO Anthony Denier. The inquiry concerns Webull’s U.S. brokerage operations, employees and registered brokers, American customers and investors, and the company’s planned merger with SK Growth Opportunities Corp.

**Why it matters** The Committee states that PRC ownership and personnel ties could affect Webull’s operational independence, the security of U.S. customer data, the reliability and liquidity of brokerage services, and the fairness of order-flow reporting. It also invokes the Committee’s authority under H. Res. 11 to investigate the Chinese Communist Party’s economic, technological, and security competition with the United States.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter dated November 25, 2024; metadata date is December 6, 2024.
- Webull’s proposed SPAC transaction was valued at $7.3 billion and announced in February 2024.
- The letter contains six numbered requests, with multiple subparts under the Rule 606 request.
- Requested response deadline: December 31, 2024.
- Cited Massachusetts consent-order docket: E-2022-005.
