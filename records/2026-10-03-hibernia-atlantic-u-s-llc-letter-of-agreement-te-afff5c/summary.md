Hibernia NGS Limited and KCK Limited provide national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety commitments to U.S. agencies in connection with proposed transfers of control involving Hibernia Atlantic U.S. LLC and Hibernia Media, LLC.

**What it does** This Letter of Assurance continues the parties’ commitments under a September 30, 2010 letter and adds conditions for the Project Express undersea cable system. Hibernia must submit an Initial Equipment Manufacturer List within three business days, maintain a detailed Principal Equipment List, provide advance notice before deploying certain equipment, and notify the agencies after specified deployments. The agencies may identify concerns, with nonresponse within 30 calendar days treated as approval in the circumstances described. Hibernia also must provide information about relevant vendors, contractors, subcontractors, software, and new unaffiliated fiber-IRU holders.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind KCK and Hibernia in connection with the FCC transfer applications, and affect HB Atlantic’s international and domestic telecommunications authorities and submarine cable landing license. They govern equipment and operational information for Project Express, while recognizing limits concerning equipment chosen by IRU holders and customers.

**Why it matters** The LOA gives Team Telecom agencies an equipment-review and deployment-notification mechanism for a cable system that would interconnect with Hibernia Atlantic and carry traffic into the United States. A breach could support a request that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or render null and void relevant authorizations.

**Key dates and numbers**
- December 15, 2014: LOA date.
- ITC-T/C-20140820-00242: principal transfer-of-control application referenced.
- Related applications: ITC-T/C-20140820-00244 and SCL-T/C-20140820-00007.
- Initial manufacturer list: due within 3 business days.
- Agency review or response: generally within 30 calendar days; agencies state they will endeavor, if practicable, to review concerns within 20 days.
- Equipment deployment notice: at least 30 calendar days in specified cases; post-deployment notice within 30 days for specified manufacturers.
