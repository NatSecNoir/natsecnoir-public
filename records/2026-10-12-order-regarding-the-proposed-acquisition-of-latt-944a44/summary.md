The President prohibited the proposed acquisition of Lattice Semiconductor Corporation by China Venture Capital Fund Corporation Limited and related Canyon Bridge entities on national-security grounds.

**What it does** The order, issued under section 721 of the Defense Production Act of 1950, finds credible evidence that the Purchasers’ control of Lattice might threaten U.S. national security. It prohibits the proposed transaction and any substantially equivalent transaction carried out directly or indirectly, including through foreign beneficial owners or subsidiaries. The Purchasers and Lattice must fully and permanently abandon the transaction, certify completion to CFIUS, and avoid transactions or devices intended to circumvent the order. The Attorney General is authorized to enforce it.

**Who it affects** The order binds the Purchasers—Canyon Bridge Merger Sub, Canyon Bridge Acquisition Company, Canyon Bridge Capital Investment, Canyon Bridge Fund I, Yitai Capital, and their parent, China Venture Capital Fund Corporation Limited—as well as Lattice. It also assigns compliance and certification roles to CFIUS and directs the Secretary of the Treasury to transmit the order to the transaction parties.

**Why it matters** This is a presidential use of CFIUS-related authority to block a cross-border semiconductor acquisition rather than permit it subject to mitigation. It demonstrates that the executive branch could prohibit both the identified deal and substantially equivalent structures designed to evade the order.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Adopted: September 13, 2017; published September 18, 2017.
- Abandonment deadline: 30 days after the order, extendable by CFIUS for up to 90 days.
- Compliance reporting: weekly until termination certification.
- Authority: section 721, 50 U.S.C. 4565; citation: 82 FR 43665, FR Doc. 2017-20005.
