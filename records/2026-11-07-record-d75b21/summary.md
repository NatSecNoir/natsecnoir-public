The FCC announces that five foreign-produced device offerings received Department of War Conditional Approvals and are consequently exempt from the FCC Covered List: Percepto’s Air Max drone ecosystem, SERCOMM’s XF10 home gateway, ABB Robotics’ specified autonomous robots, Pantograph Industries’ Pandroid Robot Hardware, and Nextpower’s APCS-UL and APV-UL product families.

**What it does** The Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau updates the Covered List to exclude these identified devices, treating the Department of War determinations as “a specific determination” that the equipment does not pose unacceptable risks to U.S. national security. The exemptions require continuing compliance with each company’s approved onshoring plan and updated product vetting. The SERCOMM approval terminates on May 8, 2028; the notice states that approvals without termination dates remain effective indefinitely subject to their conditions.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects the five named manufacturers and their identified drone, router, robotic-device, and power-inverter products. It also provides guidance for equipment authorization applicants, test labs, and telecommunications certification bodies handling these products.

**Why it matters** The notice illustrates how device-specific executive-branch determinations can remove otherwise covered foreign-produced equipment from the FCC’s supply-chain restrictions while preserving compliance and enforcement conditions. It also updates the public record of Conditional Approvals across four device categories.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released November 6, 2026; DA 26-1203.
- Dockets: WC 18-89, ET 21-232, and EA 21-233.
- Five new Conditional Approvals: one UAS, one router, two advanced-robotics, and one power-inverter approval.
- SERCOMM’s approval runs through May 8, 2028.
