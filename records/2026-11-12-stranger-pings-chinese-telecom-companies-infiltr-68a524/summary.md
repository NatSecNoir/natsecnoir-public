The report finds that China Telecom, China Mobile, and China Unicom retained parent-controlled infrastructure, network relationships, and routing pathways in the United States after losing or being denied Section 214 authority, creating continuing national-security and communications risks.

**What it does** The report presents findings from subpoenaed records, sworn interviews, routing data, federal records, and network scans. It examines corporate ownership and governance, residual points of presence and colocation, Chinese-manufactured equipment, trusted connections to PRC parent networks, acceptable-use terms imposed on U.S. customers, and BGP routing activity. It recommends congressional action to expand FCC, Team Telecom, and Commerce ICTS authority; require entity-specific Covered List determinations; fund targeted removal or mitigation; strengthen international routing security; and mandate interim logging and monitoring.

**Who it affects** The principal subjects are the U.S. operations of China Telecom, China Mobile, and China Unicom. The report also discusses Qihoo 360, CloudRadium, Integrity Technology Group, Ruijie Networks, Huawei, ZTE, and China Electronics Technology Group, as well as U.S. carriers, data centers, technology companies, and users whose traffic may traverse the identified infrastructure.

**Why it matters** The report argues that license revocation or denial does not remove hardware, colocation, interconnection, peering, IP transit, routing identities, or remote operational access. It identifies 108,891 high-confidence BGP hijack events involving PRC- or Hong Kong-associated networks and at least 477 U.S. networks, while cautioning that the data does not establish that every event was deliberate espionage.

**Key dates and numbers**
- March 5, 2025: Select Committee investigation commenced with a bipartisan hearing.
- January 2018–May 31, 2025: principal BGP assessment period.
- June 2025: network scans identified approximately 6,000 Ruijie devices and at least 143 China Mobile assets in U.S. facilities.
- April 30, 2026 / May 1, 2026: FCC adopted and released the domestic telecommunications NPRM cited as WC Docket No. 26-82, FCC 26-29.
- August 4, 2026: report date.
