The Letter of Assurances requires Aqua Comms (Americas) Inc. to implement extensive national-security and law-enforcement safeguards for the U.S. operations of the AEConnect-1 submarine cable system, as a condition associated with its FCC cable-landing authorization.

**What it does** Aqua Comms Americas replaces a 2014 letter executed by America Europe Connect Licenses Limited and commits to DHS oversight of the cable’s domestic communications infrastructure, wet infrastructure, network operations centers, principal equipment, vendors, and personnel. The commitments include a U.S.-based security point of contact; production of network, equipment, vendor, and restoration information; the ability to interrupt U.S. traffic; advance notice and DHS review of equipment, vendor, testing, service, and operational changes; logical and physical security controls; personnel screening; incident reporting; annual compliance reports; third-party audits; and DHS consultation, site visits, and access to relevant records and facilities.

**Who it affects** The obligations bind Aqua Comms Americas and its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates, and cover personnel, contractors, vendors, and other parties with access to the cable or its principal equipment. DHS may raise objections, require remediation, and review proposed changes.

**Why it matters** The agreement gives the Committee a mitigation and enforcement framework for a U.S.-connected international telecommunications facility. A breach may support a DHS request that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify the relevant license or authorization.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed January 5, 2021; intended to supersede the October 1, 2014 LOA after FCC grant of a license-modification application.
- Cable license file: FCC File No. SCL-LIC-20140206-00002.
- Key deadlines include 45 days’ advance notice for many changes, 60 days for specified information and DHS decisions, 90 days for policies and training, and 48 hours for qualifying incident reports.
- Foreign ownership or control above 10 percent must be reported to DHS within 15 days.
