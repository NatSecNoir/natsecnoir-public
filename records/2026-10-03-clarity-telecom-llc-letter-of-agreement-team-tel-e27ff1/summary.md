The Letter of Agreement establishes national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety mitigation commitments by Clarity Telecom, LLC and NTS, Inc. in connection with proposed transfers of international Section 214 authorizations and related ownership transactions.

**What it does** The agreement requires Clarity and NTSI to maintain a DOJ-approved U.S.-based law-enforcement point of contact; comply with CALEA, court orders, and other lawful U.S. process; keep U.S. records and domestic communications protected from unauthorized foreign access; and submit cybersecurity, network-security, equipment, vendor, outsourcing, audit, peering, and annual compliance information to DOJ. It also requires advance notice or approval for specified equipment, service-provider, data-storage, ownership, and service changes, and permits DOJ to seek FCC license remedies for a breach.

**Who it affects** The commitments apply to Clarity, doing business as Vast Broadband, NTSI and its licensee subsidiaries—NTS-Comm, PRIDE, NTS-Tel, and XFone—and relevant contractors, managed network service providers, vendors, and offshore service providers. Team Telecom agencies and the FCC receive monitoring, review, objection, and enforcement roles.

**Why it matters** The LOA conditions Team Telecom’s non-objection to the FCC’s review of transactions that would place NTSI under Clarity and ultimately transfer control of Clarity’s parent to Python Intermediate II. It formalizes controls over lawful surveillance, sensitive customer and government information, foreign requests, network equipment, cybersecurity, and corporate changes.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed or dated August 16, 2019; applications concern WC Docket Nos. 19-7 and 19-16 and multiple IBFS Section 214 numbers.
- LEPOC personal information is due within 15 days; cybersecurity and network-security plans and the principal-equipment list are due within 60 days.
- Foreign requests for U.S. records must be referred to DOJ within five business days; certain breaches require third-party notice within 48 hours and company notice within 15 days.
- Peering and interconnecting-carrier lists are quarterly through July 2021, then annual; DOJ may request audits through fiscal year 2022.
