The House Select Committee on the CCP asks the Secretaries of State and Homeland Security to determine whether specified PRC officials and entities connected to Xinjiang abuses should face sanctions or UFLPA Entity List treatment by October 6, 2023.

**What it does** This September 19, 2023 letter requests rigorous enforcement of the Uyghur Human Rights Policy Act (UHRPA) and Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act (UFLPA). It asks the recipients to assess 13 senior officials of the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region for sanctions eligibility, evaluate 25 entities for placement on the Department of Homeland Security’s UFLPA Entity List, and review whether entities already on that list meet UFLPA sanctions criteria. The committee requests an explanation if any official or entity is found not to qualify.

**Who it affects** The request is directed to Secretary Antony Blinken and Secretary Alejandro Mayorkas. It concerns named PRC and Xinjiang officials, companies and government bodies allegedly associated with human-rights abuses or Uyghur forced labor, as well as entities already appearing on the UFLPA Entity List.

**Why it matters** The letter reflects congressional oversight of executive enforcement against alleged genocide and forced labor in Xinjiang. It seeks additional accountability under existing sanctions and supply-chain authorities while pressing the administrations to explain any decision not to act.

**Key dates and numbers**
- September 19, 2023: letter date.
- October 6, 2023: requested response deadline.
- 13: senior PRC officials identified for sanctions review.
- 25: additional entities identified for UFLPA Entity List review.
- June 2020 and December 2021: enactment dates cited for UHRPA and UFLPA.
