The Letter of Agreement makes Telxius’s proposed transfer of negative control rights from KKR Management, LLP to Pontegadea Inversiones, S.L. subject to extensive national-security and law-enforcement mitigation commitments, clearing the path for FCC approval of the related Section 214 applications.

**What it does** The agreement requires Telxius to protect U.S. Records, domestic communications, and domestic communications infrastructure; maintain a U.S. law-enforcement point of contact; support lawful process and CALEA compliance; screen personnel; restrict foreign access and disclosure; and maintain cybersecurity and system-security plans. It also imposes notice, review, and approval procedures for principal equipment, vendors, outsourced or offshore services, network operations center locations, ownership and service changes, and storage locations. Telxius must report specified security incidents within 48 hours, provide annual compliance reports, permit site visits, and undergo a possible annual third-party audit.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind Telxius Cable USA and Telxius Cable Puerto Rico and give the Justice Department, including the FBI, continuing oversight. They also cover relevant employees, foreign personnel, contractors, managed network providers, equipment suppliers, and service providers.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for an international telecommunications authorization transfer, combining data-access controls, supply-chain visibility, cybersecurity obligations, and enforcement leverage. A breach or unresolved national-security concern may support a recommendation that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, or nullify relevant authorizations.

**Key dates and numbers**
- December 9, 2022: LOA date.
- 30 days after FCC approval: provide the LEPOC and CALEA certification; many notices and submissions use 30-day periods.
- 48 hours: report known or suspected security incidents or material breaches after discovery.
- 90 days after FCC approval, then quarterly: provide updated lists of foreign persons with relevant access.
- One year after FCC approval and annually thereafter: submit the compliance report.
- Related authorizations: FCC IBFS Nos. ITC-214-20080709-00314 and ITC-214-20040518-00203.
