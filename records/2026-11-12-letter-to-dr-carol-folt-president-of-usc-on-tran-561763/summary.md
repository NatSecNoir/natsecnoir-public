The House Select Committee on the CCP requests that the University of Southern California provide information about Chinese national students, their funding and research participation, and university safeguards for sensitive and federally funded work by April 1, 2025.

**What it does** The March 18, 2025 letter from Chairman John Moolenaar to USC President Carol Folt frames Chinese nationals’ participation in advanced STEM programs as a potential national-security and technology-transfer risk. It seeks records on students’ prior universities and research affiliations, tuition and program funding, fields of study, laboratories, research initiatives, and country-by-country admissions and enrollment. It also asks about policies governing foreign-national access to federally funded or export-controlled research, monitoring of military or dual-use work, faculty collaborations with China-based institutions, talent-program disclosures, and graduates’ post-study destinations.

**Who it affects** The direct recipient is USC. The requested information concerns Chinese national undergraduate, graduate, and PhD students; university faculty and laboratories; federally funded research involving agencies including DARPA, DoD, DOE, and NSF; and collaborations with Chinese institutions and companies such as Huawei, the Chinese Academy of Sciences, CETC, and AVIC.

**Why it matters** The letter presents university enrollment, research access, and foreign collaboration as interconnected issues involving espionage, intellectual-property theft, workforce displacement, and transfer of dual-use capabilities in AI, quantum computing, semiconductors, robotics, and aerospace. It illustrates congressional oversight focused on institutional transparency and controls around foreign participation in sensitive research.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letter dated March 18, 2025; response requested no later than April 1, 2025.
- Six numbered requests for information and 14 additional questions.
- Cites House Resolution 5, section 4(a), as the committee’s authority.
