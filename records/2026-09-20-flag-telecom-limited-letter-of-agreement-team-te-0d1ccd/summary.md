The Letter of Agreement establishes national-security and law-enforcement mitigation commitments that GCX Holdings Limited and related parties must undertake in connection with Reef Bidco Limited’s proposed acquisition of control of GCX telecommunications authorizations and companies.

**What it does** This agreement with the U.S. Department of Justice, including the FBI, governs access to U.S. records, domestic communications, and domestic communications infrastructure. It requires a U.S.-based law-enforcement point of contact and security officer; compliance with lawful U.S. process and CALEA; restrictions on foreign access and disclosure; personnel screening; cybersecurity and system-security plans; incident reporting; controls over principal equipment, network operations centers, outsourced and offshore providers, ownership, and new services; annual reporting; audits; and DOJ site visits. It supersedes the November 25, 2020 Letter of Assurances upon FCC approval.

**Who it affects** The binding parties are GCX Holdings, Vanco US, Vanco Solutions, Reliance Globalcom Services, and 3i Infrastructure. The commitments also affect their subsidiaries, vendors, foreign personnel, outsourced service providers, network equipment suppliers, and successors, with DOJ oversight and potential FCC enforcement consequences.

**Why it matters** The LOA conditions the proposed Section 214 transfer on continuing safeguards for U.S. customer and communications data and on DOJ review of operational, ownership, supply-chain, and cybersecurity changes. Breach or unresolved national-security concerns may support FCC action against relevant authorizations.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed/released: July 18, 2022.
- FCC approval triggers most deadlines; many notices and submissions are due in 15, 30, or 60 days.
- Security incidents and material breaches must generally be reported within 48 hours; CPNI incidents must be reported within seven business days.
- Annual reports are due one year after FCC approval and every year thereafter.
- Docket and file numbers: TT 21-086 to -090; WC 21-463; ITC-T/C-20211124-00181; ITC-T/C-20211130-00182; ITC-T/C-20211130-00183.
