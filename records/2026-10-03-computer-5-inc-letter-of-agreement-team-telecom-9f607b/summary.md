The Letter of Agreement requires Northwest Fiber to implement national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety safeguards as a condition of Team Telecom’s non-objection to the proposed transfer of Frontier telecommunications licenses and authorizations.

**What it does** This agreement governs Northwest Fiber’s handling of U.S. Records, Domestic Communications, customer information, lawful U.S. process, network infrastructure, personnel, and foreign access. It requires compliance with U.S. legal process; U.S.-based handling and protection of responsive records; restrictions on disclosure to foreign governments and unauthorized foreign persons; a U.S. law-enforcement point of contact; personnel screening; network security and NIST-compliant cybersecurity plans; incident and breach reporting; and advance notice or review of principal equipment, outsourced or offshore providers, foreign storage, ownership changes, and service changes. USDOJ may conduct on-site compliance reviews and seek FCC action for material noncompliance.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind Northwest Fiber and address the proposed acquisition and control transfer from Frontier, including the identified Frontier operating companies and long-distance customer assignments. They also cover relevant vendors, managed network service providers, outsourced or offshore providers, personnel, and foreign persons with access to protected information or infrastructure.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for a telecommunications transaction involving foreign investment and sensitive U.S. communications data. It places operational controls and continuing reporting obligations on the transferee while preserving USDOJ oversight and potential FCC license remedies.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated November 21, 2019; the agreement defines its operative date as the date Northwest Fiber executes it.
- Initial deadlines include 15 days for LEPOC information, 60 days for the personnel-screening description, and 90 days for updated security plans and equipment/provider lists.
- Third-party breach or loss notices are due within 48 hours of discovery; Northwest Fiber’s incident notice is generally due within 15 days, and certain unauthorized-access reports to the FBI and Secret Service within 7 days.
- Advance notice periods are generally 30 days; annual reports begin one year after execution.
