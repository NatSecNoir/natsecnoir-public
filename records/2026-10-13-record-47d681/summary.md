The FCC’s Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau announces that specified GE Grid power inverters are conditionally approved and therefore exempt from the FCC Covered List.

**What it does** The Public Notice implements a Department of War National Security Determination received October 8, 2026, granting Conditional Approval for GE Grid Solutions, LLC’s FLEXINVERTER 1.5kV Solar version, 1.5kV BESS version, 2KV Solar version, and SiC Variant power inverters. PSHSB concludes that this approval is a “specific determination” that the devices do not pose the covered national-security risks and updates the Covered List and Conditional Approvals appendix accordingly. The exemption depends on GE Grid’s continuing compliance with its onshoring plan and updated product vetting.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects GE Grid and users, applicants, test laboratories, and telecommunications certification bodies handling the identified power inverters. It also clarifies the treatment of foreign-produced power inverters, which otherwise remain on the Covered List unless granted Conditional Approval by the Department of War or Department of Homeland Security.

**Why it matters** The notice narrows the practical scope of the Covered List while preserving the broader listing of foreign-produced power inverters. It illustrates how an executive-branch national-security determination can create a product-specific exemption and how PSHSB maintains the list under 47 CFR §§ 1.50002(a) and 1.50003.

**Key dates and numbers**
- October 9, 2026: Public Notice released and Covered List updated.
- July 28, 2026: Foreign-produced power inverters were added to the Covered List.
- August 19–20, 2026: A further National Security Determination was received and implemented through a Covered List update.
- October 8, 2026: Department of War granted the Conditional Approval.
