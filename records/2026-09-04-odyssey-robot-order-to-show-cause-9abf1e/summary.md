The FCC’s Office of Engineering and Technology (OET) and Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau (PSHSB) initiate a proceeding requiring Odyssey Robot LLC to show cause why the Commission should not revoke two equipment authorizations that the bureaus tentatively find were obtained through false representations concerning Covered List equipment.

**What it does** The Order to Show Cause concerns FCC ID 2BSYT-FMAWZOD, an uncrewed aircraft system (UAS), and FCC ID 2BSYT-YMAWZOD, its remote controller. OET and PSHSB tentatively determine that both devices are Covered UAS or UAS critical components produced in a foreign country and therefore were improperly authorized. The order alleges that Odyssey certified the equipment was not prohibited covered equipment and submitted supporting materials representing that the devices were developed, designed, and manufactured in California and assembled by eTak in Texas. eTak later denied any relationship with Odyssey or any assembly work. The order proceeds under section 2.939(d) of the FCC rules.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects Odyssey and its two equipment authorizations. It also addresses eTak’s role in Odyssey’s representations and follows FCC inquiries into Odyssey’s business structure, manufacturing, affiliations, and compliance practices. The order gives Odyssey notice and an opportunity to respond; it does not itself make a final revocation determination.

**Why it matters** The proceeding illustrates the FCC’s enforcement of prohibitions on authorizing Covered List equipment and its reliance on applicant attestations. Failure to respond, or failure to provide a reasonable basis against revocation, may result in cancellation of the authorizations.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Adopted and released July 21, 2026; DA 26-746.
- ET Docket No. 26-186.
- Odyssey received certification grants on March 24, 2026, and April 20, 2026.
- Odyssey must file a written response within 10 calendar days of release.
- The FCC’s Covered List addition for foreign-produced UAS and UAS critical components was made December 22, 2025.
