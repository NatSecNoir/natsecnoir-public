The letter asks the Department of the Treasury and the Internal Revenue Service to investigate whether CCP-linked nonprofit networks misused tax-exempt organizations and donor-advised funds to influence U.S. debates over artificial-intelligence infrastructure.

**What it does** The House Select Committee on the CCP and the House Committee on Ways and Means describe alleged Chinese influence operations involving nonprofits associated with Neville Roy Singham, the Party for Socialism and Liberation, and related organizations. They request scrutiny of whether section 501(c)(3) entities operated for the private or political benefit of foreign-aligned interests, and whether intermediary entities or donor-advised funds concealed the source of foreign-directed money. The letter cites reports concerning online campaigns against U.S. data-center development, including OpenAI’s “Data Center Bandwagon” and “Tech and Tariffs” campaigns.

**Who it affects** The requested investigation would concern Treasury, the IRS, and the named nonprofit network, as well as organizations and individuals identified as connected to it. It also bears on U.S. policymakers and communities debating data centers and AI infrastructure.

**Why it matters** The letter frames alleged nonprofit funding and coordinated online advocacy as a national-security and tax-administration concern, arguing that charitable vehicles should not subsidize foreign-backed political influence or disinformation affecting American technology development.

- February 10, 2026: Ways and Means hearing on foreign influence in tax-exempt organizations.
- June 10, 2026: OpenAI report on PRC-linked influence operations targeting AI debates.
- August 2026: X reported roughly 200 suspected Chinese botfarm accounts.
- The letter cites $278 million moved into American advocacy groups and nearly $24 billion in proposed data-center investments allegedly delayed, blocked, or scaled back.
