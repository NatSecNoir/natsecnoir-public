The Letter of Assurances sets out Telxius Cable USA, Inc.’s commitments to DHS and DoD to address national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety concerns associated with the South America-1 (SAm-1) submarine cable system, in connection with the pending FCC authorization.

**What it does** The LOA requires Telxius, upon grant of FCC authority, to designate U.S.-resident citizen security points of contact; provide network, equipment, vendor, maintenance, and restoration information; and maintain operational capability to interrupt U.S.-bound or U.S.-originating traffic. It also requires advance notice of material equipment modifications, vendor or contractor changes, and significant service or operational changes. Telxius must implement NIST-compliant cybersecurity, physical-security and personnel-screening policies, report specified incidents and breaches, train relevant personnel, submit annual compliance reports, and permit reasonable on-site compliance reviews.

**Who it affects** The commitments apply to Telxius, its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, affiliates, contractors, vendors, and personnel involved in operating or securing the SAm-1 system. The USG Parties are DHS and DoD; the FCC is the licensing authority whose authorization would be conditioned on compliance.

**Why it matters** The agreement gives the U.S. government visibility into and influence over a transnational submarine cable’s domestic infrastructure, management, vendors, personnel, and control changes. A breach could support a request that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify relevant authorization.

**Key dates and numbers**
- October 2, 2017: LOA date.
- FCC File No. SCL-LIC-20000204-00003; related docket SCL-T/C-20170328-00008.
- Initial submissions are generally due within 60 business days of execution; requested system information is due within 15 business days.
- Notice periods include 10 business days for material equipment changes, 30 business days for vendor or significant operational changes, and 10 business days for specified ownership changes or incident reports.
- Foreign ownership or control above 10% triggers the change-in-control notice obligation.
