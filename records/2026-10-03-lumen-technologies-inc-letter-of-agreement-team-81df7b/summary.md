CenturyLink commits to extensive national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety safeguards as a condition supporting FCC authorization of its proposed acquisition of Level 3 and transfer of related licenses and authorizations.

**What it does** The Letter of Assurances (LOA) requires CenturyLink to maintain U.S.-based authority and capabilities for lawful interception and compliance with U.S. legal process; protect U.S. records and domestic communications from unauthorized foreign-government access; designate a U.S. security officer and 24/7 law-enforcement points of contact; provide detailed information about domestic communications infrastructure, undersea cable systems, principal equipment, vendors, and outsourcing; and report incidents, breaches, ownership changes, and annual compliance information. It also provides for USG consultation, on-site visits, and possible FCC license remedies for breach.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind CenturyLink and its successors, subsidiaries, and affiliates, covering infrastructure and cable systems acquired from or associated with Level 3. They support review by DHS, DOJ, and DoD, collectively identified as the USG Parties, in connection with pending FCC applications.

**Why it matters** The LOA places operational control, equipment oversight, foreign-access restrictions, and compliance reporting at the center of Team Telecom mitigation for a major communications-carrier transaction. It also establishes ownership-based changed-circumstances and termination mechanisms tied to STT Crossing and other foreign-controlled entities.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed September 29, 2017; obligations generally begin when the merger closes.
- Security officer and law-enforcement POCs are due within 30 business days of execution.
- Annual compliance reports are due on each execution anniversary.
- Termination may occur 30 calendar days after specified foreign-ownership thresholds fall below 10 percent, absent a USG objection.
- The related FCC filings include WC Docket No. 16-403 and ten international/ submarine-cable transfer file numbers.
