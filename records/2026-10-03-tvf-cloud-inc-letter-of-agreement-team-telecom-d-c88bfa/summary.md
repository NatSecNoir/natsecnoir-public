TvF Cloud, Inc. undertakes binding national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety commitments to the U.S. Department of Justice in connection with its Section 214 application before the FCC.

**What it does** This Letter of Agreement requires TvF to comply with CALEA and lawful U.S. process, prevent services from thwarting authorized surveillance, and refer non-U.S. government requests for U.S. Records or Domestic Communications to DOJ for approval or seek a U.S. court ruling. It requires DOJ-approved U.S. points of contact, U.S. access to records, advance notice and approval for certain foreign personnel and offshore providers, disclosure of principal equipment and service changes, security planning, and annual compliance reporting.

**Who it affects** The obligations apply to TvF Cloud, its successors, personnel, contractors, network and managed-service providers, foreign partners, and vendors whose activities could expose U.S. communications infrastructure or customer information. DOJ’s National Security Division and Team Telecom staff receive notices, plans, reports, and approval requests; the FCC application concerns authority for global or limited-global facilities-based and resale services under Section 214.

**Why it matters** The agreement conditions the applicant’s telecommunications operations on controls over access to U.S. customer and communications data, foreign involvement, network operations, and lawful-intercept capability. It also centralizes review of foreign-government requests and creates continuing oversight through advance notices, DOJ approvals, and annual certifications.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated and executed by TvF on November 20, 2017; the DOJ signature date is not filled in.
- FCC application: ITC-214-20161219-00353, under Section 214 and 47 C.F.R. § 63.18(e)(1)–(2).
- TvF must submit its Network and Systems Security Plan within 75 days of the LOA.
- Several notices and designations are due within 30 days; foreign personnel access requires 15 days’ advance notice.
- Annual reports are due each December 1, beginning December 1, 2018.
- U.S. Records are to be stored at Ragingwire facilities in Ashburn, Virginia, and Sacramento, California.
