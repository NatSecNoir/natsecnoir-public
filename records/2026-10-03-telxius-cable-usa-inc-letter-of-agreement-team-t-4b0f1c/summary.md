The Committee for the Assessment of Foreign Participation in the United States Telecommunications Services Sector requires Telxius, LATAM, and PRTC to implement national-security and law-enforcement safeguards as a condition of the FCC license for the TIKAL-AMX3 submarine cable system.

**What it does** This National Security Agreement establishes compliance officers, security policies, personnel screening, physical and logical access controls, incident reporting, supply-chain oversight, and annual reporting. The licensees must disclose covered third parties, principal equipment, service providers, foreign personnel with unescorted access, and relevant ownership or control changes. The Compliance Monitoring Agencies may object to proposed people, equipment, providers, and policies; inspect facilities; require audits; and seek FCC modification, revocation, or other relief for a breach. The agreement also requires the licensees to maintain the ability to interrupt U.S.-bound or U.S.-originating traffic.

**Who it affects** The primary obligations apply to Telxius Cable USA, Telxius Cable América, LATAM Telecommunications, and Puerto Rico Telephone Company, as well as their affiliates, subsidiaries, contractors, service providers, equipment vendors, and other covered third parties connected to TIKAL-AMX3.

**Why it matters** The agreement shows Team Telecom mitigation for a submarine cable linking the United States, Guatemala, and Mexico. It operationalizes scrutiny of foreign access, sensitive U.S. records, cable infrastructure, and supply-chain risks, including equipment associated with the FCC Covered List or Commerce Entity List.

**Key dates and numbers**
- FCC referral to the Committee: May 17, 2024; FCC File No. SCL-LIC-20240105-00001 and TT #24-020.
- Signatures: May 8, 2025 for the licensees; May 13–14, 2025 for the federal agencies; the NSA becomes effective on the last signature date.
- Initial security, equipment, service-provider, and covered-third-party submissions are generally due within 90 days of FCC approval.
- Security incidents must be reported within 48 hours; service-provider breaches within 72 hours.
