The House Select Committee on the CCP requests detailed information from five major semiconductor manufacturing equipment manufacturers about their China-related sales, exports, customers, compliance, manufacturing, and offshoring plans.

**What it does** The November 7, 2024 letters to KLA Corporation, Applied Materials, Lam Research, Tokyo Electron, and ASML ask each company to report PRC revenue for FY2022, FY2023, and year-to-date FY2024, including revenue tied to U.S. export licenses, listed or affiliated PRC entities, PRC government ownership, and distributors. They also request U.S. export-license applications submitted to the Commerce Department’s Bureau of Industry and Security since January 1, 2021; annual shipment volumes by equipment model and the most sophisticated technology node; the company’s top 30 PRC customers; China-based export-compliance staffing and advisers; geographic manufacturing expenses and shipment origins; and plans for new or expanded offshoring. The companies are additionally asked to make representatives available to committee staff.

**Who it affects** The requests directly affect KLA Corporation, Applied Materials, Lam Research, Tokyo Electron, and ASML, and concern their subsidiaries, distributors, customers, employees, and transactions involving PRC entities. The requested disclosures encompass connections to entities on the BIS Entity List, Treasury NS-CMIC List, and DoD 1260H List, as well as transactions subject to U.S. export licensing.

**Why it matters** The committee frames semiconductor manufacturing equipment exports to the PRC as relevant to China’s semiconductor self-sufficiency, military and technological capabilities, and U.S. and allied national and economic security. The letters seek company-level data to inform congressional oversight of export controls and the semiconductor supply chain while arguing that stronger controls need not undermine a robust equipment industry.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Letters dated November 7, 2024; responses requested by December 1, 2024.
- Requested export-license application data covers January 1, 2021, through the request date.
- Revenue requested for FY2022, FY2023, and YTD FY2024.
- Companies must identify their top 30 PRC customers and, when applicable, top five PRC distributors.
- Government-affiliation reporting uses a threshold of 50.1% or more ultimate ownership.
