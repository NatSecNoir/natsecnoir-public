The House Select Committee on the Strategic Competition Between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party requests a classified FBI briefing on TikTok and ByteDance’s impact on American security by December 22, 2023.

**What it does** The letter asks FBI Director Christopher Wray to brief the Committee on the FBI and Department of Justice investigation of ByteDance and TikTok, the Chinese Communist Party’s ability to surveil Americans, and steps being taken to protect sensitive U.S. citizen data. It cites reports that Chinese regulators inspected ByteDance’s Feishu workplace collaboration platform, which stores TikTok employee documents, chats, meetings, calendars, and other business records. The letter also references allegations that ByteDance employees tracked American journalists and accessed their IP addresses and TikTok user data.

**Who it affects** The request concerns TikTok and its parent company ByteDance, U.S. TikTok users and employees, American journalists, and federal law-enforcement agencies investigating the company. The signatories are members of the House Select Committee, led by Chairman Mike Gallagher and Ranking Member Raja Krishnamoorthi.

**Why it matters** The Committee frames ByteDance’s ownership and China’s legal and regulatory authorities as national-security and privacy risks, including possible access to American data, surveillance, and algorithmic influence or censorship. The letter seeks additional classified information to inform congressional oversight; it does not itself impose a restriction or make a binding agency determination.

**Key dates and numbers**
- December 7, 2023: Letter date.
- December 22, 2023: Requested deadline for the classified briefing.
- October 2022: Reported Chinese regulatory review and on-site inspection involving Feishu.
- November 2023: Reports cited by the Committee concerning access to TikTok’s backend system.
- 20th: Chinese Communist Party National Congress referenced in connection with the reported inspection.
