The report finds that the unlicensed Reedley Biolab exposed serious public-health and national-security vulnerabilities, while the federal response failed to determine the contents and risks of many biological samples.

**What it does** This congressional investigation documents the discovery, inspection, and abatement of a warehouse laboratory at 850 I Street in Reedley, California. It describes thousands of vials, including labeled, coded, and unlabeled materials; laboratory equipment; hazardous chemicals; faulty medical devices; and approximately 1,000 transgenic mice. The report examines the facility’s operation through Universal Meditech Incorporated and Prestige Biotech Incorporated, attributes control to Jiabei “Jesse” Zhu, who used the alias “David He,” and reviews alleged counterfeit medical-device sales, suspected pathogen importation, corporate ties, and unexplained payments from PRC banks. It also criticizes the CDC for relying on labels and declining to test samples.

**Who it affects** The findings concern Reedley residents, local and state officials, first responders, laboratory workers, federal agencies, and entities able to acquire pathogens or conduct private pathogenic research outside existing Select Agent oversight.

**Why it matters** The report argues that coded and unlabeled samples left the actual biological threat unknowable and identifies gaps in monitoring pathogen purchases, privately funded research, importation, and federal coordination. It calls on Congress and the Executive Branch to strengthen biosafety safeguards.

**Key dates and numbers**
- December 2022: local officials discovered the facility; March 16, 2023: inspection warrant executed.
- May 2–4, 2023: CDC onsite support; it identified at least 20 potentially infectious agents based on labels, without testing samples.
- July 5–7 and July 28, 2023: abatement actions; approximately 103.73 tons of general waste and 448 gallons of medical and biological waste were removed.
- October 19, 2023: federal agents arrested Zhu on fraud-related medical-device charges; the report was dated November 15, 2023.
