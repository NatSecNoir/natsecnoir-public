The Letter of Agreement binds Telroaming Advanced Communication Solution Ltd. to extensive national-security and law-enforcement mitigation commitments as a condition associated with its application for international Section 214 resale authority.

**What it does** The agreement establishes controls over Telroaming’s U.S. communications, infrastructure, records, lawful-process handling, personnel, foreign access, principal equipment, service providers, network operations centers, storage locations, and ownership or service changes. Telroaming must maintain U.S.-based, U.S.-citizen law-enforcement and security contacts; submit screening, cybersecurity, and system-security plans; obtain U.S. Department of Justice non-objection for specified personnel, equipment, providers, access countries, and storage arrangements; and file annual compliance reports. It also requires prompt reporting of security incidents, breaches, certain violations, and unauthorized access, generally within 72 hours.

**Who it affects** The primary regulated party is Telroaming, doing business as Webbing. The obligations apply to its affiliates, contractors, service providers, equipment vendors, foreign personnel with access, and operators of facilities or systems supporting U.S. services. The Committee and DOJ receive monitoring, approval, audit, site-visit, and enforcement authority, while the FCC remains the licensing authority.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for a foreign-owned or foreign-participated telecommunications applicant. It links operational compliance to continued FCC authorization: unresolved national-security concerns or breaches may prompt recommendations to modify, condition, revoke, or terminate the relevant authorization. It also specifically requires disclosure and review of equipment or providers associated with the FCC Covered List, Commerce Entity List, or foreign-adversary supply chains.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Signed July 14, 2025, by CEO Noam Lando and VP Finance Oren Miron.
- FCC application: ITC-214-20240522-00090; Team Telecom reference TT 24-038.
- Many initial submissions are due 15, 30, or 60 days after the Date of FCC Approval; annual reporting begins one year after approval.
- Security incidents and specified violations generally must be reported within 72 hours.
