Vocus Group Limited agrees to national-security and law-enforcement mitigation measures, allowing the Committee to notify the FCC that it has no objection to grant of the relevant application.

**What it does** This Letter of Agreement (LOA) establishes continuing obligations for Vocus, including notice of ownership, corporate-structure, and corporate-name changes; reporting concerning access, service providers, foreign persons, security incidents, and compliance; and cooperation with the Committee and its agencies. It also permits disclosure of confidential FCC submissions to specified federal departments and offices. The agreement provides that approval or non-objection periods are tolled during certain federal closures or lapses in appropriations, and that the LOA may be terminated if the mitigated risks no longer warrant it, without eliminating liability for earlier breaches.

**Who it affects** The obligations primarily bind Vocus Group Limited and apply to its corporate structure, non-U.S. owners, personnel and service providers with relevant access, and FCC-authorized subsidiaries. The FCC, the Department of Justice, the FBI, and the Committee’s other participating agencies are the principal government stakeholders.

**Why it matters** The LOA is the mitigation instrument supporting Team Telecom clearance of an FCC application. It preserves continuing oversight and the possibility of later license review even after termination if new or additional risks arise.
