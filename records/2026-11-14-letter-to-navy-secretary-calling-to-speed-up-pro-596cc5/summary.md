The House Select Committee on the CCP and a House Foreign Affairs subcommittee leadership office press the Secretary of the Navy to accelerate contracting, production, and delivery of Harpoon and SLAM-ER missiles and related systems to Taiwan.

**What it does** The letter describes delays in fulfilling U.S. arms sales to Taiwan and requests written answers by November 8, 2023. It asks when delivery of 400 ground-launched Harpoon missiles, 100 Harpoon Coastal Defense System launch systems and radar units will be completed; seeks the status and award timelines for production contracts covering 60 air-launched Harpoon missiles and 135 SLAM-ER missiles; and asks what steps, including increased production, multiyear procurement, or alternatives to the standard Foreign Military Sales process, could speed delivery. The signatories also request information about the risk that inflation and manufacturing costs will exceed Taiwan’s Letters of Acceptance budgets.

**Who it affects** The request is directed to Navy Secretary Carlos Del Toro and concerns the Department of the Navy, the Department of Defense, Taiwan, defense contractors, and U.S. officials responsible for foreign military sales and Indo-Pacific security.

**Why it matters** The letter links delivery delays to congressional concerns about deterring a possible Chinese attack on Taiwan. It argues that delays could postpone delivery beyond the urgency described by U.S. military and intelligence leaders and could require additional funding or further contract changes.

**Key dates and numbers**
- October 2020: announced sales included 400 ground-launched Harpoons, 100 HCDS systems and radar units, and 135 SLAM-ER missiles.
- December 2022: Taiwan signed Letters of Acceptance for 60 air-launched Harpoons and 135 SLAM-ERs.
- November 8, 2023: requested deadline for written responses.
- Section 5508(b) of the FY2023 NDAA required reporting on delayed Taiwan arms sales.
