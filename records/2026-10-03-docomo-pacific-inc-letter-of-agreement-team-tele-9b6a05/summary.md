DOCOMO Pacific, Inc. commits to mitigation measures requested by the Department of Homeland Security in connection with its FCC application to land and operate the Atisa private submarine cable system linking Guam with Saipan, Rota, and Tinian.

**What it does** This Letter of Assurances establishes DOCOMO’s obligations if the FCC grants the requested authorization. DOCOMO must designate U.S.-citizen security points of contact subject to DHS review, provide and update detailed lists of principal equipment and related vendors, give advance notice of material equipment or vendor changes, and submit annual compliance reports. It must also report qualifying changes in foreign ownership or control, unauthorized access or disruption, and significant changes to services or cable operations. DHS may consult with DOCOMO, negotiate over security concerns, and conduct reasonable-notice on-site reviews of domestic communications infrastructure. A breach may lead DHS to ask the FCC to modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify the relevant authorization.

**Who it affects** The commitments bind DOCOMO, its successors, assigns, subsidiaries, and affiliates, and concern the Atisa Cable System’s domestic and undersea infrastructure, vendors, contractors, subcontractors, network operations centers, and cable landing operations. DHS receives reporting, review, and visitation rights; the FCC is the licensing authority.

**Why it matters** The LOA makes national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety safeguards a condition associated with DOCOMO’s submarine-cable license application, while preserving DHS oversight of equipment, ownership, access, and operational changes.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Dated February 15, 2017; execution triggers the stated deadlines.
- Security POCs and the initial principal-equipment list are due within 60 business days of execution.
- Advance notice periods are generally 10 business days for material equipment changes and 30 business days for vendor or significant operational changes.
- Foreign ownership or control above 10 percent must be reported within 10 business days; annual reports are due on each execution anniversary.
