The House Select Committee on the Strategic Competition between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party finds that Webull’s U.S. brokerage operations remain structurally tied to PRC-linked ownership, personnel, technology, data infrastructure, and government-supported affiliates, creating national-security, privacy, compliance, and custody risks for American investors.

**What it does** The report presents six findings concerning Webull’s exposure of customer data to PRC legal and surveillance risk; allegedly inaccurate representations about employee locations and supervision; PRC-linked governance and ownership; weak Rule 606 and other regulatory controls; government-supported expansion by Hunan Weibu and Fumi Technology; and the reduced independence of Webull’s clearing and custody arrangements. It recommends codifying an SEC cross-border task force, requiring inspectable broker-dealer operations, restricting foreign-adversary access to brokerage data, regulating conflicted clearing and self-custody, and bringing certain foreign-controlled broker-dealers within CFIUS jurisdiction.

**Who it affects** The principal subject is Webull and its U.S. customers, especially users whose personal information, trading records, funds, and securities depend on Webull’s technology, compliance, clearing, and custody systems. The recommendations would also affect SEC-registered broker-dealers, clearing firms, FINRA, the SEC, Congress, and foreign-controlled financial platforms.

**Why it matters** The Committee argues that U.S. storage and registration do not eliminate risk when PRC-based affiliates and personnel support backend systems. It also highlights Webull’s October 2025 shift to directly carrying customer cash after a 2024 SEC action concerning deficient suspicious-activity reports, and identifies potential conflicts involving Apex and PEAK6.

**Key dates and numbers**
- October 2025: Webull completed its omnibus clearing migration and began directly carrying retail customer cash.
- November 22, 2024: the SEC imposed a $125,000 civil penalty over deficient suspicious-activity reports.
- December 31, 2025: Hunan Weibu had 863 employees, 62% of Webull’s global workforce.
- April 2026: FINRA approved Webull’s application for self and correspondent clearing.
- Webull reported more than 26 million global registered users and $24.6 billion in customer assets as of FY2025.
