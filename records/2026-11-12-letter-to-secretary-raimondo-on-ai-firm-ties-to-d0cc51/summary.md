The House Select Committee on the CCP asked Commerce Secretary Gina Raimondo to have the Bureau of Industry and Security investigate G42, its leadership, and affiliated companies for possible export-control risks and assess whether specified affiliates warrant placement on the BIS Entity List.

**What it does** This January 3, 2024 letter alleges that Emirati AI company G42 and CEO Peng Xiao maintain extensive commercial, research, and corporate relationships with PRC military, intelligence, state-owned, and surveillance-linked entities. It identifies connections involving DarkMatter, Pegasus Technology (Beijing), Huawei, Beijing Genomics Institute (BGI), Petuum, Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, and the Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence. The Committee asks BIS to investigate possible diversion of controlled hardware, software, cloud-computing services, research, and technology, and lists G42 affiliates for possible Entity List designation. It also requests an explanation if BIS determines that any listed entity does not warrant inclusion.

**Who it affects** The request targets G42, Peng Xiao, and the named G42 subsidiaries and affiliates, while implicating U.S. technology companies, universities, researchers, and export-controlled products and research. The letter is directed to Raimondo and does not itself impose an export restriction or make a BIS listing.

**Why it matters** The letter illustrates congressional scrutiny of AI supply chains and alleged military-civil-fusion, surveillance, human-rights, and technology-transfer risks involving a UAE-based company and PRC-connected partners. It presses Commerce to use Entity List and investigative authorities where existing controls on counterparties such as Huawei and BGI may be bypassed through commercial or research relationships.

**Key dates and numbers**
- January 3, 2024: letter date shown in the document.
- February 2, 2024: requested deadline for an explanation if an entity is not placed on the Entity List.
- March 2023: BIS reportedly added BGI subsidiaries to the Entity List, as cited by the letter.
- H.Res. 11: authority cited for the Select Committee’s investigative and policy-recommendation role.
- More than 30 foreign-trained scientists: number the letter cites in connection with BIGAI recruitment.
