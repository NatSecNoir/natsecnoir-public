The House Select Committee on the CCP urged Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to expedite the long-delayed DFARS rule implementing Section 847 of the Fiscal Year 2020 NDAA and to brief congressional staff on the Department of Defense’s actions and timeline.

**What it does** The June 16, 2025 letter argues that Section 847 requires expanded foreign ownership, control, or influence (FOCI) vetting and mitigation for defense contractors, including unclassified contracts exceeding $5 million. It asks the Department to complete and publish the DFARS amendments and requests a staff briefing on the implementation effort.

**Who it affects** The requested rule would affect the Department of Defense, defense contractors and subcontractors, and procurement officials responsible for evaluating foreign-influence risks in the defense industrial base. The letter highlights S&L Aerospace Metals LLC as an example of a contractor reportedly supplying parts for fighter jets, helicopters, and guided missile systems; it attributes concerns about the company’s leadership and Chinese intelligence ties to cited investigative reporting.

**Why it matters** The committee says the absence of a final DFARS rule leaves sensitive defense supply chains exposed to potential foreign influence and undermines the protections Congress enacted. It acknowledges DoD Instruction 5205.87, issued in May 2024, as a step forward but argues that the instruction does not substitute for the required regulatory implementation.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Section 847 was enacted in the Fiscal Year 2020 NDAA.
- The letter is dated June 16, 2025; the catalogue date is June 17, 2025.
- The requested staff briefing date was June 30, 2025.
- The letter identifies unclassified contracts exceeding $5 million as within the cited concern.
- DoD Instruction 5205.87 was issued May 13, 2024.
