The House Select Committee on the CCP requests a briefing from the Department of the Air Force about resourcing and personnel changes affecting the China Aerospace Studies Institute (CASI) by November 20, 2026.

**What it does** The November 4, 2026 letter to Air Force Secretary Troy E. Meink asks the Department of the Air Force to provide additional information about changes to CASI at Air University. Representatives John Moolenaar and Ro Khanna describe CASI’s work translating People’s Liberation Army documents, analyzing Chinese strategic thought and military modernization, and providing education and training for U.S. and allied personnel.

**Who it affects** The request is directed to the Department of the Air Force and concerns CASI, which the letter identifies as a resource for the Air Force, Space Force, other military services, Congress, and the expert community. It also bears on policymakers and military and civilian personnel who rely on analysis of the People’s Republic of China’s aerospace capabilities.

**Why it matters** The committee says that under-resourcing CASI could reduce the federal government’s ability to assess Chinese air, missile, and space capabilities and could require agencies to obtain external expertise. The letter links that concern to U.S. deterrence and strategic competition with Beijing, but does not make a finding about the Air Force’s decisions.

**Key dates and numbers**
- November 4, 2026: Letter date.
- November 20, 2026: Requested deadline for the briefing.
- August 2026: Most recent iteration of CASI’s “PLA Fundamentals” training course, according to the letter.
