The House Select Committee on the CCP finds that five U.S.-headquartered venture-capital firms invested at least $3 billion in PRC artificial-intelligence and semiconductor companies linked to the PLA, state surveillance, human-rights abuses, forced labor, or other national-security concerns, and recommends stronger outbound-investment restrictions.

**What it does** This investigative report examines investments, expertise, board participation, recruiting, introductions, and other intangible support provided by GGV Capital, GSR Ventures, Qualcomm Ventures, Sequoia Capital, and Walden International. It identifies investments in AI and semiconductor companies, including entities placed on U.S. government lists, and describes links to PRC government priorities, military-civil fusion, the PLA, Xinjiang surveillance, and forced labor. The report concludes with recommendations to restrict U.S. investment in listed entities and in strategic sectors tied to PRC military capabilities, critical technologies, and human-rights abuses.

**Who it affects** The findings concern the five VC firms, their U.S. limited partners, PRC portfolio companies, and companies such as ByteDance, SMIC, Megvii, SenseTime, DJI, 4Paradigm, and Intellifusion. The report also discusses Sequoia’s and GGV’s announced separations of their China businesses.

**Why it matters** The Committee argues that U.S. capital and expertise have helped advance PRC military modernization, authoritarian surveillance, semiconductor capacity, and technology transfer despite export controls and other restrictions. It says the sample is limited and therefore understates the broader flow of U.S. investment.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Report dated February 8, 2024.
- Five VC firms examined; more than $3 billion in identified PRC technology investments.
- More than $1 billion in PRC AI companies and more than $1 billion in over 150 semiconductor companies.
- More than $1.4 billion invested by Sequoia entities in ByteDance.
- Recommendations include restricting investment in entities on the Entity List, Section 1260H list, FCC Covered List, and related regimes.
