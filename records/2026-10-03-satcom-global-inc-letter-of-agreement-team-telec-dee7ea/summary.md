Broadband Satellite Services Limited agrees that, after the proposed transfer of control of SatCom Global and Horizon, it and SatCom Distribution will remain bound by the parties’ 2008 National Security Agreement with DOJ and DHS.

**What it does** This letter addresses pending FCC applications to transfer control of SatCom Global, Inc. and Horizon Mobile Communications, Inc. from One Horizon Group, Plc to Broadband Satellite Services Limited. BSS acknowledges the 2008 Agreement’s terms, conditions, and requirements and undertakes to continue compliance after the proposed sale of SatCom Distribution, Inc., the direct owner of the two operating companies.

**Who it affects** The commitment directly concerns BSS, SatCom Distribution, SatCom Global, Horizon, and One Horizon, and preserves the obligations owed to the DOJ and DHS under the existing national-security mitigation arrangement. The applications concern international Section 214 authorizations held by SGI and Horizon.

**Why it matters** The letter provides the incoming owner’s written continuity commitment for an existing Team Telecom mitigation agreement in connection with FCC review of the control transfer. It does not describe the agreement’s substantive safeguards or state whether the transfer was approved or completed; it transmits the commitment and identifies the 2008 Agreement as an enclosure.

**Key dates and numbers**
- January 31, 2014: letter date.
- September 24, 2008: effective date of the National Security Agreement.
- October 26, 2012: Share Purchase Agreement between One Horizon and BSS.
- FCC File Nos. ITC-T/C-20130612-00172 and ITC-T/C-20130612-00171.
- Related international Section 214 authorization numbers: ITC-214-20070110-00021 and ITC-214-20000803-00478.
