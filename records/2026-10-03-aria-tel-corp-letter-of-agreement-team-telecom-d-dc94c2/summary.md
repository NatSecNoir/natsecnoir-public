The Letter of Agreement requires ARIA TEL Corp. to implement extensive national-security and law-enforcement safeguards as a condition of the FCC’s grant of its international Section 214 authorization.

**What it does** The agreement records commitments made to the U.S. Department of Justice after review by DOJ, DHS, and DoD under Executive Order 13913. It requires a U.S.-based law-enforcement point of contact; prompt production of U.S. Records in response to lawful U.S. process; CALEA compliance; restrictions on disclosure of U.S. communications and records to foreign governments or persons; security plans, network diagrams, equipment and service-provider disclosures; advance notice of specified changes; incident reporting; annual compliance reports; and DOJ site visits. ARIA TEL must obtain DOJ objection or non-objection for several equipment, storage, personnel, and outsourcing changes.

**Who it affects** The obligations apply primarily to ARIA TEL, its personnel, vendors, managed network service providers, outsourced or offshore providers, and others with access to U.S. Records, domestic communications, or domestic communications infrastructure. The FCC is positioned to condition, modify, revoke, or cancel the relevant authorization for noncompliance.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for an international telecommunications carrier, concentrating control of U.S. customer data, lawful-intercept capability, network equipment, foreign access, and cybersecurity under continuing U.S. oversight.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Executed and dated November 10, 2020.
- FCC application: ITC-214-20161201-00337 (TT 16-064), for facilities-based and resale services between the United States and permissible international points.
- LEPOC and screening materials are generally due within 15 and 75 days after FCC approval or the LOA date, respectively.
- Certain records requests must be answered within five days; security incidents must be reported within 72 hours, and third-party breaches within 48 hours.
- The agreement contemplates an annual report one year after the LOA date and every year thereafter.
