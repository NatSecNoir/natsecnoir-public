The House Select Committee on the CCP asked Apple CEO Tim Cook for information about the cancellation of *The Problem with Jon Stewart* and urged Apple to ensure that content critical of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) or the People’s Republic of China (PRC) can be created and distributed without coercive foreign influence.

**What it does**
Chairman Mike Gallagher and Ranking Member Raja Krishnamoorthi describe reports that Apple would not produce a third season of *The Problem with Jon Stewart* amid disagreements involving potential show topics, including China and artificial intelligence. They request a briefing from Apple representatives for committee staff and a commitment that content perceived as critical of the CCP or PRC is welcome on Apple TV+ and other Apple services.

**Who it affects**
The letter is directed to Tim Cook and concerns Apple’s streaming and content decisions. It also addresses artists, writers, studios, streaming services, and technology companies whose business, financing, or supply-chain relationships with the PRC may affect editorial independence and technological dependence.

**Why it matters**
The lawmakers frame the reported dispute as part of a broader risk that fear of CCP retaliation, access to the PRC market, and PRC-based financing could chill American content critical of the CCP. They state that companies may decide what content is appropriate, but argue that coercive tactics by a foreign power should not directly or indirectly influence those decisions. The letter also urges technology companies to diversify supply chains and reduce PRC-related dependencies.

**Key dates and numbers**
- November 15, 2023: Letter date.
- The letter concerns the decision not to produce a third season of *The Problem with Jon Stewart*.
- Apple was asked for two specific actions: a staff briefing and a commitment regarding content critical of the CCP or PRC.
