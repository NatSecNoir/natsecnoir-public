The FCC Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau announces that the Department of War conditionally approved Vantiva USA, LLC’s CWA438TX router and therefore exempts that device from the FCC Covered List.

**What it does** The Public Notice updates the Covered List and its accompanying Conditional Approvals appendix to reflect the approval. PSHSB determines that the approval is a “specific determination” that the device does not pose a risk to U.S. national security, requiring the Bureau to exclude it from the list. The notice also publishes the current Covered List and identifies prior conditional approvals for routers and uncrewed aircraft systems.

**Who it affects** The action directly affects Vantiva USA, LLC and the CWA438TX router. It also informs equipment authorization applicants, testing laboratories, and telecommunications certification bodies about the effect of the update. Other manufacturers and devices listed in Appendix B remain subject to their stated conditional-approval periods.

**Why it matters** The notice illustrates how a device otherwise covered by the March 2026 foreign-produced-router designation can be removed from the Covered List following an Executive Branch conditional approval. Covered List status affects FCC authorizations and related supply-chain restrictions; the exemption is time-limited.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released July 8, 2026; DA 26-688.
- Vantiva CWA438TX approval runs July 8, 2026, through January 3, 2028.
- Foreign-produced routers were added to the Covered List on March 23, 2026, subject to specified exceptions.
- Relevant proceedings: WC Docket No. 18-89, ET Docket No. 21-232, and EA Docket No. 21-233.
