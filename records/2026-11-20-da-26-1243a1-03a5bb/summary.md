The FCC’s Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau announces that five foreign-produced device groups received Department of War Conditional Approvals and are therefore exempt from the FCC Covered List.

**What it does**
The notice removes FlyingBasket’s FB3 uncrewed aircraft system; EnGenius’s EHR320 BE3600 and EHR330 BE14000 Wi-Fi 7 routers; Zyxel’s DX4510-B1 router; SYNCROBOTIC’s “Jeff” and “Adventure” quadruped robotic platforms; and BLUEPATH ROBOTICS’s O1000 PLATFORM and OM1500 TUNNEL AMR from the Covered List. The exemptions depend on continuing compliance with the companies’ onshoring plans and, where applicable, updated product vetting. The Bureau updates the Covered List under 47 CFR §§ 1.50002(a) and 1.50003 and includes a cumulative appendix of devices with Conditional Approvals.

**Who it affects**
The action directly affects the five named manufacturers and their identified devices. It also provides practical guidance for test labs, telecommunications certification bodies, and equipment-authorization applicants handling these products. The notice concerns foreign-produced uncrewed aircraft systems, routers, and advanced robotic devices that would otherwise be covered by the FCC’s supply-chain restrictions.

**Why it matters**
The notice demonstrates how a DoW-specific determination can create an exception to otherwise applicable Covered List entries. It preserves access to FCC equipment authorization for the identified devices while making the exemption conditional: failure to follow an onshoring plan, or a false statement or misrepresentation in an application, may terminate the approval and return the equipment to the Covered List.

**Key dates and numbers**
- Released November 18, 2026.
- Conditional Approvals for all five newly listed company-device groups are dated November 18, 2026.
- EnGenius’s approval terminates May 15, 2028; Zyxel’s terminates May 16, 2028.
- The notice cites WC Docket No. 18-89, ET Docket No. 21-232, and EA Docket No. 21-233.
