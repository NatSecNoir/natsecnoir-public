GTT Communications, Inc. agrees, subject to FCC approval and closing of the proposed transaction, to become a party to and assume the commitments in Hibernia Atlantic’s existing Letters of Assurance, as amended, for the Hibernia Atlantic and Project Express submarine cable systems.

**What it does** The letter gives formal notice to the U.S. government agencies that GTT will assume KCK Limited’s and Hibernia NGS Limited’s commitments under the 2010 Letter of Assurance, as amended by the 2014 Letter of Assurance. It states that the assumption does not modify the scope of those commitments. If the commitments are breached, the agencies may ask the FCC to modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or invalidate relevant licenses or authorizations, subject to GTT’s and Hibernia Atlantic’s rights to oppose or comment. The commitments do not bind GTT if the referenced applications are not granted.

**Who it affects** The letter concerns GTT, Hibernia Atlantic U.S. LLC, Hibernia NGS, KCK, and the U.S. government agencies overseeing the mitigation arrangements. The proposed acquisition would replace the existing foreign-controlled majority ownership with 100% ownership and control by U.S. corporation GTT.

**Why it matters** This document records the continuity of national-security commitments through a change in ownership of an international telecommunications carrier and submarine cable licensee. It links those commitments to FCC approval of the transfer of control and preserves potential FCC licensing remedies for a breach.
