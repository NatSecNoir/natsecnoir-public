The letter confirms Cincinnati Bell’s commitment to work with the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) and Department of Justice (DOJ) to update or modify existing national-security mitigation documents if future evaluations identify concerns in connection with its proposed acquisition of Hawaiian Telcom’s licensees and submarine-cable interests.

**What it does** This filing describes Cincinnati Bell’s proposed indirect acquisition of 100% of Hawaiian Telcom, Inc. and Hawaiian Telcom Services Company, Inc. from Hawaiian Telcom Holdco, Inc. It identifies the affected Hawaii Island Fiber Network, Hawaii Inter-Island Cable System, and Southeast Asia-US (SEA-US) submarine cable systems. Cincinnati Bell acknowledges the 2012 Letter of Assurances and the 2017 National Security Agreement, and commits to negotiate in good faith and on a reasonably expedited basis to address national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety concerns.

**Who it affects** The commitment applies to Cincinnati Bell, the Hawaiian Telcom licensees and transferor, and the federal agencies evaluating the transaction and mitigation arrangements. It also concerns the other entities identified as parties or co-owners of the cable systems.

**Why it matters** The letter links FCC transfer-of-control review to continuing Team Telecom oversight. It indicates that DHS and DOJ may periodically assess whether the existing LOA and NSA remain sufficient, rather than treating the mitigation arrangements as static.

**Key dates and numbers**
- December 7, 2012: HTI provided the LOA to DHS and DOJ.
- January 11, 2017: HTSC and related entities entered into the NSA with DHS.
- File Nos. SCL-T/C-20170811-00019 and SCL-T/C-20170811-00020.
- The proposed transaction would indirectly transfer 100% ownership of the licensees to Cincinnati Bell.
