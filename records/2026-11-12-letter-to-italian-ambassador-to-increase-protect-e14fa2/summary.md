The House Select Committee on the CCP urges Italy to take immediate action to protect Chinese dissident Li Ying from alleged transnational repression and to cooperate with the United States against such abuses.

**What it does** The letter describes Li as an art student and prominent dissident living near Milan who gained prominence during the late-2022 White Paper protests and built a Twitter following of 1.7 million. It recounts alleged death threats, smear campaigns, frozen financial accounts, pressure on his parents in Fuyang City, surveillance, disclosure of his Italian address and passport images, and repeated suspicious approaches and intimidation in Italy. The committee asks Ambassador Zappia to support quick and decisive protective measures and to work with the United States to ensure transnational repression is not tolerated.

**Who it affects** The immediate subject is Li Ying, while the requested actions are directed to the Italian government, local Italian authorities, and the United States. The letter attributes the reported pressure to tactics employed by the Chinese Communist Party and references alleged involvement by PRC security officials and inquiries by PRC-linked actors in Italy.

**Why it matters** The committee frames transnational repression—including stalking, harassment, hacking, physical assaults, attempted forced returns, and threats against relatives—as a violation of national sovereignty and a threat to human rights and personal freedom. It calls for Italy to remain a safe haven for persecuted people and for bilateral cooperation against authoritarian interference.

**Key dates and numbers**
- 2022: Li emerged during the White Paper protests; his separation from family had lasted three years.
- December 12, 2022; March 2023; May 2023: incidents involving alleged efforts to obtain his address or whereabouts.
- September 13, October 4, and October 9, 2024: reported suspicious encounters and graffiti at his residence.
- November 13, 2024: date of the letter.
- 1.7 million: reported Twitter followers.
