The Agreement imposes national-security, law-enforcement, and public-safety mitigation measures on the operators of the Japan-Guam-Australia South System (JGA-S) as a condition of the FCC’s submarine cable landing license.

**What it does** The Licensees must provide the Compliance Monitoring Agencies (CMAs) with detailed cable, network, ownership, customer, equipment, vendor, and managed-service-provider information; maintain the ability to interrupt U.S.-bound or U.S.-originating traffic; and obtain CMA review of network operations centers, points of presence, principal equipment, testing, vendors, and material changes. It requires cybersecurity, physical-security, personnel-screening, incident-reporting, security-policy, point-of-contact, annual-reporting, audit, visitation, and change-in-control obligations. A material breach may lead the CMAs to request that the FCC modify, condition, revoke, cancel, terminate, or nullify the license.

**Who it affects** The obligations bind GU Holdings Inc. (Google), RTI JGA, RTI Connectivity, and AARNet, collectively the Licensees, and apply to their successors, affiliates, contractors, vendors, managed service providers, personnel, and JGA-S facilities. DHS, DOJ, and DoD serve as the CMAs.

**Why it matters** This is a Team Telecom mitigation agreement governing a U.S.-connected submarine cable, combining operational control, supply-chain oversight, access restrictions, and continuing government monitoring. It illustrates how national-security review can become an ongoing condition of a cable landing license rather than a one-time approval.

**Key dates and numbers**
- The underlying FCC application was filed June 21, 2019, under FCC File No. SCL-LIC-20190502-00016.
- The Agreement’s execution materials include signature dates in July 2020 and a final government signature dated October 6, 2020.
- Cable and equipment information is generally due within 45 days of execution; security policies are due within 90 days.
- Certain incidents must be reported within 48 hours, with a follow-up report within 15 days.
