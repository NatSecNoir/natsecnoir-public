The Letter of Agreement requires BillyRonks Global LLC to implement national-security and law-enforcement safeguards as a condition associated with its application for international Section 214 authority to provide global or limited global resale-based telecommunications services.

**What it does** The agreement establishes detailed mitigation commitments covering a U.S. law-enforcement point of contact, personnel screening, foreign-person access, storage and access locations for U.S. Records and domestic communications, cybersecurity and system-security plans, service-provider oversight, incident reporting, ownership and service changes, annual compliance reporting, and possible third-party audits. BillyRonks must obtain or await DOJ non-objection for specified submissions and changes, and must report security incidents or breaches generally within 72 hours.

**Who it affects** The obligations apply primarily to BillyRonks, its personnel, affiliates, vendors, managed network service providers, and other service providers with access to communications infrastructure, lawful U.S. process, or customer and network records. DOJ, including the FBI, receives notice, review, objection, audit, and enforcement-related functions; the FCC is notified that there is no objection to granting the application.

**Why it matters** The LOA illustrates Team Telecom mitigation for a communications authorization, linking operational cybersecurity, data governance, foreign access, supply-chain risks, and lawful-access capability to continued FCC authorization. Breach or failure to resolve DOJ concerns may support a request to modify, condition, revoke, cancel, or nullify the relevant authorization.
